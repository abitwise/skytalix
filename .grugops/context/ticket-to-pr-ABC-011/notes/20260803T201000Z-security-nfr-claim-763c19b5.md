## 1. The purity closure is 336,000 inputs and an argument, not a theorem

I am willing to sign "0 impure over 336,000 adversarial inputs, including the classes prior roles
named as unverified, against a control that reads 14,341 on the identical population, plus a
structural precondition argument". I am **not** willing to sign "impossible". A board shape absent
from my generator would escape it, exactly as the first pass's `L8` shape was absent from the
reachable population. The structural half is the stronger half and I believe it — guard (b)
establishes the precondition `undoSpecial` assumes, guard (a) rejects every out-of-array target — but
I did not write a proof.

## 2. Equivalence of the two unpinned mutants is measured, not proved

83,400 inputs each with zero answer, key or purity differences is strong evidence and is not a proof.
For the early-return deletion I do have a structural argument (it can only change behaviour when
`target === NO_SQUARE`, and guard (a) catches exactly that because `board[-1]` is `undefined`). For
the guards-inside-the-loop move I have only the sweep and the observation that both conditions are
loop-invariant. **If anyone deletes guard (a) later, the early-return deletion stops being equivalent
and nothing in this repository will say so** — that dependency is worth knowing and is not pinned.

## 3. Every timing figure is one machine, and the bar itself inherits that

`NFR-005`'s 1.5x bar is anchored on an audit measured on this machine class. All my figures are Apple
M3 (Mac15,13) / Node v24.12.0 / Darwin 25.5.0 arm64 and are `UNKNOWN - verify` anywhere else. The
headroom is large (>30%), so I do not expect the *verdict* to be machine-sensitive — but I did not
measure that and cannot assert it. Owner: `EPIC-004`'s non-M3 spike.

## 4. The worst-case ep ratio is protocol-sensitive and I am not claiming a value for it

Three protocols on this ticket read 1.385–1.400x, 1.401–1.412x (mine) and 1.446–1.458x. That is about
±4%, which is the size of the spread the first pass once called "resolved". **I uphold the withdrawal
of that word.** It is not a bar position and it does not affect the verdict, but nobody should quote a
settled number for it.
