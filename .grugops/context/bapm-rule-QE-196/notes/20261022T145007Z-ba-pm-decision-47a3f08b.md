**Why QE-196-a is accepted, when QE-195-a was cut** (`bapm-rule-QE-195` `…f0144de0`). The trigger class is the same: an unrelated, mergeable `src/cli.ts` refactor. - **The class stays covered by rows `src/` cannot silence.** At R3, PP-TPL, PP-REDIR and RA-PLUS are still named on all 6 limbs under the pre-`ABC-195` instrument. QE found that silencing them takes a harness edit (`QE-196-b`). `AR-193-HUNKHDR`'s close (ADR-0036 `ABC-195` block (v)) was QE's read at `ABC-195` and stands.

Since `ABC-196`, the header says the guard "checks the verdict word only, not which line fires". `QE-196-c` confirms that nothing is promised beyond that.
- **A reading that detects it already exists, and it matters only at one kind of hop.** A silent PP-EDIT can hide only an instrument regression, and only a hop that edits `clause-b.mjs` can make one. `ABC-196`'s `A5` reading (the fixed harness under the pre-`ABC-195` instrument names the 9 `TW` limbs) catches R3: QE's `fix-R3-pre195` and `m-fix-R3-pre195` runs name 8, with `PP-EDIT:limb2` missing.
