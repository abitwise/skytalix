## 4. Derivations (pinned; `raw/scripts/read223.py`, sha256 `254bad0c…6cd1427`)
`read223.py` is `qe-ABC-222`'s `read222.py` (as fixed after its rehearsal), with the budget, ceiling and mode name
changed, the longest move's place in its period added, and the foreign-window check (§ 5, A7) added.

**Per-move time (exact), as `qe-ABC-222`.** For a side's k-th move (k from 1, book moves counted):
```
elapsed_k = own clock in go_k − own clock in go_(k+1) + (900 000 if k is a multiple of 40)
```
"Own clock" is `wtime` for white, `btime` for black. A side's last move in a game has no next `go`; there `elapsed`
is the `-debug all` stamp delta `ts(bestmove) − ts(go)`. The stamp delta is cross-checked against the exact figure
on every other move (median and max difference reported).

**Closest call (A3).** Clock left after move k = own clock in `go_k` − `elapsed_k`, before any base is added; with
margin 0 that is the margin to the flag. The minimum over every move of both sides in every game played.

**Completed period (A4).** Period p of a side is complete when that side played its moves 40(p−1)+1 … 40p. Use = the
sum of `elapsed` over those 40 moves / 900 000, book moves included, counted from the side's first move after the
opening position.

**Longest move (A5).** The maximum `elapsed` over all moves, graded on the exact ms: **MET when ≤ 60 000 ms.** The PGN
prints ≥ 10 s to whole seconds (QE-222-f); every PGN time present must agree with its exact figure, and the count of
PGN strings above `60s` is reported. A6 reports where in its period the longest move fell (period, move of 40).

**The budget (A6), derived** (`ADR-0052` D4; `src/uci-session.ts` `safeClockShare` and `parseGo` at this build;
unrounded). With clock `t` for the side to move, increment `i` (0 if absent), `movestogo m` (20 if absent):
```
C = max(1, min((t − 1000 − 100·m) / m + i, t − 1100));   budget = min(59 500, C)
movetime x (if sent): budget = min(x, 59 500)
```
**Book move:** the engine wrote `info string book` between its `go` and `bestmove`. Every other move is searched.

**A6 overhead.** `elapsed − budget` over searched moves: median and max. Also over searched moves with `elapsed ≥
budget − 1 000`, which excludes early returns; the count of early returns is reported. Also reported: the clock left
after each period's 40th move; `go` lines with and without `movestogo` (game 1's first `go` quoted); `isready` lines
sent between a `go` and its `bestmove`; the period-end rows (clock, budget, elapsed, left).

**What the arithmetic predicts (not a bar).** With `movestogo` on every `go` and each move costing its budget + δ, the
clock left after a period's 40th move is 1 100 − δ ms; `ABC-222` read δ ≤ 2 ms, so ≈ 1 098 ms at every period end.
Period use ≈ 99.9–100 %: banked book time is spent later in the period (D4). No budget is expected near 59 500 ms
(the share starts at 22 375 ms).

## 5. Bars
- **A1 MET:** the pins above hold, and game 1's log shows `>skytalix-a(…): setoption name TimeMode value
  uci-clock-mode` and the same for `skytalix-b`, with no `TimeMode takes …` refusal. If the line does not reach the
  engine, QE stops, fixes the spelling, and restarts the count.
- **A2 MET:** every game keeps its PGN and `-debug all` log, and ends by a rule result, the move cap, or a loss on
  time. Any other end (illegal move, disconnect, stall at the 5 400 s watchdog, crash, "No result") is a named fault,
  and the reading is UNRESOLVED. An engine killed after a game's result line is counted and reported, not a fault.
- **A3 MET:** 0 losses on time over all games played, **and** the closest call ≥ 500 ms.
- **A4 MET:** every completed side-period uses ≥ 75 %, **and** at least 4 side-periods are complete. Coverage
  extension: if games 1–5 complete fewer than 4, the harness plays openings 6, 7, … one game at a time until 4 are
  complete, up to 10 games. Fewer than 4 after 10 games: A4 UNRESOLVED. (The "play all 10" rule does not fire:
  `ABC-222`'s overhead max was +2 ms, under 300 ms.)
- **A5 MET:** the longest move ≤ 60 000 ms, exact.
- **A6:** reported, not graded; complete when every figure in § 4 is reported from the run's own records.
- **A7 MET:**
  - ALONE: no other agent hop with an engine or vitest (a records-only Architect hop may run beside), no foreign
    vitest, no second `cutechess-cli`, and (RE-SCORED block) no foreign process at ≥ 90 % of a core in the scan
    before game 1. The host has no battery (empty `power_supply` class), so it runs on mains.
  - Tctl (k10temp) and the CPU frequency sampled every 10 s; largest gap ≤ 60 s.
  - The play window, game 1's start to the last game's end, is cut into 4 equal-time quarters; Tctl median per
    quarter and the max are reported.
  - **Frequency:** per sample the busiest core's `scaling_cur_freq`. A quarter whose median is more than 5 % under
    Q1's makes the reading **UNRESOLVED**. The precedent formula (run median / first 6 samples) is INFO.
  - **A foreign process during play** (another repository's vitest, or any foreign process at ≥ 50 % of a core in
    `foreign.log`) is logged with its pid, parent, repository (skytalix / other, by its working directory's name)
    and sample count. It is **never killed**. It is graded as an A7 gap, not a stop. If a loss on time, a closest call
    under 1 000 ms, or a move ≥ 59 900 ms falls inside a foreign vitest's window (a 10 s sample with vitest > 0
    within 60 s of the move), A3 or A5 is UNRESOLVED and not graded: load can cause a forfeit but cannot hide one.

## 7. Thermal stop and the VOID rule
- **Thermal stop:** the harness checks every 60 s. With ≥ 30 samples, a Tctl median above **85 °C** over all samples
  so far stops the run with SIGTERM. QE reports **THERMAL STOP**, and A2–A6 are not read.
- **VOID:** the harness or any `cutechess-cli` invocation exits non-zero or dies without a thermal stop, or a
  `Finished match` line or a PGN is missing. QE records it and **STOPS**, with no re-run in this hop.
- **The ticket's "re-run once"** for a named fault (A2) or a frequency fall (A7) is owed to the Orchestrator's
  schedule (another ALONE window of up to ~5 h), not done in this hop.

## 8. Verdict rules
- **PASS:** A1–A5 and A7 MET, A6 reported, no fault and no gap.
- **PASS_WITH_GAPS:** the same, with non-blocking gaps named (for example a foreign process outside any close call's
  window, or the cap not exercised).
- **FAIL (NOT MET):** A3, A4 or A5 NOT MET. Per test note 2, a `failed-attempt` note, and `ABC-221` goes back to BA/PM.
- **UNRESOLVED:** a named fault, a frequency fall, A4 short of coverage after 10 games, or A3/A5 inside a foreign
  load window.
- **THERMAL STOP / VOID:** not read (§ 7).
