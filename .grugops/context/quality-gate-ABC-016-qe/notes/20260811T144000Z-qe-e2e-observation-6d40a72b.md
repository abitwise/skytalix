## The instrument, and the one place it differs from the previous hop's

Each mutant got a mutated copy of `src/status-panel.ts`, a copy of the **delivered**
`src/status-panel.test.ts` and `src/status-panel.nfr.test.ts` pointed at it, **and a copy of the
delivered `src/__snapshots__/status-panel.test.ts.snap`.**

**That last file is the difference, and it changes the handed-down conclusion.** The `D19b` engineer
measured its mutant in a fresh probe directory with **no committed baseline**, where vitest *writes*
whatever the build emits and therefore agrees with it. That measurement is correct and its conclusion
does not follow from it. Scored against the **committed** baseline the snapshots have real
discriminating power: they killed **8 mutants that no acceptance criterion touches**, and they were
the only thing standing between four of those and a green suite.

**The corrected statement, which is narrower and still supports the engineer's warning:** the
snapshots kill any mutant that changes any of the fifteen rendered strings, but they say nothing
about *which* criterion broke, they are discharged by a single `-u`, and they are blind to every
mutant whose output the fifteen cases do not reach — which is where seven of my survivors live. They
are a real regression net and a poor specification. **"Worth nothing as a mutant net" over-states
it; "worth nothing as a criterion" is right.**

## `A7`'s width clause, measured at its own fixture

```
busy ["Black to move  1...", "last: e4  material: 0", "thinking..."]   widest = 21
idle ["Black to move  1...", "last: e4  material: 0", "           "]   widest = 21
row 3: busy 11, idle 11 — and row 3 is NEVER the widest row here
if the idle cell were DROPPED entirely, widest would still be          21
```

`A7` compares the **widest row**. Row 2 is the widest row and does not vary with `thinking`. The
reservation `A7` exists to pin is invisible to `A7`. `m23` — the idle cell dropped rather than
reserved, which is verbatim the build `D7` added the clause to kill — passed `A7` and every other
criterion, and died only to 12 snapshots.

*(Corroboration that `A7` is not inert: `m48`, which drops the **material** cell while thinking,
**was** killed by `A7` — because material sits on the widest row. `A7` catches a shrinking cell iff
that cell happens to be on the widest row, which is not a property any criterion states.)*
