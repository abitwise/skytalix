## 2. Currency — E012023 is still the newest Laws chapter FIDE publishes

FIDE's own handbook index (https://handbook.fide.com/, HTTP 200, 75,659 bytes) links exactly

    https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012024   http=200  bytes=74467  <title>FIDE Handbook </title>
    https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012025   http=200  bytes=74467  <title>FIDE Handbook </title>

    https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E01ZZZZ   http=200  bytes=74467  <title>FIDE Handbook </title>   <- control

All four return the handbook's generic soft-404 shell — identical byte count to a deliberately
nonsensical chapter id, and no chapter title. The site answers 200 for a missing chapter, so
the status code alone proves nothing; the byte count against the control is what does.
For contrast, E012023 is 186,226 bytes and its <title> is
"FIDE Handbook FIDE Laws of Chess taking effect from 1 January 2023".

result:   E012023 is the operative edition as of 2026-08-03.

## 4. The wording asymmetry across editions, measured on the retained files

The en-passant clause is written in the CAPABILITY mood and the castling clause in the
ENTITLEMENT mood, in every edition the handbook publishes:

  2014  "at the start of the sequence a pawn could have been captured en passant."
        "a king or rook had castling rights, but forfeited these after moving.
         The castling rights are lost only after the king or rook is moved."
  2017  "at the start of the sequence a pawn could have been captured en passant"
        "a king had castling rights with a rook that has not been moved, but forfeited these
         after moving. The castling rights are lost only after the king or rook is moved."
  2018  identical to 2017
  2023  identical to 2017 (9.2.3.1 / 9.2.3.2)

The 2014 -> 2017 revision reworded the FIRST half of the castling clause and left the
exclusivity sentence ("lost only after the king or rook is moved") untouched. The en-passant
clause's "could have been captured" is unchanged across all four. The asymmetry ADR-0006
rests on has survived twelve years and two revisions; it is not an artefact of one edition.
