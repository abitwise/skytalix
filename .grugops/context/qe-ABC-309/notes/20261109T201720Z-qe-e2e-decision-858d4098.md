## Gaps, by severity
- **Blocking (A3):** the Arena and BanksiaGUI routes are untested and not marked so (above).
- **Low: the Cute Chess menu path on macOS.** Step 2 says the route is for Linux and macOS, but on macOS Qt moves Settings into the application menu (`PreferencesRole`, `mainwindow.cpp:191`), so "Tools > Settings" is not there. The subsection does say its steps follow Linux.
- **Low: § Strength against § Levels.** The figure is "level 5" at 100 ms a move, but § Levels gives level 5 as 4 s. A new reader can take 1902.5 as the default level's strength in play. The "that scale only" sentence limits the claim but does not name the gap.
- **Low: `simple-tournament` "never past the clock it is given".** On `movetime 200` QE measured 202 ms, the accepted overshoot `AR-NFR004-A`. A GUI set to a fixed time a move with no margin flags the engine on time, and the README gives no hint to set a margin. The wording is the ticket's own (scope 3), so this is for the Orchestrator, not this fix.
- **Low: `level` "plays at the `Skill Level`'s time per move".** It plays at most that: a shorter GUI clock caps it (`min(request, level)`). The SE recorded this choice (`…7b77184b` item 4).
- **Low, readability.**
  - The intro (lines 69–70) says a GUI runs `node` with the path as its argument, and the Cute Chess step then says that does not start there.
  - The Arena and BanksiaGUI fallbacks send the reader forward to "Cute Chess's Windows route".
  - "`node.exe` from your Node.js folder" gives no way to find that folder.
- **Low, records.**
  - The rulings note `…4b413e8c` is in no commit (only the main checkout's working tree), yet `…5432062f` refs it.
  - `se-ABC-309-fix/` has a note but no `index.md` or `index.jsonl`.
- **Outside this ticket.**
