## Clause 2 — paired cost / `N8-LANE`. RULED: read under the catalog's protocol, bounds unchanged

**Why the as-written clause cannot close:**
- The Engineer's three sets have **no identity null control**. Under `plans/nfr-catalog.md`'s
  `N8-LANE` admissibility rule, none of them is admissible either way. That includes set 2's
  +0.79 s CPU (load1 18.6–25.6). It is neither a pass nor a fail.
- "Sign agreeing on ≥ 4 of 5" fails a build that costs nothing.
- For **wall**, the catalog measures a per-file delta at −15 to +3 ms, indistinguishable from zero.
  So the wall sign test is a coin flip: P(≥ 4 of 5 agreeing | chance) = 0.375.
- That is red by construction for the ideal build. It is not noise.

**What closes it:**
- Three out-of-repository trees: `<base>`, `HEAD`, and a `cmp`/`diff -r`-verified twin of
  `<base>`.
- n ≥ 7 interleaved rounds, the order rotated every round, a fresh process per run, round 0
  discarded, load1 logged.
- **Admissible iff** the twin reads ≤ 1.5 % CPU with its sign at chance. The two-sided sign test
  must give p ≥ 0.05, which for n = 7 or 8 means anything but 0/n or n/n.
- Then: **CPU median ≤ 0.30 s** with its sign reported, and **wall median ≤ 0.30 s**.
- An inadmissible set is re-run. After two, QE stops and hands up to Security/NFR
  (`self_fix_attempts` = 2).

**Not lowered:**
- Both 0.30 s bounds are unchanged.
- The rounds rise from 5 to 7, and a null control is added.
- The only thing removed is a sign requirement, and it fails good builds. The null twin replaces it
  as the "is this noise" test, and the twin is stricter.
- The wall bound still catches `AR-N8SPAWN-DRIFT` (the env guard removed, about +3.1 s) by 10x.

**"QE re-runs it on a quiet machine" is refused as the closing reading.** The catalog's `ABC-108`
`A1` measured that quiet cannot be bought here: the self-parallel harness alone pushes load1 above
1.0. Load is logged; the twin gates.

**`N8-LANE`.** It is read from the same admissible reading, net of the 160 ms floor for one file,
in `ADR-0019` decision 5's form. `A2`(ii)'s gross 0.30 s CPU already implies ≤ 140 ms net, which is
inside 650 ms. Security/NFR checks it against QE's raw output at its gate, per the catalog.
**Spawn account: nothing to subtract.** No child is spawned with the variable unset.
