- **Scope 5:** the `ADR-0026` d10 door is replaced. No door applies (decision 7). The change is value-changing, so the 10 D + 10 R smoke pair applies (`A7`, S-1..S-4).

- **A5:** the window is "since the halfmove clock last reset". A castling change is not a cut. A row checks that a key older than the clock is never matched.
- **A6:** the unchanged-and-green fence rows from `ADR-0054` § Consequences: the `ABC-081` `A6` rows, the `ADR-0011` licence equality and the bare-FEN pins. A red there is a build fault, not a re-pin.
- **A8 (new, probe H2):** a second occurrence keeps base's score and move. This pins the two-match rule.
- **A9 (new, probe H4):** prior keys with the root clock at 0 are node-equal to base.
- **Out of scope** adds the table, quiescence, `search()` and the doors. **Priority** is firm at P2, milestone 1 (`ABC-226` `A5` = 484).
