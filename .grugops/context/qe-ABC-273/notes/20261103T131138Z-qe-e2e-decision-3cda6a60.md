## The band-rule consequence of each outcome (`ABC-204` A4: the score inside 25–75 %, else E moves once in the needed direction and R is re-run)

1. **VOID or UNRESOLVED:** no figure. QE stops and returns the question: a re-run would be a second run, and the two together pass 1 h.
2. **50.0 ≤ P ≤ 150.0 (25–75 %, both ends inside):** E 1 810 is confirmed. The reading stands as R1's figure. No step, no second run, no owner ask.
3. **P > 150.0 (above 75 %): an upward step.** The step itself needs no owner word up to 2 600, but the second 200 games are a new run. Together the two pass 1 h, so that run is asked of the owner first (R1-A3, `ADR-0056` D6). QE stops and returns the question.

   - This run's E + X ± Y is reported, labelled "outside the band; not R1's figure".
4. **P < 50.0 (below 25 %): a downward step.** That is always the owner's question, for the step and for the run. QE stops and returns the question, with E′ stated as in 3 and the run labelled as in 3.
