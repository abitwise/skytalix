## The control I did NOT trade against, and how a later hop can check me

Rule 2's second half — *"criterion gaps at QE do not rise"* — is the half that makes the budget honest.
**Nothing measurable was removed from any criterion to save a line.** Every deletion above is narration,
one deletion is a **false** clause (`A10`), and the criteria count went **up** (11 → 12), as did the
security bars (3 → 4 plus BAR 1b).

**Where I judged the budget must yield:** at 328 I stopped cutting, because the next available savings were
in `A6`(ii)'s generator specification, `A12`'s licence bound and bar 2's ordered three-part assertion —
i.e. in the instruments themselves. **A shorter ticket bought there is exactly the failure rule 2 says to
revert.** Recorded so the overage is a decision with a reason attached, not a drift.

**Not applicable, but named so nobody mis-cites this:** the ≤ 300 control is stated over the next ten **cut**
tickets. `ABC-042` was cut on 2026-08-13 and is already in that population **at its cut length of 300**;
this hop repaired it rather than cutting it. The overage should be read as a data point about repair cost,
not as a cut ticket busting the budget.
