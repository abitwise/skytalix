## The verdict

**`PASS_WITH_RISKS`.** Stated as two separate answers, because they are not the same answer:

- **The CODE is sound and I am not asking for a production line.** `src/status-panel.ts` survived
  every probe: 63,894 renders across 400 seeded games with **0** row-count violations, **0**
  non-ASCII code points, **0** purity violations and **0** throws; `A14` re-measured independently
  and holds at 0.40x; no reachable input makes it throw. `A1`–`A14` are met, as the previous hop
  said.
- **The CRITERIA SET is not.** Twelve wrong builds pass every acceptance criterion on the ticket.
  Seven of those pass the fifteen snapshots as well. They group into **seven criterion gaps**, and
  every one routes to **BA/PM**, not to the Engineer. They are itemised in this hop's `claim` note.

**I do not issue a merge recommendation and this is not one.** What I am asking for is narrow: a
BA/PM refinement hop before this ticket leaves `In Review`. The reason is merge order — `ABC-016`
merges **first of three**, so its test file becomes the regression net `ABC-017` and `ABC-019` are
built against. A gap sealed into it at merge has no later moment that forces a revisit.

## Why the verdict is not `FAIL`

A `FAIL` would put the defect on the delivered build, and after 50 mutants and a 400-game
differential fuzz I cannot support that. The engineer's build is correct everywhere I could reach
it. The ticket's own record is that its defects have been in its **words**, not its code — `D19a`
cost nothing, `D19b` cost one test case and no production line — and this hop continues that record
rather than breaking it.

## Why the verdict is not a clean `PASS`

Three of the seven gaps are the class this board has already had to retract once:

> `D17`, on `A11`: *"A criterion that keeps claiming a protection it cannot deliver is worse than one
> claiming less: a reader stops looking."*

- **`A7`'s width clause cannot fail on the build it was written to catch** — measured, not argued.
- **`A9` cannot fail on a panel that infers the claimant** — provably, because `src/gameend.ts:170`
  derives `claimable.by` from the same `position.blackToMove` such a panel would read.
- **`A13`'s two counting clauses are measured at 0 and 1 ply only**, so the exact regression the
  ticket says *"actually binds"* walks through from ply 2.

Each of the three currently reads, to the next person, as cover it does not provide.

## How this hop was scoped, and the one instruction I leaned on hardest

The brief handed me *"the fifteen snapshots are a regression record and are worth NOTHING as a
mutant net."* **I built the harness to test that sentence rather than to assume it**, by copying the
**delivered** `src/__snapshots__/status-panel.test.ts.snap` into every probe directory so the
snapshots ran against their committed baseline. That changed the answer and it changed what I
report — see the `observation` note. It is the same move the `D19b` engineer made on BA/PM's premise,
and for the same reason: this board's recorded failure mode is believing an instrument without
checking the instrument.

I also caught my own harness lying to me — it scored all 50 mutants as survivors on the first run.
That is recorded as a `failed-attempt` rather than quietly fixed, because the shape of the bug (a
green-looking zero produced by tests that never ran) is the exact shape of the risk this role exists
to catch.
