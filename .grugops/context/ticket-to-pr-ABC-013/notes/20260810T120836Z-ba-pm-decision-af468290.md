# `ABC-013`'s acceptance repaired: 13 criteria → 22, eighteen corrections, each one traced to the `## Scope` bullet it enforces

Every correction below is a repair to the **ticket**, not to the design. `ADR-0004` was not
re-opened, `EPIC-003`'s `parseSan` contract was not touched, `src/` was not written, and no criterion
widens `## Scope` — each brings acceptance **up to** a Scope bullet that was already there. That
distinction is `ABC-022`'s precedent and it is the test I applied to every addition.

## The corrections

**1. One of thirteen criteria was executable as written.** The section opened *"Every criterion is a
hand-built FEN with an exact expected string"*; only the castling criterion was. Five named a
position in prose, three named no position at all, two were unbounded universals (*"given any
position"*), two were about the bars and the suite. **Repair:** 18 named FENs in a table at the head
of the section, every criterion referring to one by id, every move named `from`-`to`(`=P`), every
expected string exact. *Rationale: the standard set for this hop was "executable by an engineer
holding only the ticket". Prose fails it by definition, and a criterion the engineer must invent a
position for is a criterion the engineer grades themselves.*

**2. The three-queen expected value was a hedge.** `(e.g. Qh4e1)` is not an expected string, and the
pre-check could not confirm the position was even constructible. **Repair:** built, confirmed legal,
pinned as `P-QUEENS`; all three queens do reach `e1` legally and `Qh4e1` is right.

**3. Two of that position's three rows were being discarded.** `Qee1` (file unique) and `Q1e1` (file
shared, rank unique) come free from the same FEN and are the two middle rungs of the ladder.
**Repair:** all three pinned in `A6`. *Rationale: the ticket's own Test notes say the precedence is
where the bugs are; the full-square row alone cannot see a build that always emits the full square.*

**4. The file/rank PRECEDENCE was asserted by no criterion.** The ticket's knight pair (`b1`+`d1`)
stands on the same rank, so the rank could not have separated them either way — a rank-first build
produces identical output. Measured: it passes all thirteen. **Repair:** `P-FILE-PREC` (`b1`+`d5`,
different file AND rank) added as `A4`'s second pair.

**5. Disambiguation over LEGAL moves was asserted by no criterion.** `## Scope` says "the other
**legal** moves of the same piece type"; a build using the pseudo-legal list passes all thirteen.
**Repair:** `A7` on `P-PIN`, where the rival knight is pinned and the answer is `Ne4` with **no**
qualifier. *This is the highest-value single row added: it is the only place the two move lists
disagree, and it is a legality question, which is this project's stated first priority.*

**6. Same-piece-type-only was asserted by no criterion.** A build disambiguating against every piece
reaching the square passes all thirteen and goes red on 12 rows. **Repair:** `A8`, two cross-type
pairs (`Nd2`/`Kd2`, `Bd2`/`Qd2`).

**7. The base case was absent** — no quiet pawn push, no unambiguous quiet piece move, though
*"pawns implicit"* and *"no qualifier when the piece is unique"* are both in `## Scope`. **Repair:**
`A1`, the start position's **20 legal moves as an exact set**. *Rationale: it is exhaustive, it is
the most legible position in chess, and one criterion covers both Scope clauses.*

**8. A castle can give check and nothing pinned `O-O+`** — the pre-check's fifth candidate, which it
named without asserting. Confirmed by construction. **Repair:** `A16` on `P-CASTLECHK` /
`P-CASTLECHKQ`, with an ordinary rook move giving the same check as the control.

**9. Stalemate was unguarded.** `#` is checkmate — check AND no reply — and `## Scope` offers no
third suffix. A build emitting `#` on "no reply" alone passes all thirteen. **Repair:** `A17` on
`P-STALE`, with a checking move in the same position as the control.

**10. En passant was unguarded.** It is a pawn capture whose destination square is empty, so a build
deciding "is this a capture" from `position.board[move.to]` drops the `x` here and nowhere else.
**Repair:** `A10`. *Two of this project's merged defects (`ABC-011`, `ABC-022`) were en-passant
defects; leaving the one SAN row that touches it unpinned would have been a choice.*

**11. Piece captures were unguarded** — only pawn captures were pinned, though `## Scope` says
"captures `x`". **Repair:** `A11` (`Nxe5`) and `A3`'s `Rxa8+`.

**12. The `K` letter was unguarded.** `Q`/`R`/`B`/`N` are pinned indirectly through the promotion
criterion; `K` is reachable by no promotion. **Repair:** `A2`, one quiet move per letter.

**13. Purity had no witness set** (*"given any position"*). **Repair:** `A18`, quantified over the
18 named positions × every legal move × **both** call forms.

**14. The `legal`-argument equivalence had no witness set.** **Repair:** `A19`, same quantification.

**15. The module surface was asserted by nothing.** A build importing `gameend.ts`, or shipping a
`parseSan` stub across the epic boundary, satisfied every criterion. **Repair:** `A20` — exactly one
export, imports exactly the four modules `ADR-0004` clause 1 names. *`fen.ts` is named as excluded
because `toFen` belongs to the tests, not the module, and that is an easy import to add by reflex.*

**16. A `## Test notes` sentence had become false** — it described `ABC-009` (malformed positions) as
open; it merged 2026-08-09, 10 commits on `main`. **Repair:** corrected in place, with what the merge
actually means for `A18` — that guard is why `A18` is passable at all, so this ticket must not
re-break it rather than fix it. *Leaving a merged ticket teaching a false rule is what `ABC-023`'s
defect 3 was.*

**17. `toSan`'s precondition on `move` was unstated.** The contract wrote down the caller obligation
for the `legal` list and not for the move. **Repair:** both obligations stated in `## Scope`, and
behaviour on an illegal `move` declared **explicitly undefined and asserted by no criterion** —
rather than inventing a behaviour BA/PM has no authority to choose. *If `EPIC-003` ever needs a total
`toSan`, that is a contract change and belongs in a ticket.*

The NFR bars are the eighteenth repair and are a separate decision note.

## What I deliberately did NOT do

- **No criterion for `+` on an en-passant or promotion capture** beyond what `A10`/`A12`/`A15`
  already pin. The suffix logic is one code path and `A13`–`A17` exercise it five ways; more rows
  there would be padding, and padding is what makes a table nobody reads.
- **No round-trip criterion.** It needs `parseSan` and belongs to `EPIC-003`; the ticket's Test notes
  already say so and say why, and the rows are shaped so that hop extends rather than replaces them.
- **No PGN, no move numbering, no annotation glyphs.** Out of scope and staying there.
