## What the code does (read, not run)
- Base `ca6e675c` `src/uci-session.ts` `go()`: *"D4.2 / D11: the level caps G"*: `budgetMs = Math.min(request.budgetMs,
  LEVELS[skill].budgetMs)`. The default skill is 5. At the base, `LEVELS[5]` = `{ budgetMs: 3_000, blunderRate: 0 }`
  (`src/levels.ts:54`). At the head, `LEVELS[5]` = `{ budgetMs: 4_000, blunderRate: 0 }` (`src/levels.ts:59`; `ABC-228` under
  `ADR-0053` d10 (ii)).
- `rpair` gives skytalix `st=4 timemargin=1000` and no `Skill Level` option (`cutechessArgs`, the dry run in `raw/gates/`).
  cutechess-cli sends `go movetime 4000` for that.
- So the base searches min(4 000, 3 000) = **3 000 ms**, and the head searches **4 000 ms**. The same holds for every base before
  `ABC-228`'s merge `e42b637b`. The epic's first value-changing merge (`ABC-230`, `24f3e322`) comes before it, so **no base that the
  rule allows plays 4 000 ms through `rpair` as it is.**

## Consequences for the batch as pinned
1. **It is not the batch the records specify.** `ADR-0053` d10 (Architect `…b86f7d93`): *"The batches run both sides at
   4 000 ms."* EPIC-010 § "The batch: what" (*"4 000 ms on both sides from `ABC-228`'s merge, `ADR-0053` d10"*), and the brief
   (*"4 000 ms both sides"*). As pinned, it reads head at 4 000 ms against base at 3 000 ms.
2. **The confound favours the head**, so it pushes toward "not LOSS". The head gets 1.33× the base's thinking time. QE does not
   measure its size here (`UNKNOWN - verify`). By the usual rule of thumb (a doubling of time is worth tens of Elo or more at this
   level), it may be a sizeable share of the pair's ≈ ± 70–75 Elo half-width. A "not LOSS" from that run could not separate
   "the merges did not hurt" from "the head had 33 % more time".
3. **Cost (QE-M1B-b, MEDIUM, records).** At 4 000 ms the recorded R rate is 3.26–3.61 min a game (the `qe-ABC-228-ii` and
   `qe-ABC-241` R smokes: 36.1 and 32.6 min for 10 games). So a 400-game pair **with both sides at 4 000 ms costs about
   21.7–24.1 h**, not 18.5 h. The pinned mix (base at 3 000, head at 4 000) would be about 200 × 2.77 + 200 × 3.26–3.61 min ≈
   **20.1–21.3 h**. These are estimates from small samples.
