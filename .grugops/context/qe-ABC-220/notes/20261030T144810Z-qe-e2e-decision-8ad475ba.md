## 1. QE rows (`qrows.mjs`, plain node; never vitest, never the real clock)
- **Instrument.** A `node:module` `registerHooks` resolve hook sends every import of `engine.ts` inside the tree under test (except the stub's own `?real` import) to a QE stub that re-exports the real engine and wraps `chooseMove`: it records `{ budgetMs, maxDepth, maxNodes }` and either answers the first legal move at once (stub mode) or calls the real `chooseMove` (real mode, virtual clock). The session is driven through `createUciSession` with a counted `random`, `OwnBook false` (no book), and a virtual clock. Each line prints `<row> | <what> | PASS|FAIL`; the exit code is the FAIL count. The fixtures and the oracle are QE's, written from the ticket and D2/D3, not from the code.
- **Oracle (D2/D3):** in the setting, `movetime x` present → `min(x, 22 000)`; else the side to move's clock `t` present → `min(22 000, max(1, min((t − 1 000 − m·100)/m + inc, t − 1 100)))`, `inc` the side to move's increment (0 absent), `m` = `movestogo` or 20; else 22 000.
- **QE-A1.** a. head's `uci` reply equals base's with exactly one line inserted after the `Skill Level` line, `option name TimeMode type combo default level var level var simple-tournament`. b. after the setting, a second `uci` writes the same reply (the combo default stays `level`).
- **QE-A6 (ceiling and rule, generalised).** a. 3 000 seeded random `go` lines in the setting, side to move alternating (White: `position startpos`; Black: `position startpos moves e2e4`): `wtime`/`btime` absent, negative, 0–4 000 000 or decimal; `winc`/`binc` absent or 0–30 000; `movestogo` absent or 1–100; `movetime` absent (70 %) or 0–60 000; optional `depth`/`nodes`/`infinite` (then `stop`). PASS: every handed budget equals the oracle, every budget ≤ 22 000, and each of the three binding cases (T, C, `movetime`) occurs ≥ 100 times. b. overflowing numbers (400 digits): `go wtime 9…9 btime 9…9` → 22 000; `go movetime 9…9` → 22 000; `go wtime -9…9 winc 9…9` (White) → NaN, and the real engine still answers a legal move at depth 1 (`engine.ts`'s NaN rule). PASS: no budget > 22 000. c. `go movetime -5` and `go movetime 0` in the setting: budget = the value (as at a level on base), and the real engine answers a legal move. Recorded, INFO if it holds.
- **QE-A3/A7 (side to move).** a. Black to move: `go wtime 10000 btime 10000 winc 0 binc 2000 movestogo 10` → 2 800 and `… winc 2000 binc 0 …` → 800.
