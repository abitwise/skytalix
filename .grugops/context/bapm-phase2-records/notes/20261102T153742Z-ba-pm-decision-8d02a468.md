# Phase 2 records: ABC-264 closed as dropped (retry DROP); P2 out of EPIC-011 Phase 2; P1 parked on main as a title-only stub with no id; the optional owner question on the 27 cp cap (94 cp, default keep) recorded

## Context

`arch-ADR-0060-reopen` `…e17a23f4`, `…79be958e`; `ADR-0059` and `ADR-0060` § Status; `ADR-0056` D2's dated line. Records
only, at `main` `4849c4ab`; nothing run.

## Decision
1. **`ABC-264` is closed as dropped, not merged.** Front matter is `status: dropped`, `column: Done`, with a `closed:`
   line, following the `ABC-238` / `ABC-209` precedent. The board move is the Orchestrator's.
   - The top block records retry DROP, `ADR-0059` accepted and not in force, and the code kept on `4d1c0616`.
   - A re-cut is a new ticket with a named new premise. The premises and bars are given by reference to `ADR-0059`
     § Status.
   - Gap dispositions:
     - `QE-264-p2-a`: closed by the disposition.
     - `QE-264-p2-b`: **open, for tools**.
     - `QE-264-p2-c`: recorded, no action.
     - `QE-264-a`: moot.
     - `QE-264-b`: already folded.
     - `QE-264-e`: carried by any re-cut.
   - The S′ 7.0 / 10 stays unpooled. The DoR gets a dated closing line.
2. **`EPIC-011` Phase 2: P2 is dropped.** The order is P1 → P4 → P3 → P5 → P6 (`ADR-0056` D2), with P1 parked.
   - P3's and P6's "after P2" waits are struck as void.
   - P4 still waits on P1.
   - The next pull is the Orchestrator's (`arch-phase2-p3` is queued).
   - § Tickets row 16 is struck to dropped.
3. **P1 is parked on `main` as a title-only stub with no ABC id.** It is a new subsection of `EPIC-011`, § Phases, "P1,
   parked", holding:
   - the title without "history-adjusted", naming no PV term or cap;
   - state Backlog/parked, and not Ready;
   - intent GAIN (11.0 % of non-first moves reduced at d6);
   - size S–M, priority P2;
   - the bars carried unchanged by reference to `ADR-0060` d8/d9;
   - main's door counts;
   - dependencies in order: the re-measure trigger (P3 merged, or O1h), then the owner's acceptance.
   - **Why no id (BA/PM's call, as the brief allowed):**
     - § Phases says stubs get an id and a full page at their phase's refinement. Phase 2 has given an id only to the
       item about to be pulled (P2 → `ABC-264`), and P3–P6 stay title-only.
     - P1 has no rule written into `ADR-0060`. A page would carry rows against a form that does not exist and would
       copy bars whose single home is the ADR.
     - No ticket here was parked before refinement.
     - `arch-phase2-rebase` `…e4680ed2` suggested an id. That divergence is stated on the page.
4. **The optional owner question is recorded** in `EPIC-011` § Owner questions:
   - May A3's 27 cp cap be re-derived to 94 cp (⌊1.5 × 63⌋)?
   - A "yes" alone admits no form, because A4 (a) is 0.8699 against 0.85.
   - Settling answers: keep / re-derive the cap and A4 (a) / decline.
   - **Default: keep** (the Architect's recommendation, adopted). It blocks nothing today.
5. **`RISK-EPIC011-08` is added.** Phase 2 has lost P2 (+30–60) and P1 (+40–100) for now, and P4 waits on P1. R1 may
   read a small Phase 2. No bar is loosened to recover either.

## Alternatives
- **Give P1 an id and a stub page now** (the Architect's suggestion). Rejected: there is no rule to build, it cannot be
  refined or reach Ready, and it would duplicate the ADR's bars. It costs nothing to cut when the trigger passes.
- **Keep `ABC-264` "blocked".** Rejected: the retry ruling is DROP, so nothing is pending.
- **Put P3 ahead of P1 in the written order.** Not taken: that is the Orchestrator's call by `ADR-0056` D2's dated line.
  The page records P1's park and P4's wait and leaves the pull to him.

## Scope
