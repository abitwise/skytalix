## The contradiction, and what closing it must not cost

`A1` pinned the emission byte for byte and pinned the **unsafe** one: `\x1b[K` = `H − 1`, `\x1b` =
`H + 1`, delta `(H − 1) × 3`. `RF-1` needs `H`, `H + 2`, `H × 3`. **`A1` was GREEN — it described the
delivered emission correctly, and the delivered emission loses a cell per row on a real terminal.**

The constraint that makes this hard is the point: **QE measured `A1` as where 9 of 14 mutants die**,
and `A2` — the criterion the ticket exists for — kills 2 and stayed green on 7. Dropping the exact
counts, or replacing them with a range or a `toContain`, would trade a three-character defect for the
suite's strongest killer.

## The re-derived `A1` — the exact wording now in the ticket

> **A1 — the emitted redraw, byte for byte, and EVERY ERASE FIRES AT COLUMN 0. Given** a 23-row frame
> and a TTY sink reporting 24 rows, **when** one frame is written, **then** the sink receives
> **exactly one** `write()` call whose text is **exactly** cursor-home, then `\x1b[K`, then the
> frame's rows joined by newline + `\x1b[K`, then one `\x1b[0J`; and in that text the count of
> `\x1b[K` is **`H`** (23), the count of `\x1b[0J` is **exactly 1**, the count of `\x1b` is
> **`H + 2`** (25), and its length exceeds the same frame's un-cleared emission by **exactly
> `H × 3`** = **69** characters; **and every `\x1b[K` fires at column 0** — the text **begins**
> `\x1b[H\x1b[K`, and **no** `\x1b[K` in it is immediately followed by a newline.

**Every number derived from `H`.** The `23`, `25` and `69` are the reference layout's values shown in
parentheses, exactly as the merged criterion showed `22`, `24` and `66`. Nothing became a range,
nothing became a `toContain`, nothing became "at least".

## THE PART THAT IS NOT IN ANYBODY'S BRIEF, and it is the reason this hop earned its keep

**Moving the counts from `H − 1` to `H` silently weakens `A1` unless a clause is added, and I only
found that because I ran it.**

QE's mutant `M2` — `map(row => row + \x1b[K)` joined by bare newlines — is the **unsafe** shape with
`H` erases, every one at the margin. **Under the new counts it matches all four exactly**: `\x1b[K` =
23, `\x1b[0J` = 1, `\x1b` = 25, delta = 69. Under the old counts the count alone killed it.

So the three-number substitution my brief describes — correct as far as it goes — would have produced
an `A1` that is **measurably weaker against the exact failure mode this repair exists to prevent**,
while reading as a faithful repair. **The column-0 clause is what restores it**, and it is an
assertion rather than a comment for that measured reason. Recorded loudly because a future hop will
look at `not.toContain("\x1b[K\n")` beside a byte-exact `toBe` and see a redundant line.

## The stale trailing-erase note: RE-ARGUED, not struck, and the disposition FLIPS

The old note said a trailing `\x1b[K` is redundant under `\x1b[0J` and would break the slice
arithmetic, and was **not** added. Under `RF-1` there is **no trailing erase at all — every erase is
leading**, so the note describes a shape the ticket no longer has.

- **The slice-arithmetic half is spent.** That site is edited by `RF-1` regardless — measured, it is
  one of the three tests `RF-1` moves on its own.
- **The redundancy half survives and now points at a different sequence:** the reserved prompt row's
  **leading** erase, the `H`-th one. **Measured: the emission ends `\n\x1b[K\x1b[0J`, both firing at
  row `H − 1` column 0, so it is provably redundant.**

**Decision: keep it. The count is exactly `H`.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **`H × 3` is the shape that was MEASURED clean** on a real terminal at `C == W` and at 80 columns.
   The 491-character `H − 1` variant was **not measured**. This hop does not substitute an argument
   for a measurement on the one surface that has already produced this project's first FAIL.
2. **It costs a slice and a special case** in what is otherwise one expression, to save 3 characters
   against ~2,500x measured headroom. Coding rules 5 and 6.
3. **Both `H − 1` variants FAIL the new `A1`, measured.** Dropping row 0's erase is QE's `M1`.
   Dropping the prompt row's is byte-distinguishable but behaviourally identical — and this project
   already decided that class deliberately at QE's judgement 1 on `M5` (`\x1b[0K` ≡ `\x1b[K`): **the
   criteria pin the emission's spelling, not its meaning.** Same trade, taken the same way.

**One erase per row, `H` of them, is an invariant a reader can restate without an off-by-one.**
`H − 1` is the asymmetry that attached the erase to the join separator, which is how it came to fire
at the margin in the first place.
