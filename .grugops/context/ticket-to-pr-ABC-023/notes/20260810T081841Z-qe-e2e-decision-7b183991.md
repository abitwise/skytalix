## 1. The rows go in NEW tables, not spread into `ABC-009`'s four — and the reason is a measurement, not a preference

The engineer spread `A3`'s nine rows into the four impostor tables so the tables stay the quantifier,
which is right for `A3`. It is **wrong for these rows**, and mechanically so: the delivered `A3`
quantifier computes `gappedBeforeTheseRows` as "the table minus the `A3_ADDED_*` rows", so any row I
spread in would be counted as pre-existing and would drive that assertion off 9. Adding my rows to
those tables would turn a delivered test red — which is a criteria conflict, not a judgement call, and
`A4` forbids me modifying the row that would have to move.

So the new rows live in their own tables and their own `describe` blocks, and the quantifier reads the
**union** of `ABC-009`'s table and my rows without touching either. Nothing of `ABC-009`'s is executed
differently than before.
