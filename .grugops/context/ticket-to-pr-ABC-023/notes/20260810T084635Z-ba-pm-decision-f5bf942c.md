# The deep lane is NOT adequate protection for these seven — `ABC-024` cut into `Backlog`, `S`/`P2`, Definition of Ready **NOT** met (8 of 10 clean, 2 recorded ⚠️)

The question routed to me was not "add two rows". It was **whether a guard protected only by an opt-in
lane is adequately protected** — a judgement about where this project wants its net. It is answered
here, and answered **narrowly**, because a reflexive "add a row for everything perft catches" would be
the bloat this role exists to refuse.

## The ruling

**A guard whose ONLY protection is the `PERFT_DEEP=1` lane is owed a default-lane row. A guard the
default lane already names is not. The deep lane is NOT promoted into `quality.mandatory_gates`.**

Six measured grounds:

1. **The lane is in no gate.** `quality.mandatory_gates` is `["lint","typecheck","unit","build"]`.
   There is no CI — `.github/` holds one file, `copilot-instructions.md`, and `AGENTS.md` records "no
   CI, no git remote". The deep lane runs **only when a ticket's own acceptance names it**
   (`ABC-023`'s `A4` does; `ABC-022`'s and `ABC-009`'s did). That is protection by an author
   remembering, not by a net — and on `ABC-009`, across six hops, the board's own record says the lane
   was measured **once**, after the merge.
2. **This project already made the same ruling once, in code, about this same guard.**
   `src/moves.castling.hardening.test.ts:596-601`, above `NO_RIGHT_HELD`: *"The `PERFT_DEEP=1` lane
   does catch a build that drops the right test … but the default `npm test` does not, and a
   40-second opt-in lane is the wrong place for the only guard on the cheapest condition in the
   function."* Rows were written for that reason. **My ruling is not new** — it is that ruling applied
   to the slice those rows cannot reach, because every one of them sits at `- 0 1`.
3. **The margin is one assertion, not a lane.** All three right-test mutants die on exactly one of the
   32 deep rows (`position-5 perft(4)`); three of the four corridor survivors on exactly one
   (`kiwipete perft(4)`). Re-tune that single row and the guard has nothing.
4. **The signal is a wrong number, not a named failure.** A deep red reads "perft(4) expected 4085603"
   after 36–40 s; it does not read "black was offered `e8c8` with a bishop on c8". Diagnosis cost is
   what a default row buys, and it is what a rewrite needs.
5. **The cost ratio is ~25x** — 625 tests in **1.23 s** against **36.5–39.8 s**. Nobody runs that
   while editing.
6. **`EPIC-004` rewrites the `CastleOption` tables**, where four of the seven survivors live.

**What the ruling deliberately does NOT say**, recorded so it is not over-applied: the deep lane is the
strongest oracle in this codebase and is what **proved** all seven legally reachable (perft walks only
legal play, so a mutant perft can see is one ordinary play reaches); not every deep-lane kill is owed a
unit row; and promoting `PERFT_DEEP=1` into the gate is a config change costing 36–40 s on every gate,
is not this ticket's to make, and would not fix ground 4 anyway. That option is named in the ticket's
`## Out of scope` so the omission stays deliberate.

## The ticket

**`ABC-024`** — *castling guards protected only by the opt-in deep lane: the `empty` corridor on
black, and the right test off its default scalars*. Id verified free at the source
(`plans/tickets/` ends at `ABC-023`; `plans/board.md` names no `ABC-024`).

- **Size `S` (2).** 10 rows for `A1`, 4 + 2 control rows for `A2`, 2 quantifiers for `A3` — **16 rows,
  18 tests**, one existing table-driven file, zero production lines, no new module/export/dependency
  edge/design decision. `ABC-023` was `S` at 17 rows and 19 tests; this is the same calibration.
- **The cheaper shape was considered and REJECTED on a measurement**, not on taste: rows for only the
  four measured survivors is 4 rows, but the other six die by **accident** — five purely through
  incidental shallow-perft/`gameend`/`history` positions no criterion owns — and without `A3`'s
  quantifier the gap regrows the next time the tables change. Marginal cost of the symmetric shape:
  six rows in an existing table.
- **The `L` trap is named so nobody walks into it:** enumerating occupant piece types per corridor
  square (120 rows). `option.empty` tests `!== EMPTY` and is colour- and type-blind **by
  construction** — measured with an enemy occupant — so a second byte on the same square pins nothing
  new. If it is ever wanted, split it.
- **Priority `P2`.** Not `P1` (nothing is wrong in the delivered code, no user affected, no user
  surface until `EPIC-006`); not `P3` (all seven are legally reachable, proved rather than argued).
  It ranks **alongside `ABC-023`, not above it** — `ABC-023`'s `A2` class leaks past perft entirely,
  this one is at least caught somewhere.
- **TWO escalation triggers, both checked at the source and both explicitly NOT FIRED:** (i) any
  ticket proposing to change `addCastlingMoves`, the `CastleOption` tables or the right test — the
  only ticket in `Ready` or beyond is `ABC-013`, whose `## Out of scope` line 79 reads *"Any change to
  `src/legal.ts` or the rules core"*, and `EPIC-004` is still in `Backlog`; (ii) any change to
  `src/perft.test.ts`'s six positions or their `fast` depths, because six of the seven survivors hang
  on exactly one deep assertion.

## Two things I changed relative to the precedent, and why

1. **`A5` requires the mutants to die with `PERFT_DEEP` UNSET.** Without that clause a build could
   satisfy the ticket while leaving the hole exactly where it is. The lane is the witness, never the
   net — that is the ruling, written into acceptance rather than left in prose.
2. **The security trigger is NOT pre-waived; it carries a firing threshold.** `ABC-023` wrote "none,
   structural" against an expected 17-row diff and delivered **+749 lines from two agents**, and the
   Orchestrator had to over-rule that line this morning. So `ABC-024` says: the security half fires if
   the delivered diff exceeds **+525 lines** or touches any file other than
   `src/moves.castling.hardening.test.ts`, against a prediction of **~+350** derived from `ABC-023`'s
   measured +375 for 17 rows. A threshold named up front does not need over-ruling later.
