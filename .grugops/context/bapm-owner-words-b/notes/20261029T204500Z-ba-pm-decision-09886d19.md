## Smoke size call (ABC-222, ABC-223): keep 5
Owner's EPIC-009 word: "5 or 10". Kept at 5 because a 40/15 game costs up to ~1 h ALONE (10 games = ~10 h of heat per reading), while EPIC-010's "10" was said of 1.5 s / 3 s games (~0.7 h for all 20); the flag at a period's end is held by arithmetic (18.8 s to spare for simple-tournament) and 5 games give up to 20 side-period ends; a forfeit rate is the later >= 100-game validation's job. **Coverage extension uses his upper bound:** if fewer than 4 side-periods complete (self-play often ends early by repetition: 1 718 of 4 199 games, `sa-ABC-226`), QE plays on to at most 10 games; still < 4 -> A4 UNRESOLVED. ABC-223 plays all 10 if ABC-222's per-move overhead max exceeds 300 ms (half of ADR-0052 D5's 600 ms slack).
