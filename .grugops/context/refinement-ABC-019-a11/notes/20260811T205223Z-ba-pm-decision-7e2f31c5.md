# The ruling: `ABC-019` is right, `A11`'s WHOLE-TREE PROXY is what died, and the repair is `ABC-029`'s

> **Hand-authored** — `scripts/context-io.ts` does not exist here. `kind: decision` needs no stamp;

> `finding`, because the gate is RED and `human_admission` is `off`.

## 1. The question was framed as a binary and the answer is neither half of it

The brief asked: **is `A11` wrong, or is `ABC-019` wrong?** Measured, it is **neither**, and the
third answer is what makes this cheap.

**`NFR-002`'s structural premise is NOT the thing that died.** The premise the catalog row states in
its own words is scoped to **a renderer** and **a frame**, and it names this very ticket as the owner
of the quantity: *"The quantity belongs to the LOOP (`ABC-019`), not to a frame."* Probed this hop:
the render closure is **11 modules with `history.ts` NOT among them**, the probe is armed, and
**nothing imports `turn-loop.ts`**. The premise is intact and unweakened.

**What died is `A11`'s PROXY for that premise** — *"zero runtime importers among the production
modules"*. That was a sound stand-in for *"no renderer pulls the derive into a frame"* only while
every production module in `src/` was either the rules core or a renderer. `ABC-019` adds the first
that is **neither**: a driver that `ADR-0002` clause 4 **requires** to hold the single
`positionCounts` supplier. **A reading of `A11` under which the architecture-mandated build breaches
the property is not a reading of the property — it is a reading of a proxy that has outlived its
scope.**

## 3. `ABC-029` is NOT pulled in — and three MEASURED reasons, not a preference

`ABC-029`'s `A1` already owns *"both guards derive their file sets from ONE expression"* and *"a
**production module** is defined, once"*; its `## Scope` item 1 is literally **define "production
module"**. The turn loop is the **same undefinedness from the opposite side**: `RISK-ABC017-04` is *a
file the criterion covers and the instrument does not*; this is *a file both instruments cover and the
PROPERTY does not*.

Splitting the repair into `ABC-019` was refused because:

1. **A per-ticket exclusion writes a THIRD hand-maintained filename** into the two places `A1`
   forbids literal filenames, and leaves `ABC-029` a partly-done job — the exact defect it exists to
   remove.
2. **Narrowing the REGEX guard's file set destroys a measured kill.** `perft-bench.ts` is not on the
   frame path, so a frame-path filter applied to the regex removes `WPERFT-STATIC`'s **sole** catcher.
   `ABC-029`'s `## Out of scope` forbids weakening that guard by name.
3. **`A1` is indivisible from `A2`.** One expression for both guards requires `perft-bench.ts` **in**
   for the regex and **out** for the probe until the stdout channel is private — and that channel *is*
   `A2`. `ABC-019` cannot satisfy `A1` without solving `A2`.
