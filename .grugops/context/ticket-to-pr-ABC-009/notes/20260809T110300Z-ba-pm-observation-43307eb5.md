# The mutant campaign: which candidate fixes the SIX criteria as written let through

Seven candidate builds, each made by patching `addCastlingMoves` in a **copy** of merged `main`'s
`src/` outside the repository, each run against (a) `ABC-009`'s six criteria exactly as they were
written, (b) the twelve leaking rows, (c) the four legal-castle controls, (d) the full `npm test`
suite with its skip count, and (e) the `PERFT_DEEP=1` lane. **No file under `src/` in this repository
was modified**, no production code and no test was written by this role, and every mutant was
discarded. This note is the evidence under the ruling note's corrections 1, 2 and 7.

## The three things that fall out of it

**1. The rook precondition alone ships green.** It satisfies purity on the reproduction FEN, the
three-call equality, criterion 3 at exactly 5 moves, the perft-table guard, the full suite at 304/304
with skip count 0 and no existing test modified, and the deep lane at 32/32 — while `legalMoves` still
offers an illegal `FLAG_CASTLE` and still writes a phantom king on four FENs. Stated the sharpest way
round: **delete the king precondition from a correct fix and not one criterion in the ticket fails.**
Delete the rook precondition and `A3` fails. The two conditions did not fail disjoint sets; one of them
failed the empty set.

**2. Occupancy passes even the pre-check's own proposed fix.** Testing both home squares for
non-emptiness rather than for the mover's own king and rook passes all six original criteria **and all
four of the king-off-home rows the Orchestrator asked for**, while leaking eight rows. This is the
defect no pre-check named, and it is why `A5` and `A6` had to be written as presence rows rather than
absence rows. `A3` and `A4` are absence rows — an empty rook square, an empty king square — and an
absence row cannot distinguish an identity test from an occupancy test.

**3. The load-bearing deletions now fail disjoint named sets.** Measured on the mutants above:
deleting the **king** condition fails **`A4` and `A5`, nothing else** (four rows each); deleting the
**rook** condition fails **`A3` and `A6`, nothing else**; weakening both from identity to occupancy
fails **`A5` and `A6` only**. All four legal-castle controls survived every one of the three, so none
is a false red.

## Over-rejection is already covered — measured, so no criterion was added for it

The sibling `ABC-022` carries an explicit "must not over-reject" criterion (`A6`). Adding one here
would be bloat, and that was measured rather than asserted. Suppressing each of the four castling
rights in turn, one at a time, on top of the honest fix, turns the **default** suite red every time:

```
right suppressed   npm test          kiwipete                    also failing
CASTLE_WK          10 failed / 294   depth 1  48 -> 47           position-5 (44 -> 43)
CASTLE_WQ           7 failed / 297   depth 1  48 -> 47           —
CASTLE_BK           4 failed / 300   depth 2  2039 -> 1993       —
CASTLE_BQ           5 failed / 299   depth 2  2039 -> 1994       position-4 (264 -> 258)
```

`src/perft.test.ts`'s **default** fast lane asserts exact node counts, so `A8` ("the full suite is
green") already IS the over-rejection oracle. **`A7` is not, and that is now stated in the ticket**:
the perft-table criterion asserts count **equality between two calls**, not count **correctness**, and
it passes unchanged on a build that never offers a castle at all (measured: 46/46 on kiwipete instead
of 48/48, criterion still green).

  engineer's call and the ticket still does not choose it.

  `src/`; the engineer's gate establishes the tip.
