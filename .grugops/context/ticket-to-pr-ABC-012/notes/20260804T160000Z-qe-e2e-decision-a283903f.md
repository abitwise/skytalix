0/2 → 1/2; no limit approached, no override taken, no written reason owed. The `In Review` exit is
the one board move this role owns.

**It was not earned by re-running the Engineer's suite and reading the green.** Those figures were
re-run only as a control on my own instruments and they reproduce exactly — 221/221 on 10 files,
skip count 0, lint / typecheck / build all exit 0, `git diff --numstat 39e260d -- src/` empty. What
earned the verdict is **21 mutants of the shipped `src/history.ts`**: the Engineer's seven re-run as
a control on its own table, all seven dying exactly as reported, plus fourteen of my own — and
**three of my fourteen survived all 221 delivered tests.** The measurements are in the observation
note `…-observation-a0401665`.

The three survivors are all `resetTo` — the helper `takeBack` rebuilds the borrowed `Position` with
— silently failing to copy `castling`, `blackToMove`, or `epSquare`. **`src/history.ts` is correct
on all three.** These were holes in the **suite**, not defects in the module, which is where both
`ABC-011` review passes also landed. They share one root cause worth stating plainly: **every
acceptance test anchors at the START position and takes back only at EVEN ply counts**, so three of
`resetTo`'s six field copies were never observed at all.

**PASS rather than a fail is the right call, and the reason is not that the gaps are small.** Every
acceptance criterion is met (criterion 2 as ruled below), no criterion depends on a gap, the module
has **zero importers** so nothing downstream inherits anything, and the defect class the gaps hid is
**not present in the shipped code** — it is present in what the suite could prove. A suite hole
found and closed inside the gate is the gate working, not the gate failing.

## The three items the Engineer handed forward, each ruled from the evidence

### 1. Criterion 2 cannot be satisfied as worded — UPHELD, verified with the compiler

Checked against the merged types rather than accepted from the framing, and checked with an
instrument that carries its own null control: `const bad: GameStatus = { result: "draw", reason:
"threefold-repetition" }` is **TS2820**, while the control `reason: "stalemate"` compiles clean. The
claim holds structurally — `EndReason` (`src/gameend.ts:40-45`) and `ClaimableDraw`
(`src/gameend.ts:48`) are disjoint by construction, and `GameStatus`'s first arm admits only an
`EndReason`.

**The criterion's INTENT is met and GREEN**: threefold is reported before the take-back, gone after
it, and the counts rewind with the list. **The wording is routed to BA/PM and neither the ticket's
acceptance prose nor merged `gameend.ts` was edited** — both are out of scope, and this role does
not own ticket text.

**One thing for BA/PM that was on nobody's list, and it changes the shape of the correction:**
`gameStatusOf` **can** return `result: "draw"` for repetition — at **fivefold**, which FIDE 9.6.1
makes automatic and which `gameend.ts` reports as an `EndReason`. So criterion 2 has a satisfiable
re-wording in **either** direction: to the claimable shape the module produces at three occurrences,
or to the automatic draw it produces at five. That is BA/PM's choice, not mine. What I did do is
write the fivefold test, which had never existed — see below.
