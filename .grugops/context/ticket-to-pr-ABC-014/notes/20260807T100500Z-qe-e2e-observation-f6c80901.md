# The mutation campaign — 10 new mutants, ONE survivor, and 4 of the Engineer's re-run as a control

18 mutants had already been run by the Engineer. Re-running the same 18 would have re-measured its
instrument, not the module, so these 10 are **new** and were chosen to attack the places its table
showed were thin. Each is one edit to the shipped `src/render.ts`, applied and restored by a script
that re-checks the md5 after every cycle; the module is
**`733bc67fec523a7f41016bea2b026e1d` afterwards**, the Engineer's own figure.

## The survivor

| Mutant | vs the 17 delivered tests | vs the 27 delivered now |
|---|---|---|
| **N14 — an out-of-domain board byte draws a `?` placeholder instead of throwing** | **SURVIVED (17/17 still green)** | killed by **1** |

The Engineer named this decision in its hand-over (item 5): the ticket's `## Out of scope` puts
position validity on `ABC-009` / `ABC-010`, and AGENTS.md rule 8 forbids handling a scenario that
cannot occur — nothing in merged `src/` produces such a byte. **The decision is right and it is not
re-opened here.** What was missing is that *nothing pinned it*, and the alternative failure mode is
silent: a `?? placeholder` produces a cell of 10 characters, and the constant geometry `ABC-018` and
epic acceptance `4a` stand on would break with no test noticing. A comment cannot fail a build.

**The documented behaviour was verified before it was pinned:** bytes 7, 8, 15, 200 and 255 each
throw a `TypeError` out of the glyph lookup — not a placeholder, not `"undefined"` in the frame. The
new test is a **characterization** of a deliberate decision, not a new requirement, and says so.

**N11 and N13 are the interesting pair.** Both die, so neither is a hole in the strict sense — but
all 16 of their killers are the **two hand-written frames and the six snapshot tests**, and not one
is a test of an acceptance criterion. A defect class whose entire automated defence is a snapshot is
worth naming, and it is why a separate note records what a snapshot is worth in this install.

**N15 confirms the Engineer's item 1 independently.** One killer against the delivered suite, exactly
as handed over. It now has three, one of which (the raw 128-byte board compare) does not route
through `fen.ts` at all, so it does not inherit `toFen`'s correctness or its 64-square window.

## What the campaign says about the module

**Nothing in `src/render.ts` is wrong.** Every mutant that changed observable behaviour was caught
by something, and the one that survived described a scenario the module deliberately does not
handle. Every gap named in this subtask is a gap in the SUITE — which is where the `ABC-011` and
`ABC-012` review passes also landed.
