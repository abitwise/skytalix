## The two survivors (QE-221-a, LOW)
Neither is a product fault: the oracle reads the head as correct on every record. They show two behaviours that no
`uci-clock-mode` row pins.
- **HSCM, the hard stop.** A5's given (`go wtime 3000 btime 3000 movestogo 1`) cannot tell the hard stop from the
  spread:
  - With no increment and t ≥ 1 000, (t − 1 000 − 100m) ÷ m ≤ t − 1 100 for every m ≥ 1, so the hard stop never binds.
    At m = 1 the two terms are equal (1 900).
  - A12's 2 800 is under its 8 900 hard stop. No other row in the mode sends an increment near the clock edge.
  - **What the mutant does:** `go wtime 800 btime 800 winc 2000` hands 1 890 ms with 800 ms on the clock, a loss on
    time (oracle POST-HOC).
  - The shared `safeClockShare` is pinned by `ABC-220`'s `simple-tournament` row ("the hard stop on the clock left …
    winc 500 binc 500 movestogo 1 → 1 900"), so only a mode-specific regression slips through.
- **CLOCKWINSCM, movetime over a clock.** No row in the mode sends `movetime` with a clock. `ADR-0052` D3 states that an
  explicit move time wins over a clock, and D4 lists `movetime` first.
- **The fix is two test-only rows in the mode**, mirroring `ABC-220`'s:
  - `go wtime 3000 btime 3000 winc 500 binc 500 movestogo 1` → 1 900;
  - `go wtime 900000 btime 900000 movestogo 40 movetime 5000` → 5 000.
