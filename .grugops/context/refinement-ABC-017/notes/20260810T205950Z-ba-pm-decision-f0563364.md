## The sharpest defect, in full — D1

The ticket's cost criterion carried its own claim about itself:

> *"the three costs differ by **less than 2x** … **(This is the O(window) proof** — see NFR
> triggers.)"*

and the derivation under it read:

> *"replay to the window's first ply costs **2.2–3.9 µs (measured, flat**, dominated by one
> `parseFen` at 1.62 µs)"*

**Both halves are false, and they fail in opposite directions — the criterion is simultaneously too
tight to be safe and too blind to catch what it exists for.**

**(a) The pane is not `O(window)`, and there is no way to make it so with the state that exists.**
Reaching the window's first ply requires replaying **every prior ply** from `initialFen`.
`GameHistory` is `{ initialFen, moves, position }` — **no undo stack** (`ADR-0002` clause 2 forbids
one), **no stored `UndoRecord`**, **no clone function anywhere in `src/`** (`src/history.ts`'s own
header says so). There is no backward walk to take. Measured at this hop over 40 … 4,000 plies with
the window's content held constant:

**replay = 1.56 µs + 7.42 ns × plies, R² 0.99979.**

Linear, not flat. The quoted band brackets 120–378 plies and misses 40 plies (1.78 µs) at the bottom.
`ABC-016`'s own observation note already contained the contradiction and neither ticket noticed:
its item 4's replay measured **0.01 / 5.18 / 7.77 / 9.75 µs** at 0 / 40 / 120 / 300 plies — visibly
growing — under the heading that both panes *"need the same replay"*.

**(b) What the ratio actually measures is the CONTENT of the window, not the algorithm's
asymptotics.** The same correct implementation, measured on two legal long-game builders:

| builder | 40 plies | 120 | 300 | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ABC-012`'s deterministic LCG game *(the one this ticket's test notes mandate)* | 40.70 µs | 69.26 | 75.10 | **1.845x** |
| a legal knight shuffle, window content identical at every length | 51.39 µs | 52.14 | 54.48 | **1.060x** |

The spread is the cost of `legalMoves` in an opening position versus an endgame one, and the
criterion charges the implementation for it. **1.845x against a 2x bar is 7.7% headroom on a correct
build**, and 47% on the same build with a different fixture. A bar a correct build clears by 7.7% on
one legal fixture is measuring the fixture.

**And the thing it was written to catch, it does not catch.** The `O(plies)` replay term is invisible
to it: 3.80 µs of a 75.10 µs pane at 300 plies.

**The fix has three parts and the third is the one that binds:**

- **≤ 150 µs absolute, unchanged** — and now *validated by measurement* rather than by arithmetic:
  worst measured **75.95 µs at 378 plies, 0.51x**;
- **within 3x, not 2x**, for the measured reason above. `ABC-016` carries `3x` for the identically
  shaped bar and measured 1.88x; the two panes of one frame now carry the same bound;
- **`A11`'s spied call counts** — `gameStatusOf` / `repetitionCounts` / `keyHistory` / `gameStatus`
  **0**, `takeBack` / `playMove` **0**, `parseFen` **≤ 1**, `toSan` **once per rendered ply and never
  more than 16**, at 40 / 120 / 300 / 2,000 plies alike. That is the move `plans/nfr-catalog.md` has
  now made four times, and it is the only clause that fails when the pane starts deriving.

**The `O(window)` label survives as an approximation and the correction is written into the ticket
rather than smoothed away.** The practical consequence is nil inside any legal game — the pane
measures **79.50 µs at 4,000 plies, 0.53x the bar** — and the decision to bound the pane is
unaffected. But `NFR-002`'s section states an `O(window)` claim with a boundary condition and a
falsifier, and its reader is owed the coefficient. **`plans/nfr-catalog.md` was read and NOT
edited**; the coefficient is flagged for Security/NFR to record when the pane is measured on
delivered code, which is the same shape as the measurement that row already says it is owed.

**One thing about this ticket's derivation deserves saying plainly, because the last three hops on
this board have all gone the other way: the NUMBER was right.** It predicted ~70–75 µs and the
measurement is 40.70–75.95 µs. Unlike `ABC-016`'s, this derivation did not omit its dominant term and
did not misprice a term by 30–50x. **What was wrong was the label on the number, and the criterion
built on the label.**
