# The two NFR bars are replaced by four, because the old pair had no population and no aggregation — and a correct build fails both

## The decision

`toSan(position, move, legal) ≤ 0.20 µs` and `toSan(position, move) ≤ 6.5 µs` are retired **as
written** and replaced by `N1`–`N4` against a benchmark position **pinned as a literal FEN**:

```
P60 = 1rb4r/3k4/p1ppNn2/1p1np1Q1/1q1P1B1P/PRP2p2/3KP1BR/6N1 w - - 2 31   (42 legal moves, 3 give check)
```

| id | bar | measured (two runs) |
|---|---|---|
| `N1` | mean `toSan(…, legal)` at `P60` ≤ **1/5** of mean `toSan(…)` over the same 42 moves | 8.62x / 8.65x |
| `N2` | mean `toSan(…, legal)` over all 42 ≤ **0.75 µs** | 0.491 / 0.490 |
| `N3` | mean `toSan(…)` over all 42 ≤ **6.5 µs** | 4.232 / 4.241 |
| `N4` | **no single** legal move exceeds **14 µs** in either form | worst 9.34 / 9.25 |

## Why, in the order the reasons actually bind

**1. The old bars are not a build target, because a correct build fails them.** Measured twice: a
checking piece move costs 9.34/9.25 µs unassisted (1.42x over 6.5) and 4.50/4.49 µs with the list
(22.5x over 0.20). The cause is not slowness — `+` versus `#` requires `legalMoves` on the position
after the move, which is `ADR-0004`'s own definition of the mate suffix. **A bar that a correct
implementation cannot meet is a defect in the bar.** This is the same family as `ABC-023`'s `A4`,
one step milder: that criterion was unsatisfiable outright; this one is unsatisfiable under its
natural reading and satisfiable under an unstated one, which is arguably worse because it looks
fine.

**2. The aggregation and the population are now stated, because they are what the number actually
measures.** The old figures move with the position's check density and its pawn/piece mix, not with
the implementation: a pawn move needs no legal list at all (0.133 µs) and a checking move pays a
second full generation (25x a quiet one). Naming "mean over all legal moves at `P60`" makes the bar
reproducible; leaving it unnamed made two honest engineers measure different things.

**3. `N4` exists so nobody reads `N2`/`N3` as per-call bars again.** That misreading is precisely
what produced the defect, and a mean without a ceiling invites it back.

**4. `N1` is the primary bar and the only machine-independent one.** It is a ratio, so it survives a
hardware change, an `ADR-0003` clause-3 optimisation, and any future guard added to the generator.
It also asserts what the optional parameter is *for* — `ADR-0004` clause 2's justification — rather
than a number that happens to hold today. **This is the "move it off the clock" lesson this board
learned at `ABC-009`'s gate**, applied before the gate rather than after it. I set the threshold at
5x against a measured 8.6x deliberately: the ratio varies with the position's pawn/piece mix, and a
bar with less than ~1.7x of headroom against that variation would fail for the wrong reason.

**5. `N3` is the original 6.5 µs figure, re-measured and CONFIRMED — not moved.** 1.5x of today's
4.23 µs mean is 6.35 µs, so the ticket's own number still bounds it. **Nothing was moved that
measurement did not require moving**, which is this file's standing rule and `plans/nfr-catalog.md`'s.

**6. The position is pinned as a FEN because a method is not a position.** Wave 1 recorded "a
deterministic random-legal game from `startpos`, ply-60" and no seed, so its position is
unreconstructable and its 3.18 µs `legalMoves` figure cannot be compared with today's 4.58 µs. That
is the root cause of this whole defect: the bar could not be re-measured, so it was inherited for
ten days. **No regression from `ABC-009` or `ABC-022` is claimed or refuted** — 4.58 µs is inside
`ADR-0003`'s published 1.8–5.4 µs range and position variance covers the gap entirely.

## Consequences, stated

- **The Security/NFR gate's pass condition changes shape**: it must run the stated aggregation at the
  stated FEN, and `N1` is a ratio it can check on any machine. `N2`/`N3`/`N4` remain
  `UNKNOWN - verify` off Apple M3 / Node v24.12.0 / darwin-arm64.
- **The bars are ceilings, not targets.** They were derived at 1.5x from a *reference* build that
  allocates a rivals array per call, so a tuned implementation should clear them comfortably. Stated
  in the ticket rather than left to be discovered.
- **`NFR-003`'s parenthetical corrected** (~20 µs / ~5,000x, not ~4 µs / ~25,000x) and `NFR-002`'s
  frame headroom re-measured and confirmed at 2.5% of 16 ms for a 120-ply render.
- **`plans/nfr-catalog.md` is NOT edited and no catalog target moved.** `NFR-002` ("< 16 ms") and
  `NFR-003` ("< 100 ms") are untouched; everything above is a ticket-local derived bar.
