## The UNRESOLVED and re-run rules
- **VOID** (THERMAL or RUNAWAY): no figure. A void is not a miss. A re-read is a second run, **asked of the owner first**.
- **UNRESOLVED** (the tool's state, from a Stockfish or harness fault, a missing game or file, no Elo line, or the depth guard): no figure. R2-A1 allows a **re-run once, asked of the owner first** (`QE-273-c`, folded). QE does not start it.
- **A skytalix fault or time loss:** no figure. It is also a product finding on `main`, named with its game, for the Orchestrator. A re-run is the owner's call, asked first.
- QE never re-runs on its own word, never re-plays a clean reading, and never pools two runs.

3. **P > 150.0: an upward step.** The second 200 games are a new run, asked of the owner first (R2-A3). QE stops and returns the question. 4. **P < 50.0: a downward step.** Always the owner's question, for the step and the run. QE stops and returns it, with E′ and the label as in 3.
