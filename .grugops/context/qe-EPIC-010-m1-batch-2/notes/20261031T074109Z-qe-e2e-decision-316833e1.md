## Thermal: pause at the block boundary, resume the same process; hard stop (run 1's design, now scripted)

The tool has no pause and no resume of its own (`freshOut` refuses a non-empty out-dir, and any cause breaks the block
loop). The watcher (`raw/scripts/watch.mjs`, read in full before this note) ticks every 20 s and acts on our pids only:

- **Pause trigger:** the median of the last 30 `thermal.log` samples (about 5 min) **> 85 °C**.
- **Pause:** `SIGSTOP` to the harness `node` process **only**, and only while our `cutechess-cli` is alive with fewer than 20
  `Finished game` lines. The running block plays to its end, so no game is cut and no clock is touched. The stopped harness
  starts no further block. `cutechess-cli` writes to a file, not a pipe, so it never blocks on the stopped harness. The
  tool's 600 s no-output watchdog sees the file grow on resume, so it does not fire.
- **Resume:** once our `cutechess-cli` has exited (a zombie the stopped harness has not reaped counts as exited) and the
  watcher's own Tctl reads < 60 °C on 3 consecutive 20 s checks, the watcher sends `SIGCONT`. The tool judges the finished block
  as usual and goes on, with the same process, out-dir and builds.
- Each pause (stop, block end, resume, seconds) is logged. The play wall is reported with and without the cool-down.
- **THERMAL STOP:** the median of the last 30 `thermal.log` samples > 90 °C, or the median of the watcher's last 15 readings
  > 90 °C while paused. Then `SIGCONT` if stopped, and `SIGTERM` to our engines, our `cutechess-cli` and the harness. The pair is
  UNRESOLVED.

## On a crash or a halt (resume per `ABC-225`: a halted pair is re-run once)

- **Tool halt** (any cause in a block): the tool stops the remaining blocks and writes `summary.txt` with `halted:`. The pair
  is UNRESOLVED, and QE reads the halt on real data (QE-225w1-e). There is no mid-run resume.
  - If the halted session ran **≤ 2 h**: re-run once in this hop, with a fresh out-dir and the same command.
  - Otherwise: STOP and hand back UNRESOLVED. The ≈ 22 h re-run is the Orchestrator's to schedule.
  - A re-run that halts is UNRESOLVED and final for this hop.
- **VOID** (the harness dies without `summary.txt` and without a QE stop, e.g. an external kill or a reboot): recorded, under
  the same ≤ 2 h rule. QE cleans up only its own orphaned processes (by pid).
- **A tool defect** (a wrong argv, a miscount, a formula mismatch, a host-path leak in the tool's records) is a STOP with no
  verdict.

## Contamination (logged, not a stop; others' processes are never killed)

- Per block, QE reports the share of samples with a foreign process. A block more than 25 % contaminated is named
  (information).
