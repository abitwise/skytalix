# The four judgements in this ruling that a gate cannot adjudicate, separated from the measurements

> **Hand-authored.** A `claim` rather than an `observation`, and the split is the one QE drew in this
> ticket's own record: **the measurements underneath are not in doubt** — they are in this hop's
> `observation` note and every one was run — but *"this is the right amount of criterion to write"*
> is a product judgement. A gate can run what a criterion says; it can never judge what a criterion
> should have said. `confidence: UNKNOWN - verify` is on the **dispositions**, not on the readings.

## 1. Declining castle, promotion and en-passant cases on `A5`

**The judgement:** `ABC-013` owns whether `toSan` is correct; this ticket owns whether the panel
**calls** it correctly, and the only call-shape defect available is the legal-list argument, which the
disambiguator case kills. The other three shapes exercise `toSan`'s body through this panel and
duplicate `ABC-013`'s coverage.

**What would change it:** if a wrong build were found whose defect is in the panel's call and is
visible *only* through a castle, a promotion or an en-passant capture, the case earns its place. I
looked for one and did not find one — the remaining call-shape errors (wrong position, wrong move)
are already killed by `A2`'s played-check case and `A5`'s `toFen` clause. **Absence of a found
mutant is weaker evidence than a measured kill**, and it is stated as such.

## 2. Taking `A9`'s forged-status case rather than narrowing the parenthetical

**The judgement:** `D17`'s remedy allows either — close the gap or stop claiming the protection. I
closed it. The input is **constructed and not producible by merged `gameStatusOf`**, which is an
honest argument for narrowing instead: a criterion asserting behaviour on an input the producer
cannot generate is arguably testing a contract nobody can violate today.

**Why I closed it anyway:** `by` is a required field of the public `DrawClaims` interface, `status`
is a **parameter** this panel does not derive, `ABC-021` adds a second producer of claims, and this
ticket merges **first** — so the moment to pin the contract is now and it costs one case. The
criterion states the constructed-ness explicitly so no reader mistakes it for a reachable state.
**A reader who thinks the narrowing is the more honest call is not obviously wrong**, and this is
where that disagreement lives.

## 3. Three `A6` rows rather than one

**The judgement** is argued from a compensating-pair measurement (in the `observation` note) and from
my own two mis-built joint FENs (in the `failed-attempt` note). Both are real; **whether a
compensating `KNIGHT=4, BISHOP=2` build is a realistic wrong build or a contrived one is a
judgement**, and someone who calls it contrived would take QE's single row and save two lines.

## 4. That `M`/`P1` do not move

Fifth re-derivation, and the arguments are in the ticket. **The one input a gate cannot weigh** is
merge order: `ABC-016` merges first of four and its test file becomes the net for `ABC-017`,
`ABC-027` and `ABC-019`. I have recorded that as an argument about **when**, not **rank** — for the
fourth time on this ticket. **If the Orchestrator reads merge-order exposure as a priority input
rather than a scheduling one, that is its call and not mine**, and it is the one place the `P1` could
defensibly be argued at `P0`.
