# RISK-EPIC010-08 checked — C3'' effective resolution with repeated openings

## Scope
The C3'' d.pgn (400 games; 100 openings x 4: rounds r and r+100, colours swapped within each round). Method fixed
in the PREREG (`raw/c3/cluster.mjs`, output `raw/c3/cluster.txt`).

## Observation
- iid per game: SE 0.0193 -> **± 26.4 Elo** (reproduces cutechess's ± 26.4). The ADR's ± 24 assumed 0.50 draws;
  the measured draw ratio is 40 %, which alone gives ± 26.4.
- Per colour-swapped pair (200 pairs): SE 0.0183 -> ± 25.1 Elo (design effect 0.90; the colour swap helps).
- **Per opening cluster (4 games x 100): SE 0.0201 -> ± 27.7 Elo. Design effect 1.094, about 366 effective games.
  This is the effective resolution.** It is 1.05x the iid ± and 1.15x the ADR's ± 24.
- Repeat pairs (same opening, same colours, round r vs r+100): 200; identical result 98 (49.0 %); **identical
  movetext 21 (10.5 %)**, so about 1 in 10 repeats replayed the same game move for move. Per-opening correlation
  between rounds 1-100 and 101-200: r = 0.204. Both halves scored 0.545.
- Reading: the repetition costs about 9 % of the sample (400 -> ~366 effective games). That is not "much wider"
  (PREREG line: > 1.25x iid or > 30 Elo), so **nothing goes to the Architect**. The score's 95 % interval under
  the cluster SE is 0.506-0.584, which excludes 0.500.

## Risks
One read. The design effect is itself noisy at 100 clusters; another epic's read may differ. RISK-EPIC010-08 can
be stated as "± 24 reads about ± 28 at 40 % draws with 4 repeats" (INFO, QE-E010C-b).
