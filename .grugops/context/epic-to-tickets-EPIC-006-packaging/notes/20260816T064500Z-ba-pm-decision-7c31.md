# THREE tickets, and the packaging portion is ONE — the decision, never the answer

`EPIC-006`'s second half (packaging, difficulty, `undo`) is cut as **three** tickets inside
`ABC-100`…`ABC-115`. Sixteen ids were available; **thirteen are unused and that is the result, not a
shortfall.**

| ticket | subject | size | pri | DoR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ABC-100` | The distribution channel — a **superseding ADR** on the owner's word | `S` | `P1` | 8/10 ⚠️×2 |
| `ABC-101` | Difficulty — the user-facing **selection** only | `M` | `P2` | 7/10 ⚠️×3 |
| `ABC-102` | `undo` — two plies back in one command | `S` | `P1` | 7/10 ⚠️×3 |

**7 points. Largest ticket is `M`, so no `SPLIT_REQUIRED`.**

## The packaging portion is ONE ticket and it contains no packaging

The distribution channel is **UNDECIDED and an agent may not decide it**. Four sources say so and
they agree — `AGENTS.md` § Deployment posture (*"Do not fill that in."*), `ADR-0007` clause 5,
`ADR-0007` detection row `D5` (*"handled by a **superseding** ADR, not by a ticket"*), and the board
row (*"distribution stays with a human"*).

So `ABC-100`'s deliverable is **the decision record**: a superseding ADR authored by
Architect/Design on the owner's word, with npm publish / standalone binary / `git clone` + `npm
start` each priced against this repo's actual state (`private: true`, no `bin`/`main`/`exports`, no
git remote, `npm run build` is `tsc --noEmit` and emits nothing).

**No second packaging ticket was cut, deliberately.** The *scope* of the packaging work is a
function of the answer — the three channels share almost no work — so cutting it now would be
inventing scope for a channel nobody chose. **Nothing downstream of the channel is cuttable, and
that is recorded rather than smoothed.**

**What was NOT written, checked key by key:** no `bin`, `main`, `exports`, `files` or
`publishConfig`; `private: true` untouched; no npm assumption anywhere in any of the three tickets;
`production_requires_human_confirmation` untouched and asserted `true` by `ABC-100` `A5`.

## The subject boundary, honoured as ruled

- **Difficulty:** the flag, the level set, the default and what the human sees are on `ABC-101`.
  **Search depth, time budget, blunder rate and evaluation strength are `EPIC-004`'s and are not
  cut here.** `EPIC-004` is depended on **by name and behaviour**; **no `EPIC-004` ticket id is
  written anywhere in this hop's output** — its ids are being minted in parallel.
- **`RISK-002` (GO WITH CONSTRAINT, `plans/nfr-catalog.md:536-541`) is respected rather than
  rediscovered:** the per-move budget must be **time-based, not depth-based**. `ABC-101` therefore
  makes a level an **opaque ordinal** carrying no depth, no milliseconds and no node count, and
  `A7` is a grep that fails a build which attaches one.
- **`undo` is cut outright** as `ABC-102`.
- **`ABC-041`…`ABC-046` were not re-cut, re-scoped or renumbered.** The `## DECOMPOSED` section of
  the epic was not touched.

## The four product rulings made (and the ones refused)

Made, because the user-facing selection is BA/PM's: difficulty levels are integers **1…5**; the
default is **3, the middle** (derived from `NFR-004`'s two different bars — *"≤ 3 s at **default**"*
vs *"5 s at **any**"* — which have content only if some level is slower than the default); the level
is **fixed for the session**; it is shown **once at startup**, not in the per-ply status panel.
`undo` is **two plies, repeatable, refused below two plies, unconfirmed**.

Refused: **what a level does** (`EPIC-004`), **which loop arm carries a state-changing command**
(Architect/Design), **the distribution channel** (the owner).
