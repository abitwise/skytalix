# What this gate could NOT settle, one correction to the Engineer, and what I carried rather than fixed

Recorded as a `claim` at `confidence: UNKNOWN - verify` because none of it is gate-adjudicable here.
**There is no §14 gate on this install, so no `finding` is admissible on this task, none was written,
and nothing is self-stamped.** `context.human_admission` is `off`, so the `human:<name>` escape hatch
is unavailable too.

## 1. CORRECTION — "guard (a) may become deletable" is over-stated, and the mutation data says so

The Engineer's claim note item 5 says guard (a) exists because `generateMoves` is unsound on the
invalid class — it offers a capture onto the mover's own piece — so *"guard (a) is a workaround with
a named owner, not a permanent design fact. If BA/PM cuts that ticket and it is fixed, guard (a) may
become deletable."*

**Half of that is fair and half of it is wrong.** Guard (a) is load-bearing for **two** distinct
classes, and only one of them is the routed defect:

| class | example | is `generateMoves` defective there? | oracle |
|---|---|---|---|
| the **mover's OWN** piece on the ep target | `4k3/8/8/8/3pP3/4n3/8/4K3 b - e3 0 1` | **yes** — it offers the ep move; this is the BA/PM defect | `true` |
| an **ENEMY** piece on the ep target | `4k3/8/8/8/3pP3/4N3/8/4K3 b - e3 0 1` and its mirror | **no** — it correctly reads the occupied target as an ordinary capture and never offers the ep move | `false` |

Deleting guard (a) fails **both** pairs: 4 of its 8 failures come from each. Fixing the generator
would leave the second class needing guard (a) exactly as much as it does today — there, guard (a)
is what makes the fast predicate *agree* with a correct generator, which is the opposite of a
workaround.

**Naming the thread is right and I would keep it.** What I would not keep is the word "deletable",
and I would not want a later reader to delete guard (a) on the strength of a BA/PM fix and discover
the enemy-piece pair the hard way. Non-blocking, and the code is correct as shipped.
**Owner: whoever picks up the BA/PM `generateMoves` ticket; Architect/Design if it wants the
distinction written into `ADR-0005`.**

## 2. `NFR-005`'s cost bars are unverified by me, deliberately

I re-measured **no** timing figure. The Engineer's −0.3…+2.6 ns per call, ≤ +0.41 ns amortized,
20–25x on the invalid class, and its 291.3 / 313.6 ns anchors are `UNKNOWN - verify` **from this
role** — not because I doubt them, but because Security/NFR owns them next and re-gates after me,
and two roles measuring the same bar is waste. What I re-derived is the *structural* half: the
guards sit inside the callee after its own `NO_SQUARE` early return, so the caller's short-circuit
never reaches them; `src/zobrist.nfr.test.ts`'s zero-call assertions pass untouched, and over
518,474 reachable nodes neither guard fires once.

**Two things bearing on that gate, flagged rather than judged:**

- **The Engineer caught and discarded its own first benchmark protocol** after a null control on a
  structurally-zero delta read up to **1.26x**. It re-ran with 9 alternating rounds and medians and
  the control fell to +0.06 / +0.20 ns. **That self-report is honest and it is the third protocol on
  this ticket to hit the same wall.** It is also the reason the whole-key ep-set cells (+0.66 and
  −0.42 ns) are the same size as the control's own error and support nothing finer than "not
  measurably slower". I take no view on the numbers themselves. Owner: Security/NFR.
- **Two cost-shaped equivalent mutants are pinned by nothing in the repo:** deleting
  `hasLegalEnPassant`'s own `NO_SQUARE` early return (now equivalent *because guard (a)* catches it),
  and moving both guards **inside** the `EP_CAPTURE_SIDES` loop. Same answers, same keys, different
  work. No assertion about a key can ever fail them; a spied call count is the instrument, which is
  that column's shape. Owner: Security/NFR. Recorded as a gap, not as a demand.
