## The verdict rule

- The tool prints per build cutechess-cli 1.5.1's pooled `Elo difference: X +/- Y` (over its 10 blocks) and the pair line
  **X = X_head − X_base, Y = √(Y_base² + Y_head²)** on the printed one-decimal values, with `EPIC-008`'s state
  (`pair.ts` `stateOf`).
- **LOSS iff X + Y < 0. Not LOSS iff X + Y ≥ 0** (EPIC-010 layer 3; `EPIC-008`'s merge rule). NEUTRAL and GAIN are both not LOSS.
- QE recomputes X and Y from the two pooled lines and each build's pooled W/L/D with its own copy of the cutechess formula
  (`scripts/elo.py`); a mismatch with the tool's line is a tool defect and a STOP.
- **UNRESOLVED** (any cause in any block: a fault by either side, a time forfeit, a stall, fewer games, a missing raw file, a
  coverage or formula miss; or a VOID run) gives **no verdict**: neither LOSS nor not LOSS.
- The pentanomial (opening-paired) line is **information only** and judges nothing.
- **Score and half-width (both builds; the head's is the trend figure):** score s = (W + D/2) / N over the build's games; 95 %
  half-width = 1.96 × √(σ² / N), σ² = the per-game score variance over the N games (population). Reported in points of
  percentage.
- **On not LOSS:** the head's R score s (with its half-width) is the new 4 000 ms trend reference (EPIC-010 § After a passing
  batch), and the trend flag reads s − 20 points. QE records the figure; BA/PM folds it.
- **On LOSS:** QE lists the ledger's suspects with their R smoke scores (information only) and the non-EPIC-010 `src/` merges
  above; the options go to the owner via BA/PM. No re-run.

## Thermal: pause at the tool's block boundary, resume the same process; hard stop

The tool has **no pause and no resume of its own** (read in `tools/rating/rating.ts`: `freshOut` refuses a non-empty out-dir;
any cause breaks the block loop). Its halt point is the end of a block-run. So QE's pause works on that boundary, by process
signals, with no edit to `tools/`:

- A watcher ticks every 20 s. It reads the tool's `thermal.log` (10 s samples) and its own Tctl (k10temp).
- **Pause trigger:** the median of the last 30 `thermal.log` samples (≈ 5 min) **> 85 °C**.
- **Pause:** QE sends `SIGSTOP` to the harness `node` process **only**, and only while the current block's `cutechess-cli` is
  alive and has fewer than 20 `Finished game` lines (so it still writes output after the stop). `cutechess-cli` and its engines
  are not touched: the running block plays to its end, so no game is cut and no clock is touched. The stopped harness starts no
  further block. (Writing more output after the stop keeps the tool's 600 s no-output watchdog from firing on resume.)
- **Resume:** once that `cutechess-cli` has exited and the watcher's Tctl reads < 60 °C on 3 checks 20 s apart, QE sends
  `SIGCONT`. The tool judges the finished block as usual and starts the next one. Same process, same out-dir, same builds.
- Each pause window (stop, block end, resume) is logged. The tool's own sampler is frozen while paused, so the watcher's 20 s
  readings cover that window. The play wall is reported with and without the cool-down time.
- **Hard stop (THERMAL STOP):** the median of the last 30 `thermal.log` samples, or of the watcher's last 15 readings while the
  harness is stopped, **> 90 °C**. Then `SIGCONT` (if stopped), `SIGTERM` to the harness, our `cutechess-cli` and its engine
  children (by pid; nobody else's). The pair is UNRESOLVED; no re-run in this hop.
- Busy-core frequency is logged by the tool and reported per block as information; it is not a cause in the batch.

## On a crash or a halt (resume per `ABC-225`: a halted pair is re-run once)

- **Tool halt** (any cause in a block): the tool stops the remaining blocks and writes `summary.txt` with `halted:`. The pair is
  **UNRESOLVED**. QE reads the halt on real data (QE-225w1-e). `ABC-225`'s records (`se-ABC-225` `…14fdbedc`): *"A halted pair
  is a re-run, per EPIC-008's 're-run once'"*. There is no mid-run resume (the tool has none). **Re-run once in this hop only if
  the halted session ran ≤ 2 h**: a fresh out-dir, the same command. Otherwise QE STOPS and hands back UNRESOLVED; the re-run
  (≈ 18.5 h ALONE) is the Orchestrator's to schedule. A re-run that halts is UNRESOLVED and final for this hop.
- **VOID** (the harness exits non-zero or dies without `summary.txt` and without a thermal stop, e.g. an external kill or a
  reboot): recorded; the same ≤ 2 h re-run-once rule. QE then cleans up only its own orphaned `cutechess-cli` / engines (by pid).
- **A tool defect** (a wrong argv, a miscount, a formula mismatch, a host-path leak) is a STOP: QE reports it, no verdict.

## Contamination (logged, not a stop)

- The watcher logs every 60 s: foreign `vitest` count, `cutechess-cli` count beyond ours, Stockfish and skytalix engine
  processes not under our `cutechess-cli`, load1, and the top 3 CPU processes every 5 min. QE also checks about every 30 min.
- Foreign processes are **logged and never killed**. Per block, the share of samples with a foreign process is reported. A block
  more than 25 % contaminated is named in the report (information). Records-only hops beside QE start no engine and no vitest.
