## The UNRESOLVED and re-run rules
- **VOID** (THERMAL or RUNAWAY): no figure for that level. A re-read is a second run, **asked of the owner first**.
- **UNRESOLVED** (a Stockfish or harness fault, a missing game or file, no Elo line, the depth guard): no figure for that level. Any re-run is **asked of the owner first**; QE does not start it.
- **A skytalix fault or time loss:** no figure for that level. It is also a product finding on `b6366a79`, named with its game, for the Orchestrator. A re-run is the owner's call, asked first.
- **WAIT or a gate miss:** no game was played; nothing is read. A later launch is a new W, on the Orchestrator's line.
- QE never re-runs on its own word, never re-plays a clean reading, and never pools two runs.

## The band-rule consequence of each outcome (`ABC-204` A4, `ADR-0056` D5 item 4; the cut note)
Let P be skytalix's points of 200 at a level. A step is one new 200-game run at E′ = E + Δ, rounded to 10 and clamped to 1 320–3 190. **One step per level, run only on the Orchestrator's word;** QE stops and returns the question.
1. **VOID, UNRESOLVED or a skytalix fault:** no figure (rules above).
2. **50.0 ≤ P ≤ 150.0:** E is confirmed. X = E + Δ ± Y is that level's figure. No step.
3. **P > 150.0 (above 75 %):** an upward step is allowed, on the Orchestrator's word. This run's X is reported, labelled "outside the band; not the level's figure".
4. **P < 50.0 (below 25 %):** a downward step is allowed, on the Orchestrator's word. Label as in 3.
5. **P = 200 or P = 0 (Δ not finite):** reported only as "above E" or "below E" on this scale. E′ cannot come from Δ; the Orchestrator names it with the step, if he orders one.
- **A step** is a PREREG addendum sealed and committed before its first game. It names E′, its `pickDepth`, and that it is this PREREG's one allowed step at that level. It then runs `session.sh L E′ <out-root>/lL-eE′ <repo>`, these same sealed bytes, under a new W. At E′: 50.0 ≤ P ≤ 150.0 gives the level's figure. Outside that, **no further step**: P > 150 is reported as a lower bound "above about E′ + Δ − Y", P < 50 as an upper bound "below about E′ + Δ + Y" (the 95 % ends), and P = 200 or 0 as "above E′" or "below E′".
