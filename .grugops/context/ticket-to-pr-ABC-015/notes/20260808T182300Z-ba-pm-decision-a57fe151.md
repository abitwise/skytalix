## What promotion does and does not mean

**Does not:**
- **It is not the pull gate.** The Definition of Ready is applied again by the Orchestrator at
  `Ready → In Development` (Workflow 04 step 1), against the ticket as corrected. This is the column
  exit, not the hand-off to engineering.
- **It does not re-open the pull order.** The owner's 2026-08-08 call put `ABC-015` ahead of
  `ABC-009` and `ABC-022`. That is the owner's queue to order; both defects keep `P1` and their place
  in `Ready`, and this note neither ratifies nor argues with the displacement — the routing note
  already records it.
- **It does not move the ticket past `Ready`**, implement anything, or touch `src/`.
- **It does not discharge the merge-order constraint.** `ABC-015` merges **with or before `ABC-018`**,
  never after. `ABC-018` is unstarted and in `Backlog`, so pulling this now is the constraint working,
  not being bent.
