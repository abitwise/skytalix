# The soft edges of this hop — everything here is `UNKNOWN - verify` and none of it is load-bearing

Recorded so the next gate does not mistake a soft edge for a measured result.

1. **The mutant space is sampled, not covered.** Fourteen trees is more than any prior hop on this
   ticket and it is still a sample. The two half-deletions are exhaustive over *this diff* — there
   are exactly two conditions to delete — but the space of alternative implementations is unbounded,
   and three of my fourteen were survivors that nobody had built before, which is itself evidence
   that the next fourteen would find something. **"That was the last one" has been wrong on this
   board every single time.**

2. **The hardening file is a QUANTIFIER over the FEN-producible domain, not over the byte space.**
   `parseFen` accepts 13 codes per square; bytes above `0x0F` and the invalid low codes are not
   FEN-producible and make the generator throw when written directly. BA/PM's out-of-repo 512-state
   and 131,072-state sweeps remain the only cover for those, and **I did not re-derive either of
   them**. If those sweeps are wrong, nothing at this hop would have caught it.

3. **`A5`'s enemy-occupant axis is knowingly left as a sample of two piece types.** I could have
   closed it and chose not to, because closing it falsifies the sibling file's *"fails only here"*.
   That is a judgement about which of two defects is worse, not a measurement. A reviewer who thinks
   the coverage matters more than the comment is disagreeing with a choice; the remedy is one BA/PM
   edit plus ten rows, and it is cheap either way.

4. **That the three survivors are plausible engineering is a judgement.** *"Some enemy piece is
   behind it"* and *"EMPTY or the mover's own queen"* are shapes I constructed. They are the exact
   shapes `ABC-009`'s campaign found in the castling branch, which is why I looked for them — but
   "an engineer would plausibly write this" remains an argument, not a measurement. What **is**
   measured is that each passes everything and each corrupts the board.

5. **Adding 120 tests to close a criteria-level gap is the one place this hop could be wrong in a way
   anyone cares about.** I read `A1`/`A3`'s *"at minimum"* and the `ABC-009` hardening precedent as
   authorising it without a criteria amendment. A reviewer who reads those tables as closed
   enumerations would want BA/PM to widen them first. The remedy in that case is to hold the file,
   not to change the verdict — the production code is correct either way.

   count must stay at **439**" as a condition on the comment-only hops. This hop adds a new file and
   the suite is now **559 on 18 files**. I read that condition as scoped to those hops' comment-only
   instrument rather than as a standing acceptance requirement, and `A9`'s own text — *"no existing
   test is modified"* — is untouched by an addition. **Flagged because it is an interpretation.**
