# What I could NOT repair inside `ABC-042`, each with its named owner — and the four tickets I did not cut

Per the hop's limits: **no new tickets on this hop** unless a repair genuinely cannot be made inside
`ABC-042`. **None of the five items below needed one, and I cut none.** Four tickets were cut at the last
hop and none was folded in; that discipline is kept here.

## 1. `RF-6` — `plans/board.md:3`'s "0-of-47 … invariant BREAKS" sentence. **Owner: the Orchestrator.**

The sentence reads *"`src/`'s **0-of-47**-production-Node-builtin invariant **BREAKS** at `ABC-042`, which
spawns."* Under Security's RULING 1 the invariant **does not break**: production stays at 0 of 18. The
numerator is production-scoped (18) and the denominator counts tests (47), and the measured all-files
figure is **4**, not 0 and not 2. **Board text is the Orchestrator's alone; I was forbidden the file on this
hop and did not touch it.** Nothing in `ABC-042` restates the false sentence, so the ticket is not carrying
it forward.

## 2. The superseded builtin count — recorded so it is not re-cited

The Orchestrator's entry-review `claim` (`…claim-10a5bfe5`) states *"**2 of those 47** import a builtin
today."* **Measured by Security/NFR: 4 of 47** — the two missed are `src/render-seam.test.ts:39` and
`src/san.test.ts:27`, both `from "node:fs"`. **I used 4, not 2.** The claim's structural point (stated
denominator ≠ stated numerator) survives; its number does not. I have not written either figure into the
ticket — the ticket now states only the production floor (**0 of 18 import anything non-relative**), which
is the invariant that is actually true and is stronger than the one it replaces.

## 3. `AR-1` — argv is a NEW source of C1/CSI, and `R-A` was not written against it. **Owner: BA/PM (me).**

`ABC-019`'s `R-A` rules that C1/CSI (`U+0080`–`U+009F`, `U+009B` = 8-bit CSI) survives `sanitize` in
`unicode` mode — **L2, outside the active L1 set, not reachable today**. `ABC-042` makes it reachable from a
new direction: a user-typed argument echoed into a usage message. **Security ruled no fix required and said
requiring one here would be gold-plating past a standing ruling. I agree and did not add a criterion.**
What I did instead: bar 2 now **names its character class explicitly** (C0, `U+007F`, `\x1b`) and states in
one clause that C1/CSI is deliberately outside it, citing `R-A`. **So the gap is visible on the page rather
than silent, and no scope was added.** Recorded here so that when `R-A` is next read, the new source is on
the record.

## 4. `ADR-0011` clause 3's *"game-owning driver"* wording. **Owner: Architect/Design, via an `ADR-0011`
amendment hop.**

The second licence entry is a **composition root**, not a driver. Architect/Design flagged that the sentence
describes the class it happened to match rather than the class it names, refused to widen it on this hop on
principle (*"amend the rule to match what shipped"*), and named the route. **The property clause 3 guards is
satisfied and `A12` is checkable as written, so `ABC-042` is not blocked on it.** It is recorded in
`## Dependencies` as **not a blocker**, with its owner — not cut as a ticket, and not fixed by me: the ADR is
not mine and the trigger fires the day `ABC-042` lands, not before.
