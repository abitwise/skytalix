## 2. SG3 (`ADR-0077` d7), hold 1
**The instrument is S2, by `ADR-0055` D3 as amended by D3-V2, unchanged:** `qe-ABC-254-a4v2`'s driver (`…a1136cf6` § "D3-V2, as implemented"), cut to d7. `probe.mjs` and `host.mjs` are byte-identical to that hop's. The lines changed in `s2.mjs`, `analyse.mjs`, `grade.mjs` and `run.sh` are marked `298` in each file.
- **A run:** one fresh process runs `chooseMove(parseFen(fen), { budgetMs: 4000 })` on the wall clock. nps = nodes ÷ elapsedMs × 1 000. Base and head run on the same Node binary.
- **Boards:** kiwipete, position-6 and the start position. There is no in-check board (d7).
- **Statistic:** per board, head's median nps ÷ base's median nps over the 5 counted pairs, unrounded.
- **Admission, blind to build and outcome.** A pair is admitted only when, read after the pair, all of these hold:
  - no foreign vitest and no `cutechess-cli` is present;
  - both runs read foreign cores ≤ 0.8. Foreign cores = (host busy CPU-s from `/proc/stat` − the probe's own CPU-s) ÷ wall;
  - both runs' after-run Tctl is below 85 °C.
  
  A pair that is not admitted is discarded and logged with its reasons. It never enters a median, a spread or a ratio. Admission reads host counters and the probe's CPU time, never nodes, nps, depth or a ratio.
- **Count and order:**
  - a board's reading is its first 5 admitted pairs;
  - the next attempt runs base first when the board's admitted count is even, and head first when it is odd;
  - a retry keeps the discarded attempt's order;
  - boards go round by round, kiwipete, position-6, start.
- **Cap:** at most 15 attempts per board per schedule. Fewer than 5 admitted at the cap is **VOID-LOAD**: not a result, not a miss, and no re-run is owed for it.
- **The spread rule, D3's 5 %:** (max − min) ÷ median per build and board, unrounded. Above it, the board takes one re-run, then VOID.
- **The agreement guard (D3-V2 item 4)** reads d7's two numbers, 1.03 and 1.00. If the median of the 5 paired ratios and the graded ratio fall on different sides of either, the board takes its one re-run, then VOID.
- **The one re-run** is a fresh schedule for that board, under the same rules, with a fresh cap of 15. It replaces the main reading. `run.sh` launches it from `grade.mjs`'s `owed` list, inside the same hold.
- **(v1) values, unchanged:** at equal completed depth, base and head choose the same move and score. A difference is STOP-identity, with no grade (exit 4). It is F2's defect check, never a re-pin.
- **(v3) thermal, unchanged:** a board whose counted runs' median after-run Tctl is above 85 °C is a thermal STOP, UNRESOLVED.
- **Start precondition, unchanged in its terms:**
  - 0 foreign vitest and 0 `cutechess-cli`;
  - no foreign process above 50 % of one core over a 3 s sample;
  - load1 ≤ 2.0 and Tctl ≤ 60 °C, polled every 15 s.
  
  **One change (`298`): the cap is lock acquisition + 15 min, and a cap reached stops the read unread** (exit 5). It is never SHARED. In a held window a host that never clears is not a held host. **A read in which any gate passed its cap is UNRESOLVED (SHARED) and is not graded.** Both terms are stricter than `…a1136cf6`'s 45 min and its SHARED label. Neither loosens a bar.
- **No core pinning: QE-298-k.** The S2 runs are not under `taskset`.
  - d7 names the instrument "unchanged" and points at the instrument `qe-ABC-254-a4v2` graded with. `ADR-0055`'s second addendum item 2 says search reads "use D3-V2 as written, with no cool-down gate and no pinning". The first addendum rejects `taskset` pinning because it "changes what is measured (V8 helper threads)".
  - The owner's one-core rule is kept as one process at a time, under the lock, with nothing else running in the held window.
  - **If the Orchestrator's stage-B line requires `taskset -c 0` for SG3, that changes the instrument d7 fixes.** It then goes to the Architect before any figure, as an addendum sealed before the first run. QE does not choose it.
- **The bar (unrounded): MET when head ÷ base is ≥ 1.03 on at least two of the three boards and ≥ 1.00 on the third.**
- **Grading** is mechanical, by `grade.mjs`, in this order:
  1. any board owed a re-run → re-run;
  2. any board SHARED → UNRESOLVED;
  3. a thermal STOP → UNRESOLVED;
  4. any board VOID or VOID-LOAD → UNRESOLVED;
  5. else MET or NOT MET (bar).
- **Outcomes (d7):**
  - **MET:** SG3 passes.
  - **NOT MET (F4):** SG0–SG2 are green and the bar is missed. Not merged. QE records a `failed-attempt` note, and BA/PM closes the ticket as dropped. With the owner's yes to question 2, V3 closes.
  - **UNRESOLVED (VOID, VOID-LOAD, SHARED, thermal):** not a result and not a miss. It may be re-read under a fresh pre-registration, on its own runs alone, never pooled.
  - **No sealed term is waived on a close figure.**
- **Tested before the seal, with no search** (`s2/test/`, under the host lock):
  - `grade-cases.mjs`: 17 synthetic cases through `grade.mjs`. They cover MET; the edges at exactly 1.03, 1.00 and a 5.0 % spread; a bar miss; a third board below 1.00; a re-run owed by spread and by each guard bar; a re-run that fixes the board; VOID by spread and by the guard; VOID-LOAD in each schedule, which is not a miss; SHARED; and thermal.
