# G-2 disposition: no ticket, one escalation to Architect/Design, and one guard I took myself

QE's G-2 holds that `ADR-0002` clause 4's **decision** is right but the **reason recorded for it** is
weak enough that a future reader could refute it in one grep and reverse a correct decision. The ADR
records: *"making the parameter required would be a breaking change to merged, `Done`, 100%-covered
code."* QE measured that break — **9 test call sites and 0 production call sites**, because
`gameStatus` has zero production callers anywhere in `src/`. As a reason it does not survive
inspection.

The stronger reason QE identified, which the ADR does not make: **clause 3 has `EPIC-004`'s search
keep its own per-ply key array and decide repetition itself**, so a search leaf calling
`gameStatus(position)` **bare is a legitimate second caller shape, not a mistake**. A required
parameter would force the hot path to construct something it deliberately does not want.

**I agree with QE on both halves: the disposition stands, the stated reason does not.**

## The ruling, in three parts

### 1. No ticket. Explicitly.

A ticket here would produce no code, no test and no observable behaviour change — its whole
deliverable would be a paragraph in a ratified ADR that belongs to another role. Three concrete
harms, which is why this is a decision rather than a shrug:

- It would sit in `Ready` indefinitely against a `Ready` column that is already 6/8 and a delivery
  rate of zero.
- A ticket against `ADR-0002` **reads as though the clause were under review.** It is not. The
  decision is correct and nothing about it is being reopened — advertising the opposite is the
  failure this whole disposition exists to prevent.
- The Definition of Ready cannot be met honestly: there is no measurable acceptance criterion for
  "a paragraph now persuades".

### 2. Escalated to Architect/Design — the ADR's owning role, not the owner.

The stronger reason should be **on the record in the ADR**, and `ADR-0002` has already established
the exact idiom for it: on ratification it took two edits labelled in place as *"Factual refresh on
ratification — NOT a clause amendment"*, precisely so a reader can tell an amendment from a
correction at a glance. An addendum recording clause 3's legitimate bare caller fits that idiom.

**That edit is Architect/Design's to make and I did not make it.** `ADR-0002` is human-ratified
(Olger Oeselg, 2026-07-30, all four clauses as proposed) and its clauses are not mine to touch — nor
is the file. The escalation carries one condition: **if Architect/Design judges the addendum to be a
material change to clause 4 rather than a correction to its rationale, it goes to a named human.**
No agent may amend a ratified clause.

**Not escalated to the owner.** Nothing here needs a product ruling: the decision the owner ratified
is the decision that stands. Sending it up would be asking a human to re-ratify something nobody is
proposing to change.

### 3. The guard I own, and took now.

The risk QE names is *"a future reader refutes the stated reason and reverses a correct decision"*.
The reader most likely to do that is not a reader of the ADR — it is the **engineer on the ticket
that would be the site of the reversal**. There are exactly two, and both are mine:

- **`ABC-012`** (the history module, `Ready`, `P0`) — `## Out of scope` now carries *"Making
  `gameStatus`'s `positionCounts` required"* with the **strong** reason written out, and explicitly
  flags the ADR's stated reason as not the one to carry forward.
- **`ABC-020`** (cut today, `Ready`, `P0`) — the same exclusion in its `## Out of scope`, plus a
  named subsection *"Why `positionCounts` stays optional — the reason to record, which is not the one
  on file"*, plus acceptance **A8**, which turns the trap's blast radius into a **test**: with the
  counts map omitted, neither the fivefold draw nor the threefold claim is reported — and the game
  still terminates within 150 plies on the clock-based 75-move backstop.

**That is the practically important half of QE's finding closed today**, at the two places a
reversal could actually be attempted, without editing anyone's ratified ADR.

## What I did NOT do, stated so it is not mistaken for an oversight

- **I did not edit `ADR-0002`.** Not the clause, not the Consequences section, not a footnote.
- **I did not cut a ticket to make `positionCounts` required.** Clause 3 wants a bare caller; that is
  the point.
- **I did not treat G-2 as a defect.** QE measured its blast radius at zero production callers, and
  `ABC-019`'s acceptance already spy-asserts that every `gameStatus` call in a run carries a counts
  map. What is unguarded is the set of future callers — `EPIC-004`'s leaf, `EPIC-005`'s book probe,
  `EPIC-006`'s result screen — and the honest place to close that is each epic's decomposition, not a
  ticket written today against three epics that do not exist.
- **I did not raise `ABC-020`'s A8 to a claim of safety.** It pins the consequence; it does not
  remove it. The trap is assigned, exactly as the ADR says.
