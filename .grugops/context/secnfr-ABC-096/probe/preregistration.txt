  The timed span is the seam call alone, seam(history, status, false, []); status is computed before the timers. Per
  process: the first seam call (cold JIT) and 200 warm calls (median, p99, max).
  GRADED as A4 (its literal Given): a4-ascii ("x" x 100,000, ascii), a4-unicode (same, unicode), a4-astral (U+1D11E x
  100,000 code points, unicode).
  GRADED as Security/NFR's NFR-002 extension (the same bar; not A4's text): x-sep (100,000 chars of "a: ", split-heavy),
  x-max ("x" x 1,048,576 = BOOK_MAX_BYTES, the largest one parse-admitted name could be), x-real (the REAL probe over the
  shipped book at 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5, unicode; the lazy load is triggered before the timers).
  STATE per case: MET iff, in all 10 processes, the first frame < 16 ms AND the warm max < 16 ms; else NOT MET.
