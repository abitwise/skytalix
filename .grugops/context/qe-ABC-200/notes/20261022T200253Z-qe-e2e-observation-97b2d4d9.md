## How it was driven (pinning the criterion's `UNKNOWN - verify` spelling)

Keys go in with `send`: Enter is `\r`, Ctrl-D is `\004`, Ctrl-U is `\025`. The exit status is read twice: npm's, echoed as `EXITSTATUS`, and `expect`'s `wait`.
