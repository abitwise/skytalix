is now closed as a **dimension**, measured, and the remaining risks are low or not this ticket's.

## Why the exit is forward

`A11` is the criterion that caused the first backward move in this project's history, and it is met:

- **Every own piece type on both home squares is enumerated**, `QUEEN`/`ROOK`/`BISHOP`/`KNIGHT`/`PAWN`
  on the king's home square with the matching rook present, `QUEEN`/`BISHOP`/`KNIGHT`/`PAWN` on the
  rook's with the king home, each in at least two colour/side configurations, all four configurations
  per table — `A11`'s own both-colours-both-sides clause, asserted rather than asserted-in-prose.
- **The quantifier itself is a test.** The piece-type universe is derived by probing `parseFen`, so
  deleting a row or adding a seventh parseable type turns it red. This is the part that makes the
  criterion closed as a *rule* rather than as a fourth sample, and it is the direct repair for the
  error this hop exists to fix.
- **Proved on 20 mutants of the delivered diff, one process per tree:** all nine own-type admissions
  die — including the **three that passed all 33 acceptance rows, all 19 previous hardening rows, all
  356 tests and the deep lane** — two colour/side-conditional admissions die to exactly the
  second-configuration rows that exist for them, and **the nine behaviour-equivalent mutants all still
  survive at 34/34**, killed count zero.
- **The harm reproduces on every kill.** On the king's square one `legalMoves()` call destroys the
  occupant and writes a king over it for **every** own type; on the rook's square the position is
  byte-identical, so only the `FLAG_CASTLE` count can see it.
- **Zero production lines.** `src/moves.ts` is byte-identical to the Engineer's first commit
  (`0e247853ab2c05887c76105ff83d8621`, `git diff 8f540cf` empty), and **all fourteen `main` test files
  are unmodified**, each diffed individually. Nothing was repaired to make this verdict come out green.

than a criterion. Rejected because "it is only a status annotation" is precisely how a role talks itself
into marking its own work accepted, and because the seam that found both gaps on this ticket in the
first place is the one where roles route instead of editing each other's sections.
