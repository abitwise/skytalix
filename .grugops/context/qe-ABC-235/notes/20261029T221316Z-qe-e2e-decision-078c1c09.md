(owner rule `owner-word-2026-10-24` `…4c3f7639`): each written result is final, with no repeat on a miss.

  `package*.json` are unchanged since it (`git diff a37f1d87 main` empty).

## Preconditions (every reading, and before every pair)
- `pgrep -x cutechess-cli` is empty, there is no `node` process with `vitest` in its arguments, and load1 ≤ 8.
- If they do not hold: poll every 15 s for up to 60 min (a pause, not a repeat). If they never clear, the reading is
  UNRESOLVED.
- `pgrep -x cutechess-cli` is also checked right before every single timed run, and load1 is recorded per run.

## G2 (A2): which read binds
- **Binding: the instrument as it stands**, as the Architect recommends (`arch-ABC-235/…eebca902`). It is ABC-083 A5,
  ABC-076's method, as `qe-ABC-215` ran it: N = 1 000 000, best of 5 × N per root after an N/10 warm-up, the six perft
  roots, mean of the six. It calls `evaluate` on one position again and again, so the pawn memo is warm. That is what a
  search sees.
- Order: base, tip, tip, base.
- **G2 MET** when each tip mean is ≤ 1 100 ns, and the mean of the two tip runs is ≤ the mean of the two base runs
  (A2's "≤ base's in the same session"). The near-bar rule (`ADR-0031`) applies: a tip mean in 990–1 210 ns triggers a
  second session in reverse order (tip, base, base, tip), as part of this run.
- **INFO, not graded: the cache-cold mean (the miss path).** Same six roots, 20 000 calls per root after 2 000 warm-up
  calls. Before every call, and outside the timed span, the memo is emptied: on tip with `clearPawnCacheForTest()`. Base
  has no memo, so it fills two `Float64Array(2^14)` with -1 instead: the same work and the same pollution of the CPU's
  caches. Each call is timed alone, so the timer's own cost is in both trees' means alike. Order: base, tip, tip, base.

## G6 (A4): the EPIC-008 rule as written
- 5 rounds. Each round is a `kiwipete` pair, then a `position-6` pair. Odd rounds run base first, even rounds tip first.
- Each run is one fresh process: `chooseMove(pos, {budgetMs: 3000})` on the real clock. Completed depth is
  `choice.depth`.
- **FAIL** if any tip run is below depth 6.
- **"≥ base"**: tip's median completed depth ≥ base's, per board, on both boards. This is graded (owner `…2ae5fc12`).
- Depth 7 WARN is reported. Grading is over all runs.

## A3 (speed)
- Its own reading, after G6. 5 rounds. Each round is a `kiwipete` pair, a `position-6` pair and a `start` pair; odd
  rounds base first, even rounds tip first. The run is the same as G6's.
- nps = `nodes / elapsedMs × 1000` per run (the ABC-227 method the SE used). Wall time is reported beside it.
- **MET** when tip's median nps ÷ base's median nps is ≥ 1.10 on at least two boards, and ≥ 1.00 on the third.
- **The per-cut drop rule, INFO.** After A3, three more readings with the same schedule: cut 1 against base, cut 3
  against cut 1, and tip (cut 5) against cut 3.
  - A cut whose median ratio is below 1.03 on **every** board is a named gap. The Orchestrator rules on the drop; QE
    drops nothing.
  - Ratios below 1.03 on some boards only are reported.
  - The SE read "≥ 3 % on all three boards". QE reports that reading too.
- **G5** is reported from these searches: the wall time of every 3 s search (the 5 000 ms ceiling, and 3 s at level 5).
  It is also graded by the existing instruments: the `ENGINE_DEEP` engine and levels lanes, green.

## The unpruned doors (depth 2): the ruling's terms, fixed now
- `searchWithoutPruning` and `searchWithoutPruningOrQuiescence` ran at depth 2 in the SE's A1. They are brute-force
  trees: about 3.3 s per door at depth 5 on `position-3`, and out of reach on the midgame roots.
- **A depth-5 sample is run, one each on base and tip:** both doors at depth 5 on the corpus rows whose family is one of
  the six endgames or `position-3`. That is 92 + 40 = 132 positions, 264 rows.
  - The harness is `a1.mjs` plus one filter line (`a1-sample.mjs`).
  - The families are chosen because they are cheap enough for depth 5, and because they exercise the cuts' riskiest
    paths:
    - king moves, on every move of a king endgame (cut 1's `move.to` branch);
    - en passant and the rank-pinned en passant of `position-3` (cuts 1 and 3);
    - promotions and capture promotions in the pawn endgames (cut 3);
    - pawn structures that change on every pawn move (cut 5's memo).
- **The depth-2 reading is ACCEPTABLE if all of these hold:**
  - (a) `git diff a37f1d87 6327a3b4 -- src/search.ts src/engine.ts src/quiescence*.ts` is empty, so the doors' own code
    is unchanged;
  - (b) A1's eight other search functions are node-equal at depth 5 on all 301 positions;
  - (c) the depth-5 sample is byte-equal;
  - (d) the depth-2 rows are equal.
- A mismatch in (c) is a defect: STOP. If (c) cannot run, the gap is named, and the ruling becomes "acceptable with a
  gap".
