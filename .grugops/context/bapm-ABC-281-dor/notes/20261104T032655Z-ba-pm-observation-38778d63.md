# bapm-ABC-281-dor observation: BA/PM's "the seed is used is single-held by levels A9" was wrong; measured, it has 15 other default-lane killers, and the single-held property is the one draw per move (QE-087-d)

BA/PM, task `bapm-ABC-281-dor`, records only. This note reads Architect/Design's measured kill matrix (`arch-ABC-281` observation `…e969c2f0`, `raw/kill-matrix.tsv`). Nothing was re-run.

- **What BA/PM wrote** (`bapm-nfr008-rows` observation `…d012231e`, and the `ABC-281` page § The Architect's confirmation): R3 is the default lane's only row asserting that the seed is used, and the only one asserting `QE-087-d`. That was reasoned from `grep`, with no mutant run.
- **What was measured:**
  - **M3a** (the seed ignored, `seededRandom(0)`) has 16 default-lane killers at base: R3, levels A10, `ABC-242` A6, and 13 `src/uci-session.test.ts` rows. So 15 rows besides R3 hold "the seed is used". **The first half of the claim is wrong.**
  - **M3b** (an extra draw before the decision) is killed by R3 and by `ABC-242` A6.
  - **M3c** (an extra draw after the decision on a blunder, `ABC-087`'s `M5b`) and **M3d** (the same on an engine move) are each killed by R3 alone. **The `QE-087-d` half of the claim stands**, and it is narrower: it is the after-decision extra draw that is single-held.
- **Consequence:**
  - R3's remnant keeps `QE-087-d` on seed 1 over the first 10 of the thirty. The first desync falls at move ≤ 4 on every seed.
  - A6's R3 mutant is the M3c shape. M3a is dropped from A6, because it tests nothing about R3.
  - The correction is written dated on the page. The original sentence is kept, with the correction beneath it.
- **Lesson for later cuts:** a "default lane's only holder" claim from `grep` is a hypothesis. Mark it `UNKNOWN - verify` until a mutant run reads it.
