## The rule the values come from (d2 and d3, stated, not read from code)
- **Bishop pair:** the side has two or more bishops. It counts once. Square colours are not read.
- **Open file:** a file holds at least one of the side's rooks and no pawn of either colour. It counts once per file.
- **Semi-open file:** a file holds at least one of the side's rooks, none of the side's pawns, and at least one enemy pawn. It counts once per file.
- A pawn "on a file" stands on ranks 2–7. **"A file holds" is the whole file**, not the squares ahead of the rook (RW1 and RW2 pin this). Queens are not read. Attacks, pins and side to move are not read.
- **d3's table:** `BISHOP_PAIR` 50 / 50, `ROOK_OPEN_FILE` 20 / 20, `ROOK_SEMI_OPEN_FILE` 10 / 10 (mg / eg). Every part below has mg = eg.

**No definition read two ways.** Each fixture has exactly one hand value under d2's words, so nothing was sent to Architect/Design.
