## Item 2 is the one that could not be satisfied as worded, and that is the finding

**Measured (see `2fdaa736` §1): `A11` passes M3 and M5 both BEFORE the strike and AFTER it, and nothing
writable on `ABC-016` can catch them.** Both move `history` — a term `ADR-0009` cl. 2 forbids this panel
naming and `ABC-016` cannot read, because it merges **first**. The shadow form that could name it passes
all six (Alternative B, rejected by measurement).

**So `A11` was not made able to fail on M3/M5. It was made HONEST about what it covers, which is the
actual repair:**

- `A11` keeps **exactly `STATUS_ROWS` rows**, and gains two clauses it never had: **`STATUS_ROWS` is
  `3`**, and **`STATUS_ROWS` is read from the module's own export**, never a local shadow. The second
  is not hypothetical — **merged `src/frame.test.ts:38` does exactly that today** and the shadow form
  passes every mutant.
- **`D8`'s claim is retracted in half on the ticket, in writing.** `A11` protects `4b` against **this
  panel's own** row count (M1, M6 — at `status` = 5, `H` = 25, `4b` dies, and `A11` fails there) and
  against nothing else. The `history`/`board` halves are `A13`'s.
- **Rationale for retracting rather than stretching:** a criterion that keeps claiming a protection it
  cannot deliver is worse than one that claims less, because a reader stops looking. That is the same
  defect class this board has now recorded repeatedly — *a bar that cannot fail on the regression it
  exists to catch.* Pretending `A11` covers `4b` would have reproduced it while appearing to fix it.

## `ABC-017`'s new `A13`, and why each clause is worded as it is

In `src/frame.test.ts`, all three terms read from **delivered** geometry — `renderBoard(...).length`,
`ABC-016`'s exported `STATUS_ROWS`, this pane's exported `HISTORY_ROWS` — with **`= 23` as a tripwire**
and **`<= 24` as the requirement**, and `src/render.ts` neither modified nor given a row-count export.

1. **`= 23` is the only form that fails every mutant moving `H`.** No panel's own clause is: `A1` is
   blind to M1/M4/M6, `A11` to M2–M5.
2. **M4 (`renderBoard` ships 10) is caught by `renderBoard(...).length` and by nothing else** — which
   is why cl. 6 derives the board term instead of exporting one.
3. **`<= 24` is not a weaker `= 23`.** M3/M5/M6 are where `4b` dies and `<= 24` is what says so; `= 23`
   also fails M1/M2/M4 where `4b` is fine, which is the tripwire doing its job. Keeping both lets a
   future row-count change be **ratified by moving one number**.
4. **M7 (status 4 + history 7, `H` still 23) is why both clauses read delivered geometry and not
   `HEIGHT`.** `A13` correctly passes while both panels' own clauses correctly fail. A single criterion
   doing both jobs would have to fail on a frame with nothing wrong with it.
