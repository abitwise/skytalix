# QE reported TWO deep-lane-only survivors; I re-derived them and measured SEVEN — the whole `empty` corridor is guarded on WHITE and unguarded on BLACK, and the RIGHT test is pinned only at `- 0 1`

QE's two survivors were reported honestly and are correct. They were also **one instrument on one
square each**, so this hop treated them as unasserted and mutated the whole neighbourhood instead:
every square of every `CastleOption.empty` array, and all three scalar fields against the right test.
**Nothing below is inherited from QE, from the engineer, or from `ABC-023`'s text.**

## The instrument

Mutants applied to a copy of `src/` held **outside** the repository with `node_modules` symlinked.
Each anchor asserted to occur **exactly once** in the pristine source and the bytes asserted to have
changed, or the run is a harness error and never a survivor. `tsc --noEmit` per mutant, so a tree that
does not build can never be scored a survivor. **One fresh OS process per tree state** for every
typecheck, every `vitest` run and every deep-perft probe. `src/moves.ts` restored from a pristine
snapshot and **re-hashed to `6335b452fc21ffe2d0f902ecab930840` after every single mutant**, and the
repository's own `src/` was **never written** — verified after the run.

**14 mutants, 0 BROKEN-BUILD, 0 HARNESS-ERROR**, plus 7 deep-lane re-runs and 26 witness positions.
Baseline measured in the harness's own processes first: `npm test` **625 passed / 625 on 18 files** in
**1.23 s**, `tsc --noEmit` exit **0**. Machine: Apple M3 (Mac15,13) / Node v24.12.0 / Darwin arm64 —
the test counts are integers and machine-independent; the wall-clock seconds are not.

positions that reach white castling a ply earlier than black's, owned by no criterion. **1 of the 10
corridor squares is named by a row, 5 are covered by accident, 4 by nothing at all in the default
