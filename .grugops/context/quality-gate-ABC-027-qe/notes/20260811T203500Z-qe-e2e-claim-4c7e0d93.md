# THE ONE GAP I FOUND: `ABC-027` makes the screen model's RIGHT-MARGIN behaviour load-bearing for the first time, and nothing has ever measured it

> **Hand-authored** — `scripts/context-io.ts` absent from this repository and from the kit,
> `mcp__grugops__propose_note` not in this session's tools. **This is a `claim`, not a `finding`**,
> and it carries `confidence: UNKNOWN - verify` deliberately: the half of it that decides severity is
> a statement about REAL TERMINALS, and this project has no runnable terminal surface until
> `EPIC-006`. Workflow 16's honest-`claim` escape hatch is exactly this situation. **I am not
> asserting a production defect. I am asserting that a NEW dependency on an UNMEASURED assumption was
> created, and that the suite structurally cannot see it.**

## The mechanism, stated so it can be checked rather than believed

`src/frame.test.ts:178-191` states the screen model's four modelled behaviours as "assumptions stated
rather than buried". One of them is the deferred wrap:

> *"the wrap at the right margin is DEFERRED, as xterm and Terminal.app do it: a row of exactly
> `columnCount` characters leaves the cursor AT the margin and does not move down until the next
> printable character. Modelling it eagerly would put a phantom blank row into every frame"*

That justification is about **printable characters**, and it is right. The model implements it by
letting `column` run to `columnCount` — one PAST the last cell (`:250-256`).

**Before `ABC-027` that representation could not affect any assertion.** The only escape emitted was
`\x1b[0J`, once, at the very end, where the cursor sits at column 0 of the reserved prompt row. It
never fired at the margin.

**`ABC-027` makes `\x1b[K` fire at the end of EVERY row.** So for any row whose content reaches the
last column, the erase now fires exactly at the margin — and the model's `eraseToEndOfLine`
(`:212-215`) loops `for (index = column; index < columnCount)`, which at `column === columnCount` is a
**no-op**.

A VT/xterm does not represent the margin that way. It keeps the cursor at `columnCount − 1` with the
last-column / wrap-pending flag set, and `EL(0)` erases *from the cursor* — i.e. **that last cell**.

**The suite's model and a real terminal therefore disagree about `\x1b[K` at the margin, and
`ABC-027` is what made that disagreement reachable.**

## Measured, out of tree, against the DELIVERED writer

Two screen models, identical except at the margin. `merged` is the pre-`ABC-027` writer.

| screen width | model | writer | rows differing from the frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| exactly `W` = 18 | deferred (**the suite's**) | merged | 0 |
| exactly `W` = 18 | deferred (**the suite's**) | **delivered** | **0** |
| exactly `W` = 18 | pending-wrap (VT/xterm) | merged | **0** |
| exactly `W` = 18 | pending-wrap (VT/xterm) | **delivered** | **18** (9 rows × 2 redraws) |
| exactly `W` = 37 | pending-wrap (VT/xterm) | **delivered** | **2** (1 row × 2 redraws) |

The 9 rows at `W` = 18 are **the entire h-file of the chessboard plus its file label**:

```
want: "8 .♜:♞.♝:♛.♚:♝.♞:♜"     got: "8 .♜:♞.♝:♛.♚:♝.♞:"
want: "   a b c d e f g h"     got: "   a b c d e f g "
```

At `W` = 37 it is the status row that sets `W`: `White may claim: threefold repetition` →
`White may claim: threefold repetitio`.

**Three things make this worth a note rather than a shrug:**

1. **The merged writer scores 0 under BOTH models.** If the pending-wrap reading is right, this is a
   REGRESSION INTRODUCED BY THIS TICKET into merged production code, not a pre-existing condition.
2. **It is not confined to narrowing frames.** It hits every frame whose widest row fills the
   terminal, steady or not, including the first. The defect being fixed affects 5–16 of every 20
   redraws; this would affect every one.
3. **The suite asserts hardest exactly where the model is least trustworthy.** `src/frame.test.ts`
   `:379`, `:408` and `:426` all construct `createScreen(24, width)` / `createScreen(HEIGHT, width)`
   with `width = widthOf(frame)` — the margin, every time — and `:394`/`:417` then assert UNTRIMMED
   row equality there. `A6` (`:774`) does the same at `widthOf(wide)`. Those four sites are the
   suite's strongest claims and they all stand on the one model behaviour nothing has measured.

## What I am NOT claiming

- **I am not claiming a real terminal does this.** I could not run one: `AGENTS.md` records
  `Dev / run` and `E2E` as `UNKNOWN - verify`, there is no CLI entry point until `EPIC-006`, and
  fabricating a terminal measurement is exactly the thing this project's rules forbid. The
  pending-wrap model above is my reading of ECMA-48 `EL` plus xterm's last-column flag; **it needs a
  human or `EPIC-006` to confirm or refute.**
- **I am not proposing a constant-frame-width criterion.** `ADR-0010` clause 5 refuses it and it
  would not address this: the trigger is `terminal columns == W`, not `W` varying.
- **I am not proposing that the writer read the terminal's width.** `src/frame.ts:25` forbids it and
  `EPIC-002`'s SIGWINCH ruling stands. **This does not need a production change to be closed** — it
  needs the assumption named and either measured or accepted.
- **I did not touch anything.** No test was weakened, deleted or added. This is a gate report.

## Also measured, and it is GOOD news — a terminal NARROWER than the frame is not made worse

The obvious follow-on worry is that a per-row erase mid-wrap makes the too-narrow-terminal case worse.
It does not. Over the four-frame narrowing sequence, rows differing from the frame:

| columns | merged | delivered |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 26 | **24** |
| 24 | 26 | **24** |
| 20 | 27 | **24** |

**The delivered writer is equal or better at every width tested.** `EPIC-002` accepts this class as
cosmetic and out of scope; recorded so the acceptance is not later re-argued from a guess.

## Class and routing

**This is a CRITERION GAP, not a code defect, and it routes to BA/PM — not to the Engineer.** The
Engineer built exactly what six criteria asked for and every one of them is green. No criterion
mentions the margin, so no build could have covered it. Concretely, what is missing is a criterion of
roughly this shape, which I state as a target and deliberately do not write:

> *at a modelled screen width EQUAL to the frame's `W`, a row whose content reaches the last column
> survives the redraw intact* — with the screen model's margin behaviour stated as the assumption it
> is, and either measured against a real terminal at `EPIC-006` or accepted in writing.

**Non-blocking for `ABC-027`.** Nothing composes a frame in a loop until `ABC-019`, so no player can
reach this today, exactly as the ticket says of the defect it fixes. The cheapest honest close is one
sentence in the screen model's assumption block naming the margin as unmeasured, plus a
`EPIC-006` revisit trigger. **Owner: BA/PM** for the criterion; **Architect/Design** if the model's
fidelity is judged an ADR-level question.
