EPIC-001 decomposed into seven tickets. The cut follows the natural seams of a chess rules core, so each ticket is independently testable rather than being a slice of one big lump.

| Ticket | Title | Size | Pri | Depends on |
|--------|-------|------|-----|------------|
| ABC-001 | Position state and FEN round-trip | M | P0 | — |
| ABC-002 | Pseudo-legal move generation | M | P0 | 001 |
| ABC-003 | Apply and undo a move | M | P0 | 001, 002 |
| ABC-004 | Special moves (castle/EP/promotion) | L | P0 | 002, 003 |
| ABC-005 | Legal move filtering, attack detection | M | P0 | 002, 003, 004 |
| ABC-006 | Perft verification harness | S | P0 | 004, 005 |
| ABC-007 | Game-end detection | M | P0 | 003, 005 |

**Two cuts worth justifying.**

*Pseudo-legal generation (ABC-002) is split from legality filtering (ABC-005).* This is the standard engine seam and it makes both halves testable alone: ABC-002 is verifiable with exact counts on isolated pieces, and ABC-005 is verifiable with pins and check evasions. Merging them would produce one untestable lump whose only real test is perft — which is exactly the debugging position ABC-006 exists to avoid.

*Special moves (ABC-004) are one ticket, not three.* Castling, en passant, and promotion each extend both the generator and the make/unmake mechanism in the same two places. Splitting them would mean three tickets each touching the same two files with the same shape of change, which is a false independence. Sized `L` to reflect that it is one ticket carrying three sets of edge cases.

**Everything is P0, and that is not laziness.** This is a foundation epic where every ticket is load-bearing: there is no ordering information in priority to be had, because nothing here is optional. The real execution order is carried by the dependency chain above, not by priority. `ABC-001 → 002 → 003 → 004 → 005 → {006, 007}`, with 006 and 007 parallelizable at the end.

**No ticket is XL**, so the Orchestrator's no-XL-into-dev rule is satisfied and no `SPLIT_REQUIRED` is emitted.
