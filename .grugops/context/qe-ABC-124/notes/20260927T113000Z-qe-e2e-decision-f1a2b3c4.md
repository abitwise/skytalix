The ticket's `A4` literally requires the summary line to read `Fixed 0 files`. Biome 2.5.5 (checked
`npx biome --version`) prints `No fixes applied` instead, confirmed independently here exactly as
the Engineer's own observation `…18bca91a` measured. **Ruling: A4 is MET in substance, not in the
ticket's literal wording.** The acceptance criterion's underlying guarantee — no file under
`tools/` is rewritten by the coverage change — is independently confirmed two ways: `git diff
--numstat` is byte-identical before and after `npx biome check --write tools/`, and `npx biome
format tools/` reports the formatter is off for that glob (`Checked 0 files … provided but
ignored`). The ticket's exact string is a wording gap against this Biome version, not a delivery
defect; not a blocker, named as a finding for the ticket text to be corrected at the next licensed
touch.
