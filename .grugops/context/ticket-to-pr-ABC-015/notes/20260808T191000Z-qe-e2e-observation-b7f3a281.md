note records the re-derivation separately from the testing, so the arithmetic can be checked without
reading the verdict.

any other and was re-derived here by machine**, splitting each row on pipes **not preceded by a
backslash**:

| Line | Row | Raw pipes | Escaped | Cells |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 36 | header | 11 | 0 | **10** |
| 38 | `ABC-001` | 11 | 0 | **10** |
| 45 | `ABC-008` | 11 | 0 | **10** |

role edited two of its cells, and it is still ten.
