## Why this is a PASS and not a FAIL, stated plainly because both were live

The campaign found three builds that pass every criterion, all 439 delivered tests and the deep
perft lane. **Not one of them is the delivered build.** On all 44 FEN-producible configurations of
the two axes the preconditions read, the shipped generator is correct: it offers the en-passant move
exactly when the target is empty and the enemy pawn is genuinely behind it, and `legalMoves` is pure
on every one. There is no production line to change, so there is nothing to send back — a FAIL here
would return a correct module to `In Development` to have its *tests* widened, which is the work
this role owns.

**The gap is in the criteria's tables, and both criteria left room for it deliberately.** `A1` and
`A3` each say *"at minimum"*, so quantified rows are inside them as written. That is why no criterion
needed to move and none did: the closure is a new QE test file, on this board's own `ABC-009`
precedent (`src/moves.castling.hardening.test.ts`, cut by QE for exactly this reason).

## What the gate verified rather than accepted

Every figure in the `## Acceptance` set was re-derived on this role's own instruments — fourteen full
tree copies outside the repository, **one OS process per tree state**, `tsc --noEmit` exit 0 on every
one **before any red was believed**, every probe printing its FEN and re-parsing it. Detail is in the
`observation` notes published alongside this one. The three standing traps were all live and all
paid off: one mutant of mine failed to compile and would have read as a spectacular red
(`capturedPawn` undefined), and my first `A7` walk counted the wrong population.

## The two rulings I was invited to challenge

1. **The two sites beyond `A11`'s citation table — NOT challenged, and the reason is measured.**
   `src/moves.enpassant.test.ts` 82-83 and 95-97 said *"Today every row offers the capture"* and
   *"creates a phantom WHITE pawn"* / *"whose corruption is healed later in the same pass"*. All
   three are false on the delivered tree by my own measurement, and all three are now absent.
   `A11`'s binding clause is about statements this fix falsified, not about a list of line numbers,
   and BA/PM wrote the table as a floor in the same breath. Reverting them would ship the exact
   sentence the site-7 ruling refused to ship.
2. **The castling pair stays out.** `src/moves.castling.test.ts` 7-10 and 98-99 were not folded in,
   not edited and not counted against this ticket. Already routed to `ABC-023`.
3. **`A5`'s settled equivalence was not re-litigated.** What was tested is the thing the brief named:
   whether `A5` still kills what it is now claimed to kill. **It does, and it is the only thing that
   does** — see the mutation-campaign note.

## The one place this hop could be wrong in a way that matters

hardens" and "BA/PM widens the criterion" sits. I placed it on the `ABC-009` precedent and on `A1`/
`A3`'s own *"at minimum"*. A reviewer who reads those tables as closed enumerations would want BA/PM
to widen them first; the remedy in that case is to hold the file, not to revert the verdict, because
the code is correct either way.
