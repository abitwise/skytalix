# 40 mutants: 31 killed, 9 survived — FIVE of them genuinely wrong builds that pass all 22 criteria AND all 652 tests, and every one is the same defect class

Every mutant was a real edit to `src/san.ts`, restored from a byte copy before the next. Each was
scored three ways: against `src/san.test.ts` + `src/san.nfr.test.ts`, against my independent oracle
(is it actually WRONG, or merely different?), and — for every survivor — against the whole 652-test
suite and against `chess.js`.

**`src/san.ts` md5 `cbcc3be37c21414d16e8e6b4d0c68959` before the first mutation and after the last.**

## The five that matter

They pass **all 20 in-suite criteria, `A21`'s call counts, and all 652 tests**, and all five emit
strings an external implementation calls wrong.

| id | mutation | example wrong string | mismatches vs `chess.js` |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `Q33` | full origin square whenever a **ROOK** has a rival | `Ra1b1` for `Rab1` | 2,444 |
| `Q34` | full origin square whenever a **BISHOP** has a rival | `Bc4d5` for `B4d5` | 100 |
| `Q35` | full origin square on a qualified **CAPTURE** | `Nb6xd5` for `Nbxd5` | 416 |
| `Q27` | full origin square whenever the mover is **BLACK** | `Nb6xd5` for `Nbxd5` | 2,288 |
| `Q38` | full origin square for **BLACK on the rank rung only** | `Nc3e4` for `N3e4` | 60 |

**One sentence names the whole hole: minimality is asserted ONLY by the acceptance table, and the
table exercises minimality on WHITE KNIGHTS and WHITE QUEENS alone.**

- `A4` (file rung) and `A5` (rank rung) are knights. `A6` (all three rungs) is queens. **No criterion
  disambiguates a rook, a bishop or a pawn-as-piece.**
- **All 18 acceptance FENs are white to move.** There is not one black-to-move row in the ticket.
- **No criterion pins a qualified capture** — every disambiguated row in the table is a quiet move,
  and every capture row (`A3`, `A9`, `A10`, `A11`, `A12`) has a unique mover.

The two table-free properties structurally cannot cover it, and this is the important part rather
than a criticism of them: an over-qualified string is **still distinct** (injectivity holds) and
**still of the SAN grammar** (`Ra1b1` matches the shape regex). The properties catch
UNDER-qualification everywhere; OVER-qualification is caught in two piece types and one colour.

**This is a gap in the CRITERIA. It is not a defect in the code** — the delivered build is minimal
everywhere, checked against two oracles over 649,556 and 323,694 strings respectively.

## Four more mutants pass every acceptance criterion and die ONLY on the injectivity property

| id | mutation | killed by |
|---|---|---|
| `Q13` | castle side from the **absolute** destination square, not its file — wrong for **BLACK** | injectivity only |
| `Q14` | the disambiguator is **skipped on a capture** (`Nxd5` for `Nbxd5`) | injectivity only |
| `Q28` | the `x` is **dropped for black** | injectivity only |
| `Q36` | rooks are **under-qualified** (control for the asymmetry above) | injectivity only |

Every one of `A1`–`A21` is green on all four. `Q13` is the natural shape of a real bug — a `6` that
should have been a file rather than a square — and nothing in the ticket's 22 criteria can see it,
because black never castles anywhere in the table.

**The Engineer's injectivity property is therefore far more load-bearing than the note introducing
it claimed.** It was reported as *"green when written"* and as independently killing two mutants the
table also killed. It is in fact the sole guard against four wrong builds. It should not be removed,
narrowed, or have its corpus shrunk.

## The nine survivors, split honestly

| survived | oracle verdict | reading |
|---|---|---|
| `Q33` `Q34` `Q35` `Q27` `Q38` | **DIVERGENT** | five real holes in the criteria |
| `Q22` rivals matched on piece TYPE not the whole byte | EQUIVALENT | control — the colour half of the byte test is free on a one-side legal list, exactly as the Engineer wrote |
| `Q24` capture from the board PLUS the en-passant flag | EQUIVALENT | control — the other correct way to read a capture |
| `Q29` castle detected by the king's two-square hop | EQUIVALENT | control — equivalent in standard chess |
| `Q40` full square for a KING with a rival | EQUIVALENT | control — a king never has a same-type rival, so the branch is unreachable |

All four controls were confirmed equivalent by the external oracle as well (0 mismatches over
649,556 strings), which is what makes them controls rather than unexamined survivors.

## The 31 kills, and which layer caught them

The table did most of the work and did it well: `A4` alone kills rank-before-file, `A5`/`A6` kill
the rank/full-square rungs, `A17` kills `#` on a stalemate, `A10` kills the board-read capture,
`A16` kills the early castle return, `A12`/`A15` kill four promotion mutations, `A13`/`A14` kill the
suffix mutations, `A2`/`A8` kill cross-type disambiguation. Three mutations of the mate test's
short-circuit — including a build that runs `legalMoves` on every move — are string-identical to
the delivered build and die **only** in `src/san.nfr.test.ts`, which reproduces the Engineer's own
`M8`/`M9` finding from an independent direction and adds a third of the same shape.

## What this measurement does NOT establish

**That 40 is enough.** `ABC-023`'s QE hop found fourteen survivors; this one found five, and both
numbers are floors on their own mutant sets. Mine were chosen after reading the grammar and the
criteria rather than the code's tests, which is a different blind spot from the Engineer's, not the
absence of one. In particular I wrote **no mutant at all** for the `promotion` byte's encoding, for
`squareName`'s output, or for anything below `toSan` — those live in modules this ticket does not
touch and `perft` already covers.
