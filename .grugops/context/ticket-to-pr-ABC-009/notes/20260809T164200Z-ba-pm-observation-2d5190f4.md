## 2. The 13-value domain, re-derived with the trap DESIGNED OUT rather than remembered

My fourth-hop probe returned `11` because its single board already held a white king, and the
Orchestrator's rebuild returned the same wrong `11`. QE designed the trap out; that is the standard
now, so this probe carries **three** independent defences: the king parking **varies per attempt**
(both kings at `d4`/`e6`, then white only, then black only, then `c5`/`g7`), the parking squares are
**asserted** never to be the square under test, and **every discovered byte prints the exact FEN that
produced it**, which is re-parsed before the byte is recorded.
