  a second". Cut 6 of `ABC-227`'s six.
- **Scope:** item 1 is `orderMoves` without per-move wrappers. Item 2 is quiescence's capture sort with each key
  computed once. Both hold under the Architect's C1–C4: stable ties, float64 keys, input never mutated, no output
  shared between plies. One commit per item. `orderKey` and its tiers are out (`ABC-233`/`ABC-234`).
- **Acceptance:** A1 is cross-build identity at depth 5 (the `ABC-078` `A3` corpus and `ABC-121`'s 190; every
  `ADR-0026` door; `chooseMove` at budgets 3 and 5 on `tickingClock(1)`). A2 is the Architect's P1/P2 oracle, plus
  one row per condition. A3: median nps ≥ 1.03 × base on two boards and ≥ 1.00 × on the third (5 interleaved runs
  per board per build at 3 000 ms). A4: `G1`, `G4`–`G9`. A5: S2, no smoke.
- **If A3 is missed** (A1 and A2 green): NOT MET. The branch is not merged. QE files a `failed-attempt` note, and
  BA/PM closes the ticket as dropped, with no re-tune round. Nothing waits on it: `ABC-233`/`ABC-234` build on
  today's `orderMoves`. The ticket is re-opened only with a different form or a fresh profile. A red A1 or A2 is a
  defect, and the ticket goes back to the SE.
- **Size S, priority P3, DoR 10/10, column Ready.**
- **Pull order:** after `ABC-235` merges. That gives a quiet host for nps and a base that holds 235; there is no
  shared file. It goes before `ABC-233`/`ABC-234` (shared `orderMoves`). Item 2 shares `src/quiescence.ts` with
  `ABC-228`/`ABC-229`: whichever merges second rebases, and A1/A2 are read against the new base.
- **Why P3 and not dropped:** it is value-identical and costs 0 games, and it removes the 8.6–12.8 % of bytes that
  `orderMoves` allocates (`ABC-227`). The miss rule caps its cost at one SE hop and one QE reading.
