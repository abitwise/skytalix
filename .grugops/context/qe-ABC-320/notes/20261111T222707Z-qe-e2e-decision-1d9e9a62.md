  3. Gate 3 is **W's check**, one `hostcheck.sh` line. Any foreign `vitest`, `cutechess-cli` or `stockfish` is a **WAIT, not a read** (exit 4).

     - The chunk's build clone is deleted (about 800 MB), clean or not.

  5. The night ends when the guard refuses the next chunk, or when openings 1–100 are done. It writes `next-start` (the first opening no clean chunk covers), then `rsum` over the clean chunks (`rsum.txt`), then `read.mjs 5 E 4000 <clean chunks>` (`read.txt`).
- **The 8 h guard figure and its source.** A chunk's worst case is **2 000 plies at 4.05 s a ply, plus 300 s: 8 400 s (2 h 20 min)**.
  - **2 000 plies** is 10 games at a mean of 200 plies. The worst 10-game window (5 consecutive rounds, sliding) in the 1 000 graded games of ABC-316 and ABC-317 (L1, L2 twice, L3, L4) was **1 969 plies**, at L3. The worst aligned window was 1 658. 200 plies a game is also ABC-317's RUNAWAY basis.
  - **4.05 s a ply** is `st` + skytalix's 50 ms margin. A ply past it would be a skytalix time loss.
  - **300 s** covers the build and engine starts. The smoke built in about 4 s.
  - The smoke ran 3.907 s a ply, under the 4.05 s bound.
  - This is a worst case over the games measured, not a bound: `-maxmoves 200` allows 400 plies a game. The RUNAWAY stop is the bound.
- **RUNAWAY is hard: 28 800 s (8 h) since the session started.** At that moment the sampler stops the running chunk. **THERMAL** stops it when the median of the last 30 Tctl samples is above 85 °C.
  - Either stop makes that chunk **VOID**. It is not pooled; its first opening is the next start, and the session stops (exit 5).
  - Clean chunks before it stand and are pooled.
  - Each stop signals only the tool and its own descendants. A foreign process is never signalled.
  - A VOID chunk's openings are played afresh on the next night by this rule; that replay is pre-registered here, not a re-run. A RUNAWAY void selects on long games (risk named below).
- **A chunk not clean** (tool exit, games, faults, state, depth guard or record check) stops the session (exit 6). Its first opening is written as the next start. The clean chunks before it are pooled for the report, and the night's interim reading is **not valid**. What happens next is asked of the owner.

## The interim reading and the band probe
- Reported from `rsum.txt`:
  - W / L / D, points P of n (n = 10 × clean chunks), score %;
  - `cutechess-cli` 1.5.1's formula, Δ ± Y against E, and **X = E + Δ ± Y**, labelled **interim, n games, not the level's figure**.
- Also reported from `read.txt`: the colour split, and the opening-cluster interval beside the iid ± (with only n / 2 clusters).
- **Band probe:** the score is "clearly outside 25–75 %" when the 95 % iid interval of the score lies wholly above 75 % or wholly below 25 %. Then QE reports it and recommends a step. **A step needs the owner's word.** It restarts L5 at E′ from opening 1, in a sealed addendum naming E′ and its `pickDepth`, and night 1's games are then not pooled with E′'s.
  - E′ = E + Δ, rounded to 10 and clamped to 1 320–3 190. At P = n or 0 the Orchestrator names E′.
  - Inside the band, or not clearly outside it: no step. Night 2 continues from `next-start` at E 2 280 (a new W).
- **The full reading** pools all nights' clean chunks with one `rsum` once openings 1–100 are covered. The band rule is then applied to the 200 games as in ABC-317's PREREG: one step per level, on the owner's word. The pooled 200 is the level's figure for the README; no interim is.
  - `rsum` reads the raw `r.out`, so every clean chunk's out-dir (without its clone) stays in `<scratch>` until the 200 are pooled. Committed records are redacted copies.

## The UNRESOLVED and re-run rules
- **VOID** (RUNAWAY or THERMAL): that chunk has no games in any reading. Its openings are the next start (above). Any other replay is asked of the owner first.
- **A chunk not clean** (UNRESOLVED, a Stockfish or harness fault, a missing game or file, the depth guard, a record miss): the session stops, and no interim figure is given. A re-run is asked of the owner first.
- **A skytalix fault or time loss:** no interim figure. It is also a product finding on `cddd4df9`, named with its game, for the Orchestrator.
- **WAIT or a gate miss:** no game was played and nothing is read. A later launch needs a new W, on the Orchestrator's line.
- QE never re-runs on its own word, never re-plays a clean chunk, and never pools chunks across E or across subjects.

## Host gates (W and FL), as in ABC-317
- **W:** the night's first game starts only after the Orchestrator's brief line citing the owner's held host. W is asked for each night and never inherited. The session's own W check is gate 3, the class-and-count `ps` check at launch.
- **FL:** foreign load after the first game changes no line of the read. No game is voided, stopped, paused or replayed for it, and **no foreign process is touched**. It is logged and reported **by class and count only. No foreign command line, pid, user or path is written anywhere.**
  - If foreign `vitest` or `cutechess-cli` shows in more than 20 % of the night's samples, the reading is labelled SHARED. The label is reported and voids nothing.

## Risks
- RUNAWAY voids select on long games. A void needs a chunk launched late in the window that runs past 2 000 plies plus the time still left. The smoke's mean of 171.5 plies makes a 2 000-ply chunk less rare than ABC-316 and ABC-317 suggest. If a void fires, it is reported, with the replay on the next night.

- The interim's ± is wide: about ±100 at 50 games. It is a probe, not a figure.
