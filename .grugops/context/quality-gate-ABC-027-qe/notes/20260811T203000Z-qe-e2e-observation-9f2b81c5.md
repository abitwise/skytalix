## 2. THE ATTRIBUTION, which is the part worth carrying

**`A1` alone kills 9 of the 14. `A2` — "the criterion the ticket exists for" — kills 2.**

`A2` stayed GREEN on `M1`, `M2`, `M3`, `M4`, `M7`, `M14` and `M15`. That is not a defect and I am not
reporting it as one; it is a measured property of the criterion, and the reason is structural:

- **`A2` is TRIMMED (`F3`), so it can only see residue that is NOT a space.** In the four-frame
  fixture exactly one row per redraw has a non-blank tail — the status stub's third row, index 11.
  Every other row is `padEnd`-ed, so its residue is spaces and trimming erases the evidence. `M3`
  proves this precisely: a writer that clears **only row 11** and no other row passes `A2` at both 80
  and 120 columns.
- `A2` checks rows `0 … H − 2` only, so nothing it asserts can see row `H − 1` or below — which is
  why `M4` (no `\x1b[0J` at all) is invisible to it.

**This does not weaken the ticket.** `A1` is byte-exact and its four counts are derived from `H`, so
every emission-shape mutant dies there, and `A6` covers a *different* row index (9, from its own
`wide` fixture) than `A2` does (11) — the two together are broader than either. The honest statement
is: **`A2` is the criterion that proves the FIX is correct; `A1` is the criterion that kills mutants.**
The ticket's rhetoric attributes the mutation-killing to `A2`. The measurement attributes it to `A1`.
A future hop that "simplifies" `A1` because it looks like a restatement of the production line would
remove most of this suite's mutation resistance and `A2` would not notice.

## 3. `M5` — the sharpest single result in the campaign

`\x1b[0K` and `\x1b[K` are **the same sequence**: ECMA-48 defines an omitted parameter as `0`, so
`CSI K` ≡ `CSI 0 K`. A writer emitting `\x1b[0K` is byte-different and behaviourally identical on
every conformant terminal.

**It turns 8 tests red**, including `A2` and `A4`, and five of those fail by the screen model
*throwing* `screen model: unmodelled escape`. This is the concrete instance of judgement question 1
and it is argued in the `decision` note beside this one.

## 4. `M12` is the one that justifies `A3` existing

`A3` is the only thing in the suite that kills `M12` — a fixture whose narrowing still happens but
whose stale tail is spaces, so `A2` would go green forever while measuring nothing. `A3` fired alone.
The brief said this project has recorded "a bar that cannot fail on the regression it exists to
catch" six times; `A3` is the first criterion I have gated here that demonstrably prevents a seventh,
and it did it against a mutant nobody had constructed. **`A3` earns its place. Do not let a future
hop drop it as ceremony.**
