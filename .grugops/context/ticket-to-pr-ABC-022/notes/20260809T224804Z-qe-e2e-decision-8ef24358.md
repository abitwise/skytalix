# `src/moves.enpassant.hardening.test.ts` — what it quantifies, what it deliberately leaves alone, and why no criterion was touched

120 tests in a new file. **Every row passes on the delivered fix — no production line changed.** Each
row exists because a build that fails it passes all eleven criteria, all 439 delivered tests and the
deep perft lane at 32/32.

## Why a QE test file and not a criteria amendment

Three courses were available and two were rejected.

1. **Hand back to BA/PM to widen `A1` and `A3`.** Rejected: both already say **"at minimum"**, so
   quantified rows are inside them as written. Asking for an amendment that changes no requirement
   would spend a hop and leave the hole open across it.
2. **Report the gap and pass without closing it.** Rejected: this board's recorded failure shape is a
   green signal read as protection. A gap named in a note and left open in the suite is exactly that.
3. **Cut a hardening file, on the `ABC-009` precedent.** Taken. `src/moves.castling.hardening.test.ts`
   was cut by QE at `ABC-009` for the identical reason and states the identical contract in its own
   header: *"Every row here PASSES on the delivered fix — no production line changed."*

`## Acceptance` was not edited (section md5 `7123d5c641045a44c6f63c2c20dab415`, identical to `HEAD`),
and `src/moves.ts` was not touched.

## What it covers

- **Axis 1, the byte on the target** — all six of the mover's own piece types on **both colours to
  move**, plus two EMPTY controls that stop the table passing by rejecting everything. 14 rows, each
  scored on `generateMoves` and on `legalMoves`.
- **Axis 2, the byte one rank behind the target** — every FEN-producible piece code of both colours
  plus EMPTY, on both colours to move, with exactly two rows permitted to offer the move. 26 rows,
  each scored twice.
- **Purity over all 40 positions**, 128 board bytes and five scalars, field by field, **across the
  call AND per make/unmake pair**. The pair oracle is not decoration: four `t-notOwnPawn`
  configurations read CLEAN across the call and DIRTY per pair.

Result: the three survivors now fail **4 / 30 / 60** tests, **every one of them in the new file**.

## What it deliberately does NOT cover, and this was the hard call

**The ENEMY occupant on the target is `A5`'s axis and is left to `A5`.** Quantifying it here would
make the colour-shaped restructure fail in two files, and that would falsify the acceptance file's own
sentence — *"kept as the ONE guard on this board against a colour-shaped RESTRUCTURE … and fails only
here"*. That sentence is a present-tense claim about the generator's en-passant branch in a comment
under `src/`, which is the precise class `A11` exists to remove. **Creating one while reviewing the
ticket whose subject is false comments would be a poor trade**, and the cheap direction is to leave
the axis alone and report it.

Measured rather than assumed: **the restructure mutant passes all 40 hardening rows** (0 wrong,
0 impure), so the sentence stays true.

The consequence is named, not hidden: **`A5`'s enemy-occupant axis remains a sample of two piece
types.** A restructure that drops the ordinary-capture `continue` for only one piece type would
survive. That is the same shape as `ABC-009`'s `A11` and it belongs to BA/PM or to `ABC-023`, not to
a side effect of a QE file.

## Two smaller shape decisions

- **Rows are FENs, never direct board writes.** Every row is parsed and re-rendered inside the test
  (`toFen(parseFen(fen))` must equal the input), so a mistyped board cannot become a green assertion.
  The king rows relocate the kings because `parseFen` refuses two of a colour — that instrument limit
  is stated in the file header rather than worked around, and bytes that are not valid piece codes
  are out of reach entirely.
- **Duplicate FENs across the two tables are deliberate.** The `ABC-009` hardening file makes the
  same call and gives the same reason: a quantifier has to be exhaustive *where it is written*, not
  dependent on which rows another table happens to carry.
