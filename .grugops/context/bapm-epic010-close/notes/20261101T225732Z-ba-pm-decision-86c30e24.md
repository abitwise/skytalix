## Decision
- **S' pool (500 ms, E 1 650):** qe-ABC-255 A5 9.0/10 on becf571a + qe-EPIC-010-close S' 9.0/10 on 053733d2 =
  **18.0/20 = 90.0 %**. Under 30 games: no re-pin. **A next S' at >= 8.0/10 makes >= 26.0/30 = 86.7 % > 85 % and

- **The pool carries to EPIC-011 as it stands and does not restart** at the epic boundary: the rule names no
  restart, and every S' is read at the same E and control. EPIC-011 § Close-out carries it as a row.
- **The 4 000 ms R trend pool closes empty** with EPIC-010 (no value-changing merge after the batch; the owner's
  100/400 rule replaced the trend). Its reference stays on record only and is not carried.
- **RISK-EPIC010-08 closed:** effective 95 % interval ± 27.7 Elo (cluster SE, design effect 1.094, ~366 effective
  games); iid ± 26.4; colour-pair ± 25.1; the stated ± 24 assumed 0.50 draws (measured 40 %). Under the PREREG's
  "much wider" line, so nothing goes to the Architect. For later C3'' reads, ± 24 reads about ± 28; carried to
  EPIC-011 as a row (its G7).
- **QE-E010C-c** (lane figures 57 tests / 577 s; ladder 23 s) stays the Scribe's; EPIC-010's older lines are history.
- **Q-SP-2 lapsed:** ADR-0055 D3's bars were used only by ABC-250 A4, which closed ungraded.
