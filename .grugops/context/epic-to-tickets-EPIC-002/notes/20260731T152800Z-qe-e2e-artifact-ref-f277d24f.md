# Tests added, commands run, and why nothing red or skipped was left behind

## What was added — `src/gameend.test.ts`, two new `describe` blocks, 11 tests, all green

Both blocks assert behaviour that is **correct today**, so they are regression guards, not
defect markers. Neither locks in a deviation.

- **`dead by material (FIDE 5.2.2), across the combinations that decide it`** — 8 tests.
  Two draws (`KBB` both bishops one colour vs K; `KBB` vs `KB` all three on one colour) and six
  non-draws (`KNN` vs K, `KN` vs `KN`, `KBN` vs K, `KBB` on opposite colours, `KBB` on one colour
  vs `KB` on the other, `KQ` vs K). These are the rows the existing table did **not** cover, and
  they are the ones where the FIDE test — *mate is impossible*, not *mate cannot be forced* —
  actually bites. The `KNN` vs K row is the guard against the single most common
  insufficient-material regression.
- **`fifty-move rule — the halfmove-clock boundary`** — 3 tests at 99 / 100 / 150 plies. The
  existing suite reached 100 through a played move; nothing asserted 99 directly, so the
  off-by-one had no oracle.

Nine of the eighteen combinations I checked are now in the suite. The other nine were already
covered by the existing `ABC-007` acceptance and `insufficient material` blocks; I did not
duplicate them (rule 9 — preserve adjacent code).

## What was deliberately NOT added, and why

**No test was added for any conformance gap.** That is a judgment call, made this way:

- **G-1 (automatic vs claimable) is a product deviation, not a defect.** A test asserting the
  current behaviour would *lock the deviation in* and turn a future correction red. A test
  asserting FIDE behaviour would be red today. Neither belongs in a suite the whole team gates
  on. The reproduction is exact and complete in the observation note, ready to paste into a
  ticket if a human reopens the ruling.
- **G-3 (dead position with pawns) is under-detection whose mitigation is the 50-move rule.** A
  test would pin the *mitigation*, and go red the day 5.2.2 is implemented properly.
- **G-2 (the `positionCounts` trap) already has its guard specified** — `ABC-019`'s acceptance
  spy-asserts every `gameStatus` call carries a counts map. Duplicating it here would test a
  caller that does not exist.
- **No skipped tests.** The suite's skip count is **0** and two `Ready` tickets reference that
  baseline in their acceptance criteria (`ABC-011`: *"green with a skip count of 0"*). Raising it
  to record findings that are already recorded in prose would cost the gate more than it buys.
