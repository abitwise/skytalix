# THE PULL ORDER FOR `Ready`, with a reason per ticket — and what was re-read and deliberately NOT moved

`Ready` holds five after this task: `ABC-009`, `ABC-012`, `ABC-013`, `ABC-014`, `ABC-022`. **The pull
itself is the Orchestrator's**; this is the ranking it is taken against.

## The order

1. **`ABC-012`** — Game history module (`M`, `P0`, `EPIC-002`). Unchanged from the 2026-07-31
   sequencing, and its stated blocker is now gone: `ABC-011` is merged, so its repetition tests can
   be written against a key that means what FIDE means instead of being shaped to dodge a defect and
   left permanently blind. No unbuilt dependency, no design hop. **`ABC-022` does not displace it** —
   see the `decision` on item 1.
2. **`ABC-014`** — Board frame renderer (`M`, `P0`, `EPIC-002`). The other `P0` the product's
   existence rests on, and **pullable for the first time**: the `ABC-015`/`RISK-005` knot the
   Orchestrator twice declined to walk into is untied, and this ticket **never blocked on `ABC-015`**
   — its own Dependencies clause was the correct half of the contradiction. No unbuilt dependency,
   no design hop.
3. **`ABC-009`** — `legalMoves` mutates the position it documents as unchanged (`S`, `P1`,
   `EPIC-001`). First of the two rules-core defects. **Ahead of `ABC-022`** on two grounds, neither
   of which is recency: it has held that rank since 2026-07-31, and its reachable surface is
   marginally wider — `gameStatus` calls `legalMoves` on its first line, so `ABC-019`'s loop reaches
   it once per ply, which is the highest-frequency route in the built product.
4. **`ABC-022`** — `generateMoves` en-passant preconditions (`S`, `P1`, `EPIC-001`). Beside
   `ABC-009`: same class, same trigger (a semantically invalid FEN), same eventual gate (`ABC-010`).
   **Different function** — `addPawnMoves` against `addCastlingMoves` — so the two do not conflict in
   the diff and either may ship first; the order above is a ranking, not a dependency.
5. **`ABC-013`** — SAN rendering (`M`, `P1`, `EPIC-002`). Renderer work, below every `P0` and below
   the two rules-core defects. The 2026-07-31 rule that rules-core beats renderer **inside** one
   priority still holds and is what puts it fifth rather than third.

## The shape of the order, stated once

Two `P0`s that make the product exist, then two `P1` rules-core defects, then the `P1` renderer
ticket. That is the 2026-07-31 order with the two `Done` tickets (`ABC-020`, `ABC-011`) removed and
one new `P1` inserted at its rank. **The order was not re-derived from scratch and did not need to
be.**

## WHAT WAS RE-READ AND DELIBERATELY NOT MOVED

A re-prioritisation that moves everything is a reflex, not a judgment, so each of these carries its
reason:

- **The 2026-07-31 re-sequencing is NOT re-opened.** Its comment under `Ready` stands unamended, as
  the routing note required. Nothing found at this task bears on it: item 1 produced a **new** defect
  in the class that sequencing already ranked, and item 2 was a documentation contradiction, not a
  priority question.
- **`ABC-012` stays `P0`, `ABC-014` stays `P0`.** Neither is touched by either item. `ABC-014` gained
  only the note that it is unblocked.
- **`ABC-009` stays `P1`, and is NOT reordered against `ABC-022`.** The temptation was to promote it
  on the strength of the new evidence — `ABC-022`'s half (b) is `ABC-009`'s symptom by another route,
  so the two now look more alike than they did. That is a reason to rank them **together**, which
  they already are, not to move either.
- **`ABC-013` stays `P1` and stays last.** Renderer work; nothing found here touches SAN.
- **`ABC-010` stays `P1` in `Backlog`** — even though this task found a real gap in it (its
  validation closes `ABC-022`'s half (b) trigger but not half (a)'s). The gap is **named on
  `ABC-022` and in `memory-bank/60-progress.md`, not fixed**: amending `ABC-010`'s acceptance is a
  third item and this task was scoped to two. Its reachability is unchanged — it goes live when
  `EPIC-003` accepts a user FEN, which is still unbuilt.
- **`ABC-015` stays `S`/`P1` in `Backlog`**, not promoted. Untying the merge order did not remove the
  build dependency on `ABC-014`.
- **`ABC-016`, `ABC-017`, `ABC-019`, `ABC-021` untouched.** None is named by either item.
- **Every epic priority unchanged** — `EPIC-002`/`003`/`004` `P0`, `EPIC-005` `P1`, `EPIC-006` `P2`.
- **`ABC-009`'s Scope-vs-Acceptance mismatch is named, not amended.** Its scope promises
  byte-identical return *"for every position it accepts"* while every acceptance criterion is a
  castling FEN. Recorded on `ABC-022` so the two tickets do not silently overlap; changing
  `ABC-009`'s criteria would be a third item.

## WIP and the limit

`Ready` 4/8 → 5/8 against the configured limit of 8 (`.grugops/factory.config.json`
`wip_limits.Ready`). **No limit approached and no override taken; no written reason is owed for the
count.** `ABC-022` qualifies on this board's own `Ready` test rather than on its priority — **no
unbuilt dependency at all**, the defective code being merged on `main` — which is the exact criterion
that keeps `ABC-015`..`ABC-019` and `ABC-021` in `Backlog`. Five is well under the ceiling and
`Ready` remains a buffer feeding `In Development` (WIP 3), not a target.

**No other column moved.** `Ready → In Development` is the Orchestrator's pull and was deliberately
not taken. Nothing was merged and nothing was deployed — a human holds both.
