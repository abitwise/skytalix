# Game 1 analysis: skytalix (level 5, White) vs Apple Chess, 0-1, 35 moves

_Written 2026-10-23 by Architect/Design (subtask `arch-analyse-game-1`), on the owner's ask in the play-test log (game 1, "i would like to have PGN file, then i can analyse it"). Game: `plans/uat/EPIC-006-game-1.pgn`. Annotated copy: `plans/uat/EPIC-006-game-1.annotated.pgn`. Engine source read at `main` `f4eec73d`. Nothing in `src/` was changed. The improvement list is a set of proposals for the owner. Nothing was cut._

## 1. For the owner: the short version

- **skytalix lost because it left its king in the centre while the centre opened.** One blunder (13.d4??) and two mistakes (8.e5?, 10.Ne4?) account for half of everything it lost over the game (721 of 1,428 centipawns). By move 13 Stockfish rates White as lost (−6.3 pawns). Apple Chess then converted cleanly.
- **skytalix did not see the danger.** From move 10 to move 29 its own score was 3 to 5.5 pawns more optimistic than Stockfish's. It judged itself clearly worse only at move 22. Stockfish saw that at move 10.
- **The main cause is the evaluation, not the search.** At 13.d4 skytalix prefers the losing move at every depth from 1 to 10. Its king-safety term charges 35 centipawns for opening the d-file in front of an uncastled king. Stockfish charges about 360. Stockfish's refutation wins material only around move 20-23, about 20 plies beyond any depth skytalix reaches in 3 s, so more depth alone could not have saved this move.
- **Depth is the second cause.** 8.e5 and 10.Ne4 are depth-limited. skytalix finds Stockfish's move (Be2) at depth 7 and depth 8. On this machine those depths take about 3.9 s and 5.8 s, against a 3 s budget.
- **A cheap fix looks promising, but it rests on one game.** In a scratch copy of the engine (not `src/`), tripling the existing king-exposure penalty while queens are on the board changed all three bad moves into the right ones (13.Ng4, 8.Be2, 10.Be2). This was a probe, not a tuned change.
- **Rough strength, hedged:** skytalix at level 5 played this game at an average centipawn loss (ACPL) of about 41-49. That is roughly club level: **somewhere around 1400-1900 on a human rating scale.** One game is not a rating, and this range could be off by several hundred points. Apple Chess played at ACPL 8 with no mistakes. Its difficulty setting was not recorded, so no rating is claimed for it.
- **Top proposals, ranked:**
  1. Make king safety bite while queens are on.
  2. Build a rating harness against Stockfish's strength-limited mode, so changes are measured, not guessed.
  3. Make the search faster without changing its answers.
  4. Re-weight mobility so early queen trips stop scoring well.
  5. Add null-move pruning.

  Section 6 gives each proposal's gain, cost and risk.

## 3. Move classification

**Thresholds.** The loss for a move is the score of Stockfish's best move minus the score of the move played, both from the mover's side. Both scores come from the same depth-20 search. Scores are capped at ±10 pawns, and a mate counts as ±10.
- **best:** loss ≤ 10 cp, or the played move is Stockfish's best.
- **good:** 11-49 cp.
- **inaccuracy:** 50-99 cp.
- **mistake:** 100-299 cp.
- **blunder:** ≥ 300 cp.

The cp scale is primary. A win-probability cross-check, using the logistic curve Lichess publishes, is given too.

## 5. Why skytalix went wrong

### How the reproduction was done

- **Level 5, as the game played it.** Level 5 is `{ budgetMs: 3_000, blunderRate: 0 }` (`src/levels.ts:49`). With rate 0 every move is plain `chooseMove` at 3,000 ms (`src/levels.ts:119-123`), behind the opening book (`src/cli.ts:210-214`, `src/book-engine.ts:19`).
- **The book.** It answered moves 1-3 and was out of book from move 4. The book probe was run on every game position.
- **The level-5 reruns reproduce the game.** Every White engine move was rerun with `chooseMove(position, { budgetMs: 3000 })` on the wall clock, on this machine. Run 1 reproduced 30 of 32 moves. Run 2 reproduced 32 of 32. The two run-1 differences (26.b4 for f4, 29.Bxg7 for Bd2) come from the wall clock deciding the depth reached. The level-5 replay therefore stands in for the game.
- **Reached depth:** 6 to 10 plies, about 330,000-750,000 nodes per move.
- **The depth sweep.** To separate "too shallow" from "wrong evaluation", the shipped search was rerun to a fixed depth. It ran with a frozen virtual clock and `maxDepth` (`src/engine.ts:161`, `:503-508`), so it is the same iterative-deepening code at depths 1 to 8. The plain `search(position, depth)` (`src/search.ts:561`) was run to depth 10 at two positions.
- **The PV.** It was read back from the transposition table after the search.
- **The eval breakdown.** It was taken from a scratch re-implementation of the terms in `src/evaluate.ts`. That re-implementation was checked equal to `evaluate()` on all 71 game positions.

### Per error

**4.Qa4?! (inaccuracy, 72 cp): evaluation, with the book a minor factor.**
- **What skytalix saw.** Depth 8, +0.78, PV `Qa4 Nc6 d3 e5 Nb5 e4 Nxa7` (a pawn grab). Stockfish: −0.06, with e4 at +0.66.
- **Depth does not settle it.** skytalix's choice flips between d4, d3 and Qa4 from depth to depth. At depth 7 it is d4 (+0.76) and at depth 8 Qa4 (+0.78), so the pick is a near-tie the evaluation decides.
- **Why the queen move scores well.** Nothing in the evaluation penalises an early queen move or rewards development. Its four terms are material plus piece-square, pawns, king safety and mobility (`src/evaluate.ts:369`). Mobility counts each queen square at the same 4 cp as a minor piece's square (`src/evaluate.ts:199`, `:355-356`). The queen piece-square table scores a4 at 0 against d1 at −5 (`src/evaluate.ts:109-118`).
- **The book.** It ended after 3...b6 (English Symmetrical), so the engine had to choose at move 4.

**8.e5? (mistake, 144 cp): search depth within the time budget, with mobility weight secondary.**
- **What skytalix saw.** Depth 6, +1.02, PV `e5 Nh5 Be3 Bxf3 gxf3 Nc6 f4`. Stockfish: −1.85, with Be2 at −0.41.
- **The sweep.** The shipped search picks Be2 at depths 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8. It picks e5 only at depth 6.
- **Why depth 6 was played.** Depth 7 took 3.9 s here against the 3.0 s budget. The clock aborted iteration 7 and discarded it, and the depth-6 move was played (`src/engine.ts:224-229`, `:497-521`).
- **Mobility.** Mobility was 88 cp of the 148 cp static score, close to its 100 cp cap (`src/evaluate.ts:199-200`, `:364`). A mobility-heavy evaluation rewards space grabs like e5, which kicks the f6 knight.

**10.Ne4? (mistake, 217 cp): search depth, with king safety secondary.**
- **What skytalix saw.** Depth 7, +0.24, PV `Ne4 dxe5 Nxe5 Qc7 Nf3 Bxe4 dxe4 Nc6`. Stockfish: −3.16, with Be2 at −0.99.
- **The sweep.** Ne4 at depths 1-7, Be2 from depth 8.
- **Too deep for the budget.** Depth 8 took 5.8 s here. That is above even the 5 s hard ceiling (`src/engine.ts:129`).
- **The king in the centre costs nothing.** The untapered king table scores e1 and d2 at 0 (`src/evaluate.ts:121-130`). The king-exposure term is 15 cp in this position (`src/evaluate.ts:189-191`, `:249-280`).

**13.d4?? (blunder, 360 cp): evaluation, the size of the king-safety term.**
- **What skytalix saw.** Depth 8, −0.79, PV `d4 cxd4 Bxd4`. Stockfish: −6.32, with Ng4 at −2.72.
- **More depth does not change the move.** skytalix prefers d4 at every depth from 1 to 10. At depth 10 (86 s) it scores d4 at −0.86 and Ng4 at −1.67, because after Ng4 it loses the b2 pawn.
- **Stockfish's refutation is far beyond the horizon.** Stockfish's line is `d4 cxd4 Bxd4 Qf4 Bc3 Qe4+ Kd2 Rd8+ Nd3 Nf4`. It wins material only around moves 20-23, when the pinned knight on d3 falls. That is about 20 plies out, beyond any depth skytalix can reach, so only the evaluation could have known.
- **The evaluation's view.** Opening the d-file raises White's king-exposure penalty from 25 to 60 cp, a 35 cp change. The penalty cannot exceed 105 cp in total: three files at most, 15 + 20 each (`src/evaluate.ts:189-191`, `:263-277`). It has no term for attackers near the king and does not depend on whether queens are on (`src/evaluate.ts:187`, "Untapered by decision").
- **Checks at the horizon are not the cause.** Quiescence searches no checks (`src/quiescence.ts:21-24`). A check-extension probe in the scratch copy left the move unchanged, so this was not the deciding factor here.
- **The probe that flipped it.** Tripling the exposure penalty while the enemy queen is on the board made skytalix play 13.Ng4, Stockfish's move, at the real 3 s budget.

**17.Kc1? (mistake, 114 cp): evaluation, the king piece-square table.**
- **What skytalix saw.** Depth 8, −2.11. Stockfish: −6.93, with Nd3 at −5.79.
- **The sweep.** skytalix picks Nd3 at depth 1 and Kc1 from depth 3 onward.
- **The king table rewards the walk.** It scores c1 at +10 and b1 at +30, the same as a castled g1, against 0 on d2 (`src/evaluate.ts:128-129`). The walk Kd2-Kc1-Kb1 earns up to 30 cp "as if castled", whatever the open c- and d-files say.
- **No probe flipped this move.** The scaled king safety, the centre-king penalty and the check extension all left it unchanged. Stockfish's margin is small among bad options. The fix is `UNKNOWN - verify`.

**29.Bd2? (mistake, 129 cp, in a lost position): search horizon.**
- **What skytalix saw.** Depth 8, −6.12, PV `Bd2 Rxc4 Kb1 Nf2+ …`. Stockfish finds mate in 8 for Black, which is 15 plies.
- **When skytalix saw the mate.** It first scored a mate at move 32 (Rb2, −29,992).
- **Clock-dependent.** Run 1 played Bxg7 here instead.
- **Effect on the result.** None. The game was already lost.

### What is not the cause

- **The blunder draw.** Level 5's rate is 0 (`src/levels.ts:49`).
- **The transposition table.** It is cleared every move (`src/transposition.ts:17-20`), and both reruns reproduce the game.
- **Move ordering.** It is lossless (`src/search.ts:163-190`). It affects only how deep the search gets within 3 s.
- **Tactics.** No move lost material to a short tactic within skytalix's own horizon. Every serious error was strategic or beyond the horizon.

## 6. Improvement candidates (proposals for the owner; nothing is cut)

The ranking weighs evidence from this game, expected gain, cost and risk together. "Gain" means in this game unless stated otherwise. Every gain beyond this game is `UNKNOWN - verify` until candidate 2 exists.

1. **King safety that bites while queens are on.**
   - **The change.** Scale the existing exposure penalty when the enemy queen is on the board. Taper the king table, so b1 and g1 earn their bonus only with the shelter intact. A king-zone attacker term can follow later if it is still needed.
   - **Evidence.** The probe at ×3 fixed 13.d4, 8.e5 and 10.Ne4, which is 721 of 1,428 cp (50 %).
   - **Cost:** S-M. Constants, a queen-present flag taken from the board walk that already exists, and relation tests.
   - **Risk.** `ADR-0031` (king safety ships alone): the values are hand-tuned and "expected to move" (`src/evaluate.ts:12-14`), but a new term or taper is worth an ADR addendum. The `A5` eval cost bar of ≤ 1,100 ns per call must be re-read, though a flag adds almost nothing. `NFR-005` depth needs a re-read on `kiwipete` and `position-6`. The `A1` mirror and the `A2`/`A3` relation tests hold if the change is colour-symmetric. The `PUZZLE_DEEP=1` gate is the regression check. `NFR-008`: no new lane cost if the tests stay relations.
2. **A rating instrument (an enabler, not a strength change).**
   - **The change.** A match harness: skytalix's UCI entry against Stockfish with `UCI_LimitStrength`, outside `npm test`.
   - **Why.** Without it, candidates 1, 4 and 5 are tuned on one game.
   - **Gain:** measurability. 100 games give about ±70 Elo.
   - **Cost:** M. No match runner is installed (`cutechess-cli` and `fastchess` are absent), and 100 games take hours of wall time.
   - **Risk.** It must never enter the default lane (`NFR-008`, ≤ 10 s wall). It depends on a host tool, as this analysis already does. It touches no ADR.
3. **Faster search with identical answers.**
   - **Where the time goes.**
     - Every leaf generates legal moves twice: `gameStatus` (`src/engine.ts:245` → `src/gameend.ts:137`), then `quiesce` (`src/quiescence.ts:108`).
     - Quiescence generates every legal move and keeps only the captures (`src/quiescence.ts:107-110`).
     - The late-move-reduction rule plays and unplays each candidate move to test for check (`src/search.ts:262-264`).
   - **Evidence.** 8.e5 is fixed at 1.3× the time and 10.Ne4 at 1.9×.
   - **Gain:** medium. About +0.5 to 1 ply at the same 3 s. The real speed-up is `UNKNOWN - verify` with `npm run perft` and the `NFR-005` reading.
   - **Cost:** M.
   - **Risk.** The search must stay value-identical: `ABC-078` `A3` identity sweep, and `src/search.ts`'s pinned exports (`A6`). `NFR-004` abort granularity must not change. `NFR-005` improves, and `NFR-008` may get faster. A value-identical change needs no ADR, but leaf terminal detection is subtle (mate and stalemate at the leaf).
4. **Re-weight mobility per piece, with the queen lower, and add a small development or early-queen term.**
   - **Evidence.** Together with a centre-king term, the probe turned 4.Qa4 into 4.e4, Stockfish's move.
   - **Gain:** low to medium.
   - **Cost:** S.
   - **Risk.** The `A4` ±100 cp mobility cap. `ADR-0031`'s mobility form. The eval cost bar needs a re-read.
5. **Null-move pruning.**
   - **Status.** Listed in the engine header as "none requested" (`src/engine.ts:69-71`).
   - **Gain:** medium to high in general, typically +1 to 2 plies. This game's evidence is the depth-limited 8.e5 and 10.Ne4 (`UNKNOWN - verify`).
   - **Cost:** M.
   - **Risk.** It changes values, so per `ADR-0026` it gets a test-only off door and a bounded oracle. A new ADR is needed, and it must guard against zugzwang. `NFR-005` improves.
6. **Keep the transposition table across moves.**
   - **Status.** `ADR-0025` decision 4 clears it per search (`src/transposition.ts:17-20`).
   - **Gain:** low (`UNKNOWN - verify`).
   - **Cost:** S.
   - **Risk.** An `ADR-0025` amendment. `NFR-009`'s growth clause needs a re-read, though the size stays 4 MiB. Deterministic-mode tests assume a fresh table.
7. **Check extension, or checks in the first quiescence ply.**
   - **Gain:** low in this game. The probe changed none of its errors.
   - **Cost:** M.
   - **Risk.** It reverses `ABC-079`'s recorded decision (`src/quiescence.ts:21-24`). Node growth against `NFR-004` and `NFR-005`.
8. **A deeper opening book for the English lines.**
   - **Evidence.** Out of book at move 4.
   - **Gain:** low (72 cp here).
   - **Cost:** S (data only).
   - **Risk.** `NFR-006` ≤ 1 MB (356,526 B today). The `ADR-0037` format is unchanged.

## 7. Details

- **Positions.** The game was replayed with `src/san.ts` and `src/apply.ts`, and every position written out as FEN. The book probe used `src/book.ts` and `data/opening-book/book.json`.

- **skytalix runs.** Node v26.11.1 on this host, from the analysis worktree at `f4eec73d`. The scripts and raw outputs are temporary, in the session scratchpad, and are not committed. The numbers in this report are their outputs.
- **Hardware.** The owner's game ran on "Owner's Mac". Whether that is this machine is `UNKNOWN - verify`. The two reruns reproduced 30 of 32 and 32 of 32 moves, which suggests comparable speed.
