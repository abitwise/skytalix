# `ABC-197` A5: the SE's two claims hold from `src/` and in scratch, and the band is 2 × step, not the step. Counts only, no timing

This is an observation, not a finding: there is no stamp. Every reading below is from scratch trees (`git archive` of `051d557a` and `20e397c8`) under `scratchpad/bapm197/`. Each source was restored and `cmp`-checked. No timing ran.

## From `src/` at `20e397c8` (unchanged on `main` `01bf3b8d`)
- **The parse-side G.** `parseGo` sets the default G to `HARD_CEILING_MS` = 5 000. A dropped `depth`/`nodes`/`mate` token leaves it there.
- **The level cap.** `go` caps it with `Math.min(request.budgetMs, level.budgetMs)` (`uci-session.ts` L281). `LEVELS` tops out at 3 000 (level 5 = `DEFAULT_LEVEL`). `request.budgetMs` has no other reader.
- **The clock.** `virtualClock(s)` returns its reading and then adds `s` (`virtual-clock.ts`). `chooseMove` stops at the first reading ≥ start + G, at a node check every 1 024 nodes or at the loop head. It then reads once more for `elapsedMs`.
- **So** `time = (⌈G/s⌉ + 1) · s` for the capped G, as long as the search runs to the deadline.
