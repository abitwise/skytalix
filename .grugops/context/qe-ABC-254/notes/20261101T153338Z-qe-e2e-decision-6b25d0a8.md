## A4 (speed) + G6 + G5 report: one schedule
- **Start precondition (ALONE):** 0 foreign vitest (bracketed `pgrep -f "[/]vitest"`, waited on by PID with `kill -0`), 0 `cutechess-cli`, load1 ≤ 2.0, Tctl ≤ 60 °C; poll 15 s. **Wait cap 45 min**; then proceed and mark the reading SHARED.
- **Schedule:** 5 rounds. Each round runs, in this order, a `kiwipete` pair, a `position-6` pair, a `start` pair and a `position-4` pair; base first in odd rounds, head first in even rounds. 40 runs.
- **A run:** one fresh process, `chooseMove(parseFen(fen), { budgetMs: 4000 })` on the real clock (`qe-scripts/probe.mjs`). nps = `nodes / elapsedMs × 1000` (the se-ABC-254 / ABC-227 method). Wall, depth, move, score, purity recorded.
- **Per run, recorded:** load1 before/after, Tctl before/after, foreign cores = (host busy CPU-s from `/proc/stat` − the probe's own CPU-s) / wall, and processes above 20 % CPU.
- **Before every pair:** a foreign vitest is waited out by PID, a `cutechess-cli` by poll, and Tctl ≥ 85 °C pauses until ≤ 75 °C.
- **A pair is clear** when no foreign vitest or `cutechess-cli` is present after it and both runs read foreign cores ≤ 1.0. A pair that is not clear is re-run once, at once; the second attempt is graded whatever it reads, and is labelled.
- **Validity (ADR-0055 D3, adapted):**
  - (v1) values: at equal completed depth, base and head must choose the same move and score; a difference is a defect: STOP, no grade. (Values are owned by A1/G4.)
  - (v2) spread: per build and board, (max − min) / median nps ≤ 5 %. Over 5 %: that board's whole 5-round schedule (both builds, same order rule) is re-run once after the main schedule, and the re-run replaces it. Over 5 % again: that board is **VOID** (not a result).
  - (v3) thermal: if the median of the after-run Tctl samples over the graded runs exceeds 85 °C, the reading is thermal-STOPPED (void).
- **Bar (graded):** head's median nps ÷ base's median nps **≥ 1.10 on at least two of kiwipete, position-6, start and ≥ 1.00 on the third**; **in-check guard position-4 ≥ 0.97**. A void main board → A4 UNRESOLVED (no MET). Valid and below the bar → A4 NOT MET, a `failed-attempt` note.
- **SHARED** (cap reached): graded as above but labelled SHARED; any ratio within 0.03 of its bar under SHARED → QE recommends an ALONE re-read.
- **G6 (NFR-005), from the kiwipete and position-6 runs of this schedule** (each round has a kiwipete pair then a position-6 pair, base first in odd rounds, as the EPIC-008 rule says; start/position-4 pairs follow in the same round, disclosed): FAIL if any head run < depth 6; "≥ base" = head median completed depth ≥ base median, per board (graded); depth 7 WARN reported. Both sides at 4 000 ms.
- **G5 report:** every one of the 40 searches' wall ≤ 5 000 ms (ceiling); level 5's 4 s bar read on `elapsedMs` (reported like qe-ABC-235's "≤ 3 002.5 ms against 3 s"). G5 graded by the `ENGINE_DEEP` engine and levels lanes green.
