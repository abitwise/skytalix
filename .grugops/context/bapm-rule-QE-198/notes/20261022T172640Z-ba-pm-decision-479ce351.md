## Why cut, and why this lever
- **The timeout lever is not mine.** The owner ruled on 2026-10-19 that "the per-test timeout is not raised" and that heavy rows that time out are lightened instead, "the same goes for any row with the same profile" (`…6e1d9b47`). These two rows have that profile. A per-row timeout above 5 s reverses his ruling. Lightening applies it.
- **Accept-with-re-run does not pay.** The class has now hit two hops: it stopped `ABC-173` on 2026-10-19 and gave `ABC-198`'s SE an unexplained red today. `ABC-198`'s red cost a lost log and 146 QE runs to classify. On a host that runs at load1 up to 12, a 2×–3.5× margin is spent by ordinary bursts (one burst took row 2 to 73 % of the bound). `QE-198-b` narrows it about 5 % more. The fix is two constants and a step, under an existing pattern.
- **NFR-008 headroom.** The lane is unread on 4.1.12, and the last admissible headroom is 0.605 s. Of the three options, only lightening moves the lane at all, and it moves it down: an estimated ≈ −0.2 s of in-thread CPU, ≈ −0.1 s of wall. A raised timeout or an accept costs 0. `ABC-199` takes no full-lane reading. It merges after `ABC-178`, so `ABC-178`'s B7/B8 reads a tree without it. `N8-LANE` (≤ 650 ms CPU per one-file set) is Security/NFR's to rule owed or not for a work-removing diff.
- **No bound, level or requirement moves.** `ABC-170` A2 ("maxNodes aborts and plays the last completed iteration") and `ABC-167` A7 (the D12 limits) are still proved, by the same identity and precondition. Only the test's budget and step change, as `ABC-173` did for `ABC-170` A3 and `ABC-167` A5. So no STOP for the owner.
- **Discrimination is the risk.** It is held by K1–K4 red-first at base, QE's re-plant (A7), and `ADR-0046`'s "a row that loses a plant goes back to its prior step alone".

## Interim rule for `QE-198-a`, until `ABC-199` merges (and afterwards, for a row A4's fallback leaves unchanged)
A red on `ABC-170 A2` row 1 or 2, or on `ABC-167 A7` row 1, is a re-run on a quieter host, not a FAIL, when all three hold:
1. its message is `Test timed out in 5000ms`;
2. the run was a coverage form, or load1 was over 8;
3. the same row passes on the re-run, and no other row is red.

Any other red on those rows is a real red. A timeout red inside the reporter's exit-code-only row (`BUMP_RERUN_LIST` (2)) voids that reading. It does not fail the reporter, and it is re-run on a quiet host (QE's route 3). Classify a coverage run by `numFailedTests`, never by rc (`QE-198-d`).

## Why QE-198-b is INFO, not an Architect hop
- No criterion, NFR or budget pins coverage-mode CPU. `NFR-008`'s lane is the uncovered `npm test`, where QE saw no shift.
- The cause is in an upstream package (`@vitest/mocker`'s code-generation delta) that this repository does not control. An investigation would cost a hop and could change no decision we own: the bump is merged for AR-1, and reverting it would bring back the advisory pairs it cleared (10 → 3).
- The only harm is the margin it takes from `QE-198-a`, and `ABC-199` restores that margin with room to spare.
