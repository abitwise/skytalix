## The sharpest defect, in full — D1

Item 7 read: *"Read from `gameStatusOf(history)` — the same call the panel's other items already sit
beside — never recomputed."*

**Both halves are false, and the second one is expensive.**

There is **no such call beside the panel's other items.** Items 1, 3 and 5 read `Position` fields,
item 2 is `isInCheck`, item 4 is a replay plus `toSan`, item 6 is a flag. Nothing else in this panel
touches the repetition machinery. The sentence describes a call that does not exist, and it is the
sentence that makes 47.9 µs read as free.

And calling it here is **not** "not recomputing" — it is the second computation of the ply.
`src/history.ts:152`: `gameStatusOf(h)` is `gameStatus(h.position, repetitionCounts(h))`, and
`repetitionCounts` → `keyHistory` re-parses `initialFen` and replays **every move in the game**,
hashing each ply. **`ABC-019` — the loop that renders this panel — already runs that derive once per
ply before the render**, and rules on this exact question in words:

> *"The loop must derive the counts once per ply, not once per call site — `gameStatusOf` after the
> human ply and after the engine ply are two derives of two different lists and that is correct;
> **three derives of the same list is not**."*

**Two of `ABC-019`'s acceptance criteria are made false by the panel as it was written**, not merely
made expensive: on a `rejected` input and on the non-mutating `board` command it requires that *"a
frame is re-emitted, the ply is not advanced and `gameStatus` is **not** re-evaluated"*. Re-emitting
the frame is what runs the panel. A spy-based assertion — which that criterion asks for by name —
fails.

**Measured at this hop against merged `src/`** (Apple M3 / Node v24.12.0 / darwin-arm64; nine rounds,
medians of per-round means, discarded warm-up; `UNKNOWN - verify` on other hardware):

| plies | `gameStatusOf` | whole panel as written | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 4.41 µs | 4.62 µs | 95% |
| 40 | 21.06 µs | 26.24 µs | 80% |
| 120 | **47.93 µs** | **56.12 µs** | 85% |
| 300 | **95.64 µs** | **105.00 µs** | **91%** |

**The fix: the claimable set becomes an INPUT.** The loop evaluates `gameStatus` once per ply before
it renders and hands the answer in as `status: GameStatus`; the panel reads `status.claimable` and
calls `gameStatusOf`, `repetitionCounts` and `keyHistory` **exactly zero times** — pinned by `A13`,
which is a **spied call count and not a timing**, because a bar with an order of magnitude of
headroom cannot fail on the regression it exists to catch. That is the move `plans/nfr-catalog.md`
has now made three times (`NFR-005`'s predicate sub-bar, `NFR-001`'s two bars, `ABC-009`'s guards).

The panel's whole compute drops to **≈ 0.2 / 5.4 / 8.0 / 10.0 µs** at 0 / 40 / 120 / 300 plies. The
ticket's original estimate of *"well under 20 µs"* becomes **true, and provable**, instead of false.

## The new NFR bar, and what happens when it does not bind

The Orchestrator's second flag was right and its diagnosis was right: a bar reverse-engineered from
an estimate, the shape of `ABC-025`'s `N5`. **One thing differs and it is stated rather than
smoothed: this bar is not breached today** — 105.00 µs against 250 µs is 0.42x at 300 plies. It is
not breached because it is 12x its own estimate, and the estimate was 5.3x wrong. Growth is
~0.335 µs/ply and 250 µs arrives at roughly **730 plies**, which is a reachable legal game length
(FIDE 9.6.2 bounds each irreversible epoch at 150 plies, not the game).

The replacement has three parts and the third is the one that binds:

- **panel compute ≤ 25 µs at each of 40 / 120 / 300 plies** — measured worst cell 10.0 µs, 0.40x;
- **the three figures within 3x** — measured 1.88x, the machine-independent half, the same shape
  `ABC-017` carries. Reintroducing the derive reads **4.0x** across the same range;
- **`A13`'s spied call counts** — zero repetition derives, exactly one `isInCheck`, one `toSan`, one
  64-square scan.

**A miss on any part is BA/PM's to rule on, not the gate's to accept and not the engineer's to
absorb**, and re-deriving the bar is a valid ruling. Instrument sanity: `gameStatusOf` at 120 plies
reads 47.93 µs here against `plans/nfr-catalog.md`'s independently measured 47.75 / 47.59 µs — 0.4%.
