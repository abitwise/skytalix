## 3. The move cap, its unit
- **`-maxmoves 80` counts full moves.** It is read from cutechess source at `5e84232b` (the pinned build's commit),
  `gameadjudicator.cpp:146-147`: `board->plyCount() >= 2 * m_maxGameLength`.
  - `Board::plyCount()` is `m_moveHistory.size()`, the moves played since the EPD position was set.
  - So the cap is 160 plies from the opening position. It ends the game right after black's 80th move.
  - Each side reaches its move 80, which is two complete periods.
- **Checked in the PGNs:** any capped game must show `PlyCount` 160 and a result naming "maximal game length". If no
  game reaches the cap, the cap is reported as not exercised.

## 4. Derivations (pinned; `raw/scripts/read222.py`, sha256 `c8db1f2d…75cd4`)
**Per-move time (exact).** cutechess `TimeControl::update()` sets `timeLeft −= lastMoveTime` (ms). At the end of a
period it adds the 900 s base, and the PGN prints that same `lastMoveTime`. For a side's k-th move:
```
elapsed_k = own clock in go_k − own clock in go_(k+1) + (900 000 if k is a multiple of 40)
```
- k counts from 1, the first move the side plays from the EPD position. Book moves count.
- "Own clock" is `wtime` for white and `btime` for black. A side's colour is the PGN `White`/`Black` tag.
- **A side's last move in a game** has no next `go`. For it, `elapsed` is the `-debug all` stamp delta,
  `ts(bestmove) − ts(go)`, in ms since the match start. The stamp delta is cross-checked against the exact figure on
  every other move, and its median and max difference are reported.

**Closest call (A3).** Clock left after move k = own clock in `go_k` − `elapsed_k`. This is the clock before any
base is added. cutechess expires a move when `elapsed > timeLeft + margin`, and the margin is 0, so this is the
margin to the flag. The closest call is the minimum over every move of both sides in every game played.

**Completed period (A4).** Period p of a side is complete when that side has played its moves 40(p−1)+1 … 40p, so its
40th move of the period was made and accepted.
- Use = the sum of `elapsed` over those 40 moves / 900 000, book moves included.
- The period counts from the side's first move after the opening position.
- A game that ends before a side's move 40p does not complete that side's period p.

**Longest move (A5).** The maximum `elapsed` over all moves. It is MET when it is **< 22 500 ms**.
- **The PGN's precision:** cutechess `chessgame.cpp` `evalString` prints whole seconds for t ≥ 10 000 ms, 0.1 s for
  1–10 s, 0.01 s for 0.1–1 s, and 0.001 s below that.
- So the PGN reads a 22 s move as `22s`, which is the whole-second reading itself. A move of ≥ 22 500 ms prints as
  `23s` or more (`UNKNOWN - verify` is closed by the source).
- A5 is graded on the exact ms. Every PGN time string present must also be consistent with its exact figure, and
  none may read above `22s`.
- A book move whose eval is empty and under 500 ms has no PGN comment (`MoveEvaluation::isEmpty`). Such a move is
  counted from the clock.

**Book move.** A move is a book move if the engine wrote `info string book` between its `go` and its `bestmove`.
Every other move is a searched move.

**A6 overhead.** `elapsed − 22 000` over searched moves: the median and the max.
- Also reported over searched moves of ≥ 21 000 ms, which excludes early returns (a forced move or a depth limit).
  The count of searched moves under 21 000 ms is reported.
- The longest move's excess over 22 000 ms is set against `AR-NFR004-A`'s ≤ ~31 ms. `qe-ABC-220` `A11` measured the
  in-process overshoot at ≤ 1.0 ms (`…58af5b6a`), so the rest is the pipe and cutechess.
- **`movestogo`:** a count of `go` lines with and without it, and game 1's first `go` line quoted.
- **`isready` during a search:** a count of `isready` lines sent to an engine between its `go` and its `bestmove`.

## 5. Bars
- **A1 MET:** the pins above hold. Game 1's log shows `>skytalix-a(…): setoption name TimeMode value
  simple-tournament` and the same line for `skytalix-b`, with no `TimeMode takes …` refusal.
  - If the line does not reach the engine, QE stops, fixes the spelling, and restarts the count.
- **A2 MET:** every game played keeps its PGN and its `-debug all` log. Each ends by a rule result, the move cap, or
  a loss on time.
  - Any other end is a named fault, and the reading is UNRESOLVED. That includes an illegal move, a disconnect, a
    stall (no result inside 5 400 s, so the watchdog kills it), a crash, or "No result".
  - An engine killed after a game's result line is counted and reported, and is not a fault.
- **A3 MET:** 0 losses on time over all games played, **and** the closest call is ≥ 500 ms.
- **A4 MET:** every completed side-period uses ≥ 75 %, **and** at least 4 side-periods are complete.
  - The coverage extension: if games 1–5 complete fewer than 4, the harness plays openings 6, 7, … one game at a
    time until 4 are complete, up to 10 games in all.
  - If 10 games still complete fewer than 4, A4 is UNRESOLVED.
- **A5 MET:** the longest move is < 22 500 ms, and no PGN time reads above `22s`.
- **A6:** reported, not graded. It is complete when every listed figure is reported from the run's own records.
- **A7 MET:**
  - The run is ALONE: no other agent hop, no foreign vitest, and no second `cutechess-cli`. The host has no battery
    (an empty `power_supply` class), so it runs on mains.
  - Tctl (k10temp) and the CPU frequency are sampled every 10 s (≥ every 60 s required), and the largest gap is
    ≤ 60 s.
  - The play window runs from game 1's start to the last game's end, cut into 4 equal-time quarters. QE reports
    the Tctl median of each quarter and the max.
  - **Frequency:** per sample, the busiest core's `scaling_cur_freq` (the max over the CPUs). This is the same
    measure as the `qe-ABC-228`/`-229`/`-230`/`-241` precedent. A quarter whose median is more than 5 % under Q1's
    median makes the reading **UNRESOLVED** (`RISK-EPIC009-04`). The precedent formula, 1 − median over the run /
    median of the first 6 samples, is reported as INFO.
  - **A foreign process during play** (another project's vitest) is named, with its sample count. It is graded as an
    A7 gap, not a stop. If a loss on time, a closest call under 1 000 ms or a move ≥ 22 400 ms falls inside its window,
    A3 or A5 are UNRESOLVED and not graded, because load can cause a forfeit but cannot hide one.

## 6. Before game 1
- **Wait-clear** (`raw/scripts/wait-clear.sh`, `qe-ABC-225-w1`'s logic): QE snapshots the pids of any foreign
  vitest and any `cutechess-cli`, then polls `kill -0` on each until all are gone. It never uses `while pgrep -f`.

- **One plumbing rehearsal, not graded:** the same command with `tc=40/20 -maxmoves 4` and the output in a separate
  scratch dir. It is a few seconds of play, and it proves the log and PGN formats the reader parses. Its PGN and
  log are not part of the reading. A reader fix after it may correct parsing only. No derivation in § 4 changes, and
  any fix is listed with its diff in the artifacts note.

## 7. Thermal stop and the VOID rule
- **Thermal stop:** the harness checks every 60 s. Once there are ≥ 30 samples, a Tctl median above **85 °C** over all
  samples so far stops the run with SIGTERM. QE reports **THERMAL STOP**, and A2–A6 are not read.
- **VOID:** the run is VOID when the harness, or any `cutechess-cli` invocation, exits non-zero or dies without a
  thermal stop. A missing `Finished match` line or a missing PGN also makes it VOID. QE records it and **STOPS**,
  with no re-run in this hop.
- **The ticket's "re-run once"** for a named fault (A2) or a frequency fall (A7) is owed to the Orchestrator's
  schedule. It needs another ALONE window of up to ~5 h, so it is not done in this hop.

## 8. Verdict rules
- **PASS:** A1–A5 and A7 MET, A6 reported, no fault and no gap.
- **PASS_WITH_GAPS:** the same, with non-blocking gaps named (for example a foreign process outside any close
  call's window, or the cap not exercised).
- **FAIL (NOT MET):** A3, A4 or A5 NOT MET. Per test note 3, it goes in as a `failed-attempt` note, and `ABC-220`
  goes back to BA/PM.
- **UNRESOLVED:** a named fault, a frequency fall, A4 short of coverage after 10 games, or A3/A5 inside a foreign
  load window.
- **THERMAL STOP / VOID:** not read (§ 7).
