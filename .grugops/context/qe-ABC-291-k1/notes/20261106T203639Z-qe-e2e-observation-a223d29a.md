- **The term's values did not move:** the bundle-off row above.

## Why M3 reacts, by reading only
M3 skips quiets with a zero ordering key at shallow null-window nodes. A small score change moves the window and the history keys, and so which quiets are skipped. That is the likely path from a 10 cp term to a 79 cp tree change. I did not isolate it.
