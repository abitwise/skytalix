## Method
- **A run (unchanged):** one fresh process runs `chooseMove(parseFen(fen), { budgetMs: 4000 })` on the real clock (`probe.mjs`). nps = nodes / elapsedMs × 1000.
- **Recorded for each run (unchanged):** load1 and Tctl before and after; foreign cores = (host busy CPU-s from `/proc/stat` − the probe's own CPU-s) / process wall; foreign processes above 20 % of one core over the run, from two `/proc/<pid>/stat` snapshots.
  - **V2-INFO (QE-254-d, evidence only, enters no rule):** a second list, `busyWithChildren`, adds each process's reaped-children CPU (`cutime + cstime`). A short-lived `git`/`node`/`sh` child then shows up on its surviving parent.
- **Start precondition (unchanged from qe-ABC-254-a4).** All of these, polled every 15 s:
  - 0 running foreign vitest. The detector is `pgrep -f "[/]vitest"` with `/proc/<pid>/comm` = node or vitest; flock, sh, bash and npm waiters do not count;
  - 0 `cutechess-cli`;
  - no foreign process above 50 % of one core over a 3 s `/proc` sample, the driver excluded;
  - load1 ≤ 2.0;
  - Tctl ≤ 60 °C.
- **Wait cap (unchanged):** lock acquisition + 45 min (`CLEAR_DEADLINE`). At the cap the read proceeds and is labelled **SHARED**. Waits on the lock are outside the cap and the run-time budget.
- **Gate before each attempt (unchanged):** a foreign vitest is waited out by PID (`kill -0`), never past the cap. A `cutechess-cli` is waited out by poll. Tctl ≥ 85 °C pauses the read until Tctl ≤ 75 °C.

## D3-V2, as implemented (`a4v2.mjs`)
1. **Admission (discard, never force).** A pair is admitted only if, read after the pair:
   - no foreign vitest and no `cutechess-cli` are present;
   - both runs read foreign cores ≤ **0.8**, compared on the unrounded value;
   - both runs' after-run Tctl is **< 85 °C**.

   Anything else is **discarded**. A discarded pair is logged with its reasons and foreign-cores readings, and never enters a median, a spread or a ratio. Nothing is graded "whatever it reads".
2. **Blind, fixed count.** Admission reads host counters and the probe's CPU time only. It never reads nodes, nps, depth or a ratio. A board's reading is its **first 5 admitted pairs**, and no attempt runs past the fifth admitted pair.
   - **Order:** base first when the board's admitted count is even, head first when it is odd. A retry keeps the discarded attempt's order, so the counted pairs go BH HB BH HB BH (3 : 2).
3. **Rounds and cap.**
   - Boards go round by round in the order kiwipete, position-6, start, position-4.
   - In a round, a board retries at once until one pair is admitted or the board has made **15 attempts** in this schedule (main or re-run).
   - A board at its cap with fewer than 5 admitted pairs is **VOID-LOAD**: it gets no more attempts, it is not a result, and it is not a miss. The miss rule does not fire.
   - A VOID-LOAD board gets **no** board re-run: D3's re-run is owed by the spread rule and the agreement guard, not by load. The other boards still run and are graded.
4. **(v1) values (unchanged).** It is checked on every attempt, admitted or not. At equal completed depth, base and head must choose the same move and score. A difference is a defect: STOP, no grade.
5. **(v2) spread (unchanged bar).** Per build and board over the 5 counted runs, (max − min) / median nps, unrounded, must be ≤ 5 %.
6. **Agreement guard.**
   - The median of the 5 counted paired ratios (head nps ÷ base nps per pair) is reported for every board.
   - The bars are 1.10 and 1.00 on kiwipete, position-6 and start, and 0.97 on position-4.
   - If the paired median and the graded ratio fall on different sides of any of the board's bars, the guard fails. ("Same side" means both ≥ the bar, or both < it.)
7. **The one board re-run (D3).**
   - A main-schedule board with spread > 5 % on either build, or with a failed agreement guard, is re-run once, right after the main schedule. The re-run is a fresh schedule for that board under the same rules and a fresh cap of 15 attempts, and it replaces the main reading.
   - In the re-run, spread > 5 % or a failed guard → **VOID**, and fewer than 5 admitted pairs → **VOID-LOAD**.
   - `run.sh` launches the re-run itself from `grade.mjs`'s `owed` list.
8. **(v3) thermal (unchanged).** If a board's counted runs have a median after-run Tctl above 85 °C, the read is thermal-STOPPED (UNRESOLVED). Admission already requires < 85 °C on every counted run, so this cannot trigger unless the rule is broken.
